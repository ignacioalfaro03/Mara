## Production note

Capricho/purchase reward scripts are deterministic product payoffs. The payment event controls eligibility; the user's explicit preference controls the reward grammar. A high contribution may increase the scope of a guaranteed digital entitlement if that scope was disclosed, but it never grants control over Mara's affection, safety boundaries or relationship status.
