# MARA VERA — ALPHA COMMERCIAL CONTENT V1

Status: execution inventory for the first monetizable story arc.

This document converts the social/story thesis into a concrete content production plan. It does not activate payments or authorize publication.

Primary objective:

> Prove that one coherent Mara story can create acquisition, DM curiosity, return, multiple digital SKUs and repeat purchase without requiring runtime media generation.

---

## 1. First arc

Internal arc key:

`SOFI_FRIDAY_01`

Working conflict:

Sofi captured or knows something about Mara's Friday that Mara does not fully explain publicly.

The story must remain simple enough to understand with zero context.

Narrative grammar:

`NORMALITY → ODD CLUE → DENIAL/MINIMIZATION → CONTRADICTION → TENSION → NEW EVIDENCE → PUBLIC NON-RESOLUTION → PRIVATE CONTINUATION`

The exact creative details may change. The arc key and commercial structure should remain stable for measurement.

---

## 2. Seven-day public sequence

### Day 1 — odd clue

Job:

- create first curiosity;
- introduce Sofi as alternate POV;
- no commercial ask.

Public beat:

Sofi posts/captures something that makes viewers wonder where Mara was or what happened.

CTA:

none or profile curiosity only.

### Day 2 — Mara minimizes

Job:

- establish conflicting versions;
- strengthen character.

Mara responds publicly without fully resolving the clue.

### Day 3 — contradiction

Job:

- prove the story is moving;
- reward previous viewers.

A second clue makes Mara's public version incomplete.

### Day 4 — Sofi knows more

Job:

- increase World value;
- make alternate POV important.

Sofi reveals enough to create a specific unanswered question.

### Day 5 — consequence

Job:

- create emotional/narrative consequence;
- avoid constant escalation.

Examples:

- tension between Mara/Sofi;
- removed post;
- awkward callback;
- changed behavior.

### Day 6 — evidence without full answer

Job:

- maximize curiosity before web handoff.

A clue narrows what happened but still leaves a concrete unknown.

### Day 7 — public boundary / private continuation

Job:

- route qualified curiosity into Mara's owned product.

Mara does not need to say “buy”.

Desired behavior:

viewer understands that talking to Mara is the natural next step.

Attribution entry concept:

`?source=instagram&arc=sofi_friday_01`

# 7. Offer timing

Do not show commercial offer immediately on entry from Instagram.

Minimum desired sequence:

`ENTRY → MARA RESPONSE → USER PARTICIPATION → FIRST VALUE → STORY KNOWLEDGE / CURIOSITY → ELIGIBLE OFFER`

Exact threshold remains configurable and should be tested.

A user declining an offer continues the story normally.

# 8. Adult reward attachment

If the product lane later uses an adult Reward Engine, reward attachment must be independent from the public story itself.

# 11. Content QA

Every asset needs PASS/FAIL for:

- Mara identity;
- adult-age clarity;
- World/canon continuity;
- correct characters;
- correct story beat;
- no impossible chronology;
- no accidental fake evidence presented as real-world fact;
- natural phone/editorial grammar appropriate to source;
- no excessive ad feel;
- entitlement/visibility metadata;
- no unintended duplicate pose/scene where variation is required;
- final asset actually exists at registered location.

# 12. Measurement contract

At arc level measure:

### Acquisition

- reach/views;
- completion where platform data is available;
- profile action;
- web entry by `arc`.

### Product

- DM activation;
- first value completion;
- World/Sofi engagement;
- return D1/D3/D7 where measurable truthfully.

### Commerce

- offer shown;
- checkout intent;
- paid;
- revenue by SKU;
- AOV;
- repeat purchase;
- post-offer continuation;
- refund/chargeback.

### Economics

- variable conversation cost;
- contribution margin;
- media production cost when known;
- revenue per arc.

Do not claim attribution precision that the data cannot support.

---

# 13. Arc decision gate

### KEEP

The arc deserves another episode/variation if it produces meaningful curiosity, DM engagement, return and/or purchase without damaging Mara credibility.

### FIX

Attention exists but users do not understand the story, web handoff or paid value.

Change the weakest link, not the entire product.

### KILL

Kill/retire the arc pattern if it repeatedly produces neither qualified engagement nor commercial/retention value after meaningful testing.

Do not keep a story just because the images look good.
