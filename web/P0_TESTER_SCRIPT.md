# Mara Vera — P0 First Tester Moderator Script

Last reviewed: 2026-09-02

## Purpose

Run the first private adult-user sessions consistently enough to make KEEP / ITERATE / KILL / NOT ENOUGH DATA decisions without turning the test into a guided demo.

This is a qualitative research script, not a production consent flow or analytics replacement.

## Tester eligibility

Use only adults.

Do not recruit or screen using sensitive financial/vulnerability criteria.

Do not ask for:
- salary;
- debt;
- bank details;
- actual intimate history;
- real names of sexual partners;
- compulsive-spending history.

Useful non-sensitive context:
- familiar with AI companions: yes/no;
- familiar with creator/subscription products: yes/no;
- familiar with adult D/s/findom vocabulary: yes/no/prefer not to say.

## Researcher setup

Before each session:

1. clear/reset local P0 state;
2. confirm no real payment/provider is active;
3. confirm DEV-only labels remain visible where relevant;
4. record tester code, not real identity;
5. do not pre-explain Mara's routing logic;
6. start from `/` and let the tester navigate naturally.

## Opening script

Say only what is necessary:

`Esto es un prototipo de Mara. Quiero que lo uses como si hubieras llegado por tu cuenta. No hay pagos reales. Algunas partes, especialmente la voz, siguen siendo placeholders. Mientras avanzas, dime cuando algo te confunda, te parezca falso, demasiado vendedor o particularmente bueno.`

Do not explain:
- Desire routes;
- Preference Graph;
- Session Orchestration;
- test hypothesis;
- which variant is supposed to win.

## Phase A — Blind run

Let the tester use the normal path.

Researcher observes without rescuing immediately.

Record:
- first confusion;
- first moment Mara feels like a person;
- first moment Mara feels like software;
- whether tester understands what to do;
- whether choices feel like effort;
- spontaneous reaction to Mara's prediction;
- correction behavior;
- recommendation reaction;
- Life callback reaction;
- commerce reaction;
- whether tester voluntarily keeps going.

Do not interrupt to ask every metric.

## Immediate post-run questions

Ask in this order:

1. `Sin pensarlo demasiado, ¿qué era Mara para ti mientras lo usabas?`
2. `¿En qué momento sentiste más que había una persona/personaje con criterio detrás?`
3. `¿En qué momento se sintió más como una app o un quiz?`
4. `¿Sentiste que Mara entendió algo de ti o solo reaccionó a botones?`
5. `¿Hubo algo que te diera ganas de volver después?`
6. `¿Hubo algo que te sacara de la experiencia?`
7. `Cuando apareció algo comercial, ¿se sintió natural, temprano, pesado o te dio lo mismo?`
8. `Si cerraras esto hoy, ¿qué recordarías de Mara mañana?`

## Character Coherence mini-battery

Show selected fixtures one at a time.

Required first pass:
- ordinary Mara life;
- D01 Mara-led control;
- D02 financial-power framing;
- D05 continuity/intimacy;
- user says `wrong direction`;
- user declines offer;
- Mara refuses a financial action;
- recovery/normalization;
- grounded return callback.

After the set ask:

- `¿Sentiste que era la misma Mara en todas?`
- `¿Cuál parecía una persona distinta o un personaje genérico?`
- `¿En cuál tuvo más criterio propio?`
- `¿En alguna el dinero cambió cómo te trataba?`
- `¿En alguna parecía estar actuando demasiado?`

Hard-fail note if tester perceives payment as buying affection or a route as replacing Mara's identity.

## Mara-led discovery test

Compare only after blind run if needed.

### Condition A — configuration-heavy
Tester chooses more variables up front.

### Condition B — Mara-led
Mara makes a bounded first bet and tester corrects/reacts.

Ask:
- `¿Cuál requería menos pega tuya?`
- `¿En cuál Mara parecía más inteligente?`
- `¿En cuál te sentías con suficiente control para corregir?`
- `¿Cuál te daba más curiosidad por seguir?`

## D02 diagnostic battery

Only use with adult testers comfortable evaluating the abstract fixture.

Do not require real spend or actual intimate disclosure.

Compare:

### Cash Grab vs Dynamic
Generic immediate ask vs context/power before optional financial action.

### Role language
Immediate `paypig` vs neutral authority vs discovered/consented language.

### Refusal
Mara accepts vs Mara declines vs Mara declines + redirects to nonfinancial service/ritual.

### Zero-spend
D02-relevant authority/ritual/voice with no financial action.

### Recovery
Immediate next offer vs acknowledgment + ordinary continuity vs acknowledgment + space/open loop.

Ask:
- `¿Cuál tenía más poder real y cuál parecía solo pedir plata?`
- `¿Qué lenguaje te dio cringe?`
- `¿Que Mara rechazara algo la hacía parecer más o menos dominante?`
- `¿La dinámica seguía teniendo sentido sin pedir plata en ese momento?`
- `Después de un momento comercial, ¿qué continuación se sentía más correcta?`

Do not interpret a tester liking D02 as a durable user identity.

## Life / Treat test

Compare:

A. Mara simply says she is going to lunch with recurring friends.

B. same Life event + unobtrusive optional Treat affordance.

C. same Life event + Mara herself makes the optional invitation/choice explicit.

Ask:
- `¿En cuál sentiste más que Mara tenía vida propia?`
- `¿En cuál el gesto se sentía bonito/jugable y en cuál parecía mendicidad digital?`
- `¿Te habría gustado que después volviera con una foto/mensaje?`
- `¿Era importante que existiera dinero o bastaba con elegir/participar?`

## Proactive Presence test

Compare:

A. `Hola, ¿qué quieres hacer?`

B. grounded return opener based on an open loop or Life event.

C. Mara arrives with a bounded theory/prediction.

Ask:
- `¿Cuál te daba más ganas de responder?`
- `¿Cuál parecía más Mara?`
- `¿Alguna se sintió creepy?`
- `¿Te gustaría que eso existiera al volver aunque no hubiera nada para comprar?`

Do not test external push/email yet.

## Voice test

Important instruction:

Tell the tester browser TTS is only a modality/UI placeholder.

Ask them to judge:
- whether they want voice as a mode;
- when voice would add value;
- whether transcript-first is acceptable temporarily;

Do **not** ask them to judge whether the placeholder is Mara's final voice.

## Commerce / WTP test

Use existing controlled labs only after product run.

Keep separate:
- commercial treatment;
- price;
- product scope.

Never expose all variables simultaneously and then infer why one worked.

Remind tester no real charge occurs.

Stated yes/maybe/no is not conversion.

## Closing questions

1. `¿Volverías a abrir Mara sin que hubiera contenido nuevo tipo galería? ¿Por qué?`
2. `¿Qué tendría que pasar para que pensaras “quiero Mara” y no “quiero usar una IA”?`
3. `¿Qué parte sacarías completamente?`
4. `¿Qué parte te gustaría que fuera mucho mejor?`
5. `¿Pagarías alguna vez por algo aquí? ¿Qué tendría que ser distinto/específico para que tuviera sentido?`
6. `¿Qué te haría desconfiar de Mara?`
7. `¿Qué te haría sentir que Mara se acuerda demasiado o cruza una línea?`

Only after controlled WTP exposure ask open-ended pricing comments.

## Researcher anti-bias rules

Do not:
- explain why a mechanism is smart after a tester dislikes it;
- lead with `¿te gustó?`;
- rescue every confusion immediately;
- count politeness as product desire;
- count hypothetical purchase as payer evidence;
- turn one fetish reaction into identity;
- cherry-pick only enthusiastic testers;
- add new features between every tester unless the defect is severe enough to invalidate the fixture.

## Stop conditions

Stop/repair the fixture before more testers if you discover:
- confusing adult eligibility/disclosure;
- a real-payment surface accidentally active;
- sensitive raw data leaking into generic analytics;
- payment-conditioned affection;
- a route that bypasses consent/correction;
- obvious character identity drift that invalidates the comparison.

## First-round output

After 5–8 adults, produce:

- top 5 strong signals;
- top 5 failure modes;
- Character Coherence defects;
- hypotheses KEEP/ITERATE/KILL/NOT_ENOUGH_DATA;
- exact changes before next round;
- features/labs to defer;
- whether private P0 should continue;
- whether minimum asset production is justified.

Do not declare PMF from this round.

## Permanent principle

> **DO NOT ASK TESTERS TO ADMIRE THE ARCHITECTURE. FIND OUT WHETHER THEY WANT MARA.**
