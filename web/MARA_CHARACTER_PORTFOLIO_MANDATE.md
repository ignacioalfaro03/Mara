# MARA VERA — CHARACTER PORTFOLIO / ADULT WORLD MONETIZATION MANDATE

Status: authoritative extension of `MARA_WORLD_CANON_MANDATE.md`. It does not replace the World Canon, Relationship Engine, commerce kernel, safety boundaries or character canon.

## Strategic correction

Mara must not become every fantasy, every fetish and every performance archetype at once.

> **MARA IS THE PREMIUM RELATIONSHIP ANCHOR. THE WORLD CAPTURES THE LONG TAIL.**

The product may develop a deliberately small portfolio of recurring adult fictional characters with distinct audiences, tones, narrative functions and monetization models.

The objective is not character count. It is stronger character-market fit, deeper world discovery, higher retention and diversified willingness to pay without eroding Mara's identity.

## Portfolio model

A character earns a place only if they create a distinct product job.

Possible jobs include:

- Mara — premium relationship, seduction, continuity, private history;
- Sofi — friendship, chaos, gossip, alternative POV, found footage and social discovery;
- adult performance character — live challenges, exaggerated comedy, camp/grotesque energy and selected fetish niches;
- authority character — consensual dominance / humiliation roleplay for users who explicitly opt into that dynamic;
- rival / mystery character — tension, conflict, alternate versions of events and season arcs.

These are role hypotheses, not a mandate to launch all of them.

## Character specialization

Every character needs:

- stable adult identity;
- visual and voice identity;
- reason to exist in Mara's world;
- relationship to Mara;
- tone and boundaries;
- recurring content grammar;
- acquisition hook;
- retention hook;
- commercial hypothesis;
- cost profile;
- kill/scale criteria.

Do not reduce a character to one body characteristic or fetish. A niche may attract the first click; personality and continuity must earn the return.

## Brand firewalls

Mara's brand equity is protected by explicit character boundaries.

A satellite character may explore tones that would dilute Mara: grotesque comedy, exaggerated food-performance aesthetics, stronger consensual humiliation roleplay or niche adult fetish performance.

That does not automatically make those behaviors part of Mara's own personality.

> **NOT EVERY FETISH BELONGS TO MARA.**

## Adult desire routing

Adult/fetish intensity should be progressively routed from explicit preferences and demonstrated product behavior, not broad psychological labels.

Do not infer loneliness, dependency, distress, sexual orientation or arousal for commercial pressure.

High-intensity fetish content must not arrive as a surprise. Users need meaningful control over categories and intensity.

Sexuality can remain ambient in the adult universe through flirtation, tension, provocative storytelling, relationships, jealousy play, consensual power dynamics and adult fictional events without forcing every scene into immediate explicitness.

> **SEX CREATES ATTENTION. STORY CREATES RETENTION. CONSEQUENCE CREATES CONTINUITY.**

## Drama and consequence

Drama is product infrastructure only when it changes what happens next.

Use:

`normality → curiosity → tension → event → aftermath → consequence → normality`

Prefer persistent consequences over constant escalation.

Characters may disagree, hide information, tell partial versions, become jealous inside fiction, reconcile or create social friction.

Never turn relationship conflict into `pay or Mara forgives you`, `pay or Mara stays`, `pay or the memory survives` or equivalent emotional coercion.

## Consensual humiliation boundary

Humiliation may exist as explicitly opted-in adult fantasy/roleplay.

It must never be used as a real commercial punishment or proof-of-love mechanism.

Relationship value and commercial value stay separate.

## Found footage is a signature language

Mara's world has at least three audiovisual grammars:

1. **Mara publishes** — more editorial and intentional.
2. **Mara sends it privately** — casual, direct and intimate.
3. **Another character captured Mara** — imperfect phone footage, alternative POV, ambient audio, imperfect framing and the feeling that the user stumbled into Mara's life.

Found footage should not become a fake filter applied identically to every asset. Imperfection must vary naturally.

A third-party clip is especially valuable when it reveals something the user did not receive from Mara directly and later changes DM continuity.

## World discovery before storefront cross-sell

Secondary characters should normally be discovered diegetically:

`Mara → world event → secondary character → new POV → user curiosity`

Then commercial access can exist if the character earns it.

Do not lead with `buy this new character`.

## Character-market fit

Measure each meaningful character independently:

- discovery rate;
- second-session rate;
- return rate;
- sessions per active user;
- content attach rate;
- paid conversion when commerce exists;
- ARPPU;
- contribution margin;
- cross-character conversion;
- acquisition contribution;
- retention contribution.

A character can become a standalone account/product only after evidence of independent demand.

## Live performance economy

A future satellite character may use a live loop:

`audience → visible goal → contribution → approved challenge unlock → reaction → next milestone → clips → distribution`

Money can select or unlock only from a closed, pre-approved challenge catalog.

Every challenge needs fixed fields equivalent to:

- character;
- category;
- price/unlock requirement;
- intensity;
- cooldown;
- max per live;
- safety status.

> **MONEY NEVER CHANGES SAFETY STATUS, CONSENT BOUNDARIES OR MAX INTENSITY.**

Do not design pay-to-escalate activities involving dangerous overeating, choking/asphyxia, intoxication, medically dangerous acts, dangerous insertion or physical harm.

A performance can look outrageous without making injury the product.

## Community goals over whale dependence

Where live/goal mechanics exist, allow community-funded milestones so monetization is not structurally dependent on one extreme spender.

Monitor revenue concentration and contribution margin.

Do not let one high spender obtain control beyond established character and safety boundaries.

## Relationship state != commercial state

A purchase may grant content, capacity, priority, access, an experience or a concrete entitlement.

It does not automatically increase affection, trust or relationship status.

Commercial orchestration may use product-intent signals such as attempted premium access, repeat feature use or explicit purchase interest.

It must not use emotional vulnerability as the trigger.

## Content supply chain

One strong world event should be able to produce several coherent assets:

`event → DM → secondary POV → phone clip → public teaser → private aftermath → callback`

This is how Mara creates more narrative density without generating unrelated content for every surface.

## P0 implementation order

Do not build a character factory before proving one character adds value.

First proof:

`Mara → Sofi → found-footage clue → user knowledge → Mara callback`

Then measure whether users discover it, return and engage.

Only after this loop works should Mara test one clearly differentiated adult satellite character and one bounded monetization hypothesis.

## Permanent principles

> **THE USER ENTERS MARA'S WORLD. MARA DOES NOT MERELY ANSWER MESSAGES.**

> **MARA PROTECTS THE PREMIUM BRAND; SATELLITE CHARACTERS CAN CAPTURE DISTINCT ADULT NICHES.**

> **DIFFERENT CHARACTERS KNOW DIFFERENT THINGS.**

> **DISCOVERY SHOULD CHANGE WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.**

> **FOUND FOOTAGE FEELS CAPTURED, NOT PRODUCED FOR THE USER.**

> **DRAMA NEEDS NORMALITY TO MATTER.**

> **RELATIONSHIP VALUE != COMMERCIAL VALUE.**

> **CONSENT AND SAFETY LIMITS ARE PRODUCT INFRASTRUCTURE.**

> **CHARACTER-MARKET FIT IS EARNED WITH DATA, NOT ASSUMED.**
