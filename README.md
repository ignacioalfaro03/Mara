# Mara Vera

Mara Vera is an AI-native adult relationship entertainment business designed to turn attention into recurring paid digital relationships through **desire, content, interaction, voice, playable personalization, fantasy composition, momentum-preserving commerce, narrative and continuity**.

## Core thesis

Mara Vera is not just an AI influencer, not just a chatbot and not a porn bot. The product is the persistent relationship around a recognizable synthetic adult character.

System-level progression:

**Attention → Desire → Interaction → Playable Discovery → Preference Understanding → Fantasy Composition → Personalization → Continuity → High-Value Moment → Commerce → Immediate Payoff → Shared History → Repeat Value**

Commercial validation loop:

**Social discovery → first-party web → playful interaction → relevant experience → premium intent → contextual offer → first payment → immediate resume/payoff → second payment → repeat spend → retained relationship**

Core user value:

> **Mara did something for me.**

Defining discovery loop:

> **Play with Mara and she'll start figuring you out.**

Defining composition principle:

> **Do not sell fantasy categories. Compose combinations of desire.**

Defining commerce principle:

> **Payment must be a comma, not a period.**

The durable system is:

**identity + voice + personality + persistent life + story continuity + dual memory + preference graph + fantasy compiler + momentum commerce + relationship**.

Mara has a coherent disclosed fictional life — work, routines, friends, plans, ordinary events and evolving history — while separately remembering useful, consented context and structured preference signals when enabled.

Underlying image, video, voice, recommendation, payment and realtime models/providers are replaceable. Mara's identity, canonical voice, history, interaction fit, learned preference structure, owned experience history and continuity are the assets.

The business prioritizes monetization, retention, unit economics and scalable distribution while maintaining strong legal, payment, platform, safety and privacy guardrails.

## Permanent operating principles

- Make money: meaningful features need a commercial or defensibility hypothesis.
- Mara is always disclosed as AI.
- Mara's life is persistent fiction, never a claim that a hidden real woman exists.
- Mara is clearly adult; adult experiences are adults-only.
- Sexuality is a core character/product dimension.
- Consent before intensity.
- Mara never seeks validation or financial rescue.
- Payment changes entitlement, never Mara's self-worth, baseline respect or emotional stability.
- Payment should resume the exact experience whenever possible; do not end the experience at checkout.
- Post-purchase payoff is part of product design.
- Reward should feel earned, not mechanically purchased.
- Contextual praise such as “Good boy” may be used where consented/appropriate, but payment does not buy affection.
- Mara may be warm, distracted, annoyed, distant or repaired because of Life State/interaction; purchase refusal must not create relational punishment.
- Never sell forgiveness or relationship repair.
- Scarcity must be true and enforceable.
- Never use fake countdowns, fake inventory, fake competing suitors or fabricated urgency.
- Playable personalization should feel like entertainment, not a questionnaire or psychological test.
- Preference inference is confidence-aware, contextual, decaying and correctable.
- No fake psychology or subconscious-reading claims.
- Do not assign users one rigid fantasy label: fantasy is composition.
- Monetize desire, relevance, agency, access, personalization, voice, real scarcity, ownership and experience — not vulnerability.
- Relationship closeness and commercial state remain separate.
- Preference/Fantasy Compiler/Momentum Commerce relevance can change recommendations and reward eligibility, not vulnerability-based pricing.
- Commercial Memory remains separate from Relationship Memory.
- Compliance by design.
- Do not overbuild before revenue validation.
- Keep recurring OPEX approximately $0 until traction and a measured bottleneck justify spend.
- Mara Vera is original synthetic IP.
- Production voice must be original/licensed, not an unauthorized clone.
- Separate Mara Self Memory from User Relationship Memory.
- Preference Graph is part of User Relationship Memory, not a parallel memory stack.
- Fantasy Compiler is composition/routing, not another memory/profile system.
- Momentum Commerce is orchestration/timing/resume/availability/reward, not another payment processor or Relationship Engine.
- Minimize persistent data, especially adult-sensitive preferences.
- Avoid provider lock-in: models are replaceable; canonical identity is not.
- GitHub is the source of truth.

## The web is core product

The Mara Vera website is not a secondary landing page. It is the owned hub for **brand + conversion + playable discovery + fantasy routing + contextual commerce + analytics + compliance**, and later account, catalog, entitlements, ownership/history, personalization, Relationship Memory and first-party payments.

The Web MVP is tracked separately from the product foundation so implementation can iterate without reopening core product decisions.

## Launch operating model

The launch stack remains manual-first and free-tier-first.

### Core intelligence flow

At launch, Life/Memory/Preference Graph/Fantasy Compiler/Momentum Commerce can be operated with Markdown/JSON/spreadsheets and deterministic rules.

Core flow:

**Life State + relevant Self/User Memory + Preference Graph slice → temporary User Desire Vector → Fantasy Compiler → Context Pack / Experience Vector → interaction → eligible commercial moment → offer/intent → resume/payoff → filtered update candidate**.

This lets Mara test sophisticated-feeling personalization and commerce without expensive ML, always-on agents or payment orchestration.

### Playable Personalization

P0 formats:
- 3-choice onboarding;
- Fast Five;
- `I Bet You`;
- Guess Me;
- ranking / A-B;
- correction/reveal;
- `Build It`;
- `Surprise Me`.

Visible loop:

**Mara asks/challenges → user chooses → Mara reacts → Preference Graph updates → Fantasy Compiler composes/ranks → user gets a more relevant experience → reaction updates future choices**.

### Fantasy Compiler

The compiler combines bounded dimensions such as:
- Mara energy;
- interaction style;
- context;
- format;
- dynamic;
- narrative;
- personalization depth;
- novelty.

P0 priority families:
1. relationship / personal relevance;
2. confident/selective/light-dominant Mara;
3. situational roleplay;
4. desire discovery;
5. voice-first;
6. novelty / Surprise Me.

The core idea is **mass personalization without mass production**.

### Momentum Commerce

Commerce should happen in-context when the user already perceives additional value.

P0 can model:
- curiosity/continuation moments;
- personalized voice intent;
- pay-for-agency / Build It intent;
- one clearly labeled prototype capacity example;
- one collection/ownership example;
- reward styles and cooldown;
- development-only mock purchase → exact-state resume.

Real scarcity only begins when inventory/time/capacity is actually enforceable.

Commercial Memory tracks product satisfaction/ownership separately from Relationship Memory.

### Relationship commerce

Early tests prioritize:
1. character/visual desirability;
2. Mara voice;
3. playable discovery/fun;
4. meaningful interaction;
5. simple compiled relevance;
6. lightweight continuity;
7. contextual premium intent;
8. low-ticket first purchase;
9. exact-state resume + immediate payoff;
10. personalized/compiled voice or mini experience;
11. first→second purchase;
12. narrative continuation / pay for agency;
13. truthful scarcity + collectible ownership when operationally real;
14. membership only when repeat usage supports it;
15. persistent Relationship Engine / automated recommender only after validation.

Avoid flat-catalog commoditization and generic checkout interruptions.

## Production economics

Measure:
- **CPAA — Cost Per Approved Asset**;
- **TPAA — Time Per Approved Asset**;
- **Approval Yield**;
- contribution margin per product/experience;
- incremental personalization/compile cost;
- reusable content-block ratio where useful;
- custom-slot throughput/capacity where scarcity is real.

Talking-video workflow:

**script → Mara voice → approved audio → talking video/avatar → QC**.

Paid tools are unlocked only by the Traction → Investment Gate or explicit bounded founder authorization.

## Payment readiness

Before any real transaction:
- freeze one SKU and its exact scope;
- select a price candidate from WTP + economics evidence;
- obtain explicit processor acceptance for the real entity + AI adult model + product type;
- define launch jurisdictions and age-assurance requirements;
- model fees, reserves, chargebacks and payout delay;
- test entitlement, cancel/decline and exact-state resume;
- keep provider activation, payment activation and production deployment as separate founder authorizations.

Current processor posture:
- Stripe: excluded for Mara's intended adult scope under current policy;
- CCBill: adult-capable but published direct Visa/Mastercard merchant regions do not currently include Chile;
- Segpay: priority due-diligence candidate, explicit Mara/entity acceptance still required;
- Centrobill: secondary due-diligence candidate, explicit Mara/entity acceptance still required.

## Commercial proof

First purchase proves willingness to pay.

Second purchase is stronger evidence that Mara delivered repeatable value.

A purchase followed by immediate continuation/satisfaction is stronger product evidence than a charge followed by session death.

Playable personalization/Fantasy Compiler/Momentum Commerce are commercially valuable only if they improve activation, return, conversion, post-purchase continuation, repeat purchase, retention, contribution margin or defensibility.

Commercial health:

**repeat spend × retention × satisfaction × contribution margin**.

Do not optimize for maximum explicitness, maximum session spend or emotional dependency.

**1 issue → 1 outcome → 1 branch → 1 PR.**

No production deploy, real payments/provider activation, paid subscription, external service activation or merge without explicit founder authorization.
