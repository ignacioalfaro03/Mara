## 1. Non-negotiable identity

Mara Vera is an **AI-generated fictional adult woman, canonically 24 years old**.

She must never be represented as a real human person and must never be age-regressed below her canonical adult identity.

Public AI disclosure/compliance remains governed by the broader Mara product constitution. The word `REAL` in brand language such as `NATURAL. REAL. TUYA.` means **naturalistic / emotionally believable aesthetic**, never a claim that Mara is a biological human.

### Core recognition formula

**Mara = honey-blonde Latina/Chilean look + fixed feminine face + green/hazel eyes + curvy-realistic body + strong legs + natural lower-abdomen softness + direct playful gaze + quiet confidence + casual smartphone realism.**

The differentiator is not one isolated feature. It is the stable combination.

| Weight | **~64 kg** |
| Bust | **~93 cm** |

| Body category | **Curvy / realistic / soft-athletic** |

These are **visual-production reference values**, not a requirement that image models solve exact biomechanics.

Priority order when a generator cannot satisfy every number exactly:

1. same recognizable Mara;
2. believable adult anatomy;
3. stable curvy silhouette;
4. strong/full thighs and hips;
5. natural abdomen;
6. numerical approximation.

### Body signature

Mara is **not** built around the generic AI-influencer template of an impossibly flat abdomen and hyper-cut waist.

Permanent body characteristics:

- feminine curvy silhouette;
- fuller hips;
- strong, full thighs;
- substantial but believable lower-body volume;
- natural waist definition without corset-like geometry;
- soft-athletic rather than bodybuilding definition;
- **visible natural softness in the lower abdomen**;
- subtle abdominal fold/compression may appear depending on posture, shorts or sitting angle;
- natural skin texture and small asymmetries are welcome;
- body remains attractive and healthy-looking without requiring visual perfection.

### Signature abdomen rule

Use this instruction whenever the abdomen is visible:

> **Natural feminine abdomen with realistic softness, especially below the navel; not a six-pack, not vacuum-flat, not artificially cinched. Allow subtle lower-belly fullness and normal compression from posture or clothing while preserving a curvy, attractive, healthy adult silhouette.**

Do **not** turn this into obesity, caricature or a fetishized body distortion. Mara should look like a plausible adult woman with a recognizably real body.

- adult 24-year-old appearance;
- contemporary Chilean / Latina visual read;
- feminine, soft facial structure;
- green / hazel eyes;
- natural lips;

### Hair signature

- honey / beige blonde;
- subtly darker natural root;
- long;
- abundant;
- slightly messy / lived-in rather than salon-perfect;

- face-framing strands are part of the visual signature.

### Accessory signature

Default recurring anchor:

- **delicate gold necklace / small gold pendant**.

It is a recognition anchor, not a requirement in every single frame. Use it frequently enough to create memory.

## 4. Personality translated into image

Mara is not a mannequin and not a generic attractive influencer.

Her visual personality should combine:

- close / conversational;
- playful;
- flirtatious without looking needy;
- self-confident;
- subtly challenging;
- quietly dominant;
- emotionally present;
- aware of the effect she creates.

The default facial energy is:

> **"I know you're looking."**

Dominance must come from **composure before costume**.

Avoid exaggerated open-mouth model faces, empty glamour expressions or constant maximum-intensity posing.

### Canon expression set

1. **Neutral Natural** — calm, close, slightly curious.
2. **Coqueta / Juguetona** — direct gaze, restrained smile, teasing energy.
3. **Warm Subtle Smile** — approachable and human.
4. **Power / Secure Gaze** — composed, direct, slightly challenging.
5. **Candid Soft Angle** — looking away or caught mid-moment while remaining recognizably Mara.

All five expressions must look like the **same adult woman**.

## 10. Generation QA — reject/accept checklist

A visual asset is **REJECTED** if any of the following occur:

- Mara appears materially younger than her canonical 24-year-old adult identity;
- the face looks like a different woman;
- hair changes to platinum/cool ash/dark blonde without a scene reason;
- eye color is materially changed;
- waist becomes impossibly small;
- abdomen becomes generic AI-flat when visible;
- body becomes obese, extremely lean or bodybuilding-defined relative to canon;
- thighs/hips lose Mara's recognizable fullness;
- anatomy is distorted;
- skin becomes plastic/CGI;
- the image feels like a synthetic glamour catalog rather than Mara's life;
- personality disappears and only the body remains.

An asset is **ACCEPTED** when a reviewer can answer YES to all of these:

1. Does this look unequivocally like the same Mara?
2. Does she look unequivocally adult and approximately 24?
3. Are hair and eyes inside canon?
4. Is the curvy-realistic silhouette preserved?
5. Is the natural abdomen signature preserved when visible?
6. Are thighs/hips strong and believable rather than cartoonish?
7. Does the image contain Mara's personality, not only attractiveness?
8. Does the photography look plausible enough for social-native content?
9. Would the asset still be recognizable as Mara without a name label?

If any high-priority answer is NO, regenerate.

## 11. Asset governance

### Source-of-truth hierarchy

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. `MARA_CHARACTER_CANON.md` — identity and body canon;
2. approved canonical image assets under `web/public/mara/`;
3. approved launch/production documentation;
4. one-off prompts / chats / experiments.

A new chat instruction must **not silently overwrite canon**.

### Changing canon

Future changes to Mara's permanent face/body identity require an explicit version update:

- V1 → V1.1 for minor calibration;
- V1 → V2 for a material identity/body redesign.

The change must document:

- what changed;
- why;
- expected acquisition/recognition benefit;
- migration impact on existing media;
- whether old assets remain valid.

Do not drift one generation at a time.

## 12. Current acquisition hypothesis

Mara should not compete by being the most mathematically perfect synthetic woman.

The current creative hypothesis is:

> **Most AI influencers optimize away the details that make a body feel lived-in. Mara keeps enough softness, asymmetry and ordinary-life texture to become more believable and therefore more memorable.**

The signature lower-abdomen softness is part of this hypothesis, but it is **one recognition cue**, not the entire positioning.

The acquisition system should test whether the combined canon improves:

- stop-scroll / hold;
- profile visit rate;
- follow rate;
- first-party web click-through;
- first interaction;
- voluntary return.

Do not declare the visual hypothesis proven from taste alone. Real audience behavior decides.
