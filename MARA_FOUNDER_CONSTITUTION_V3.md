# MARA — FOUNDER CONSTITUTION V3

Status: **SOLE HIGHEST-PRIORITY COMPANY AUTHORITY**
Effective: **2026-09-20**

Founder boundary: **NO MERGE unless Ignacio explicitly writes `mergea`.**

> **THE CREATOR IS THE PROTAGONIST. MARA IS THE BUSINESS INFRASTRUCTURE BEHIND HER SITE.**

# 1. COMPANY THESIS

Mara is a **web-first creator commerce, audience intelligence and demand network**.

Each creator or influencer can operate **her own site inside Mara** and use it as a direct commercial destination for her audience.

Canonical framing:

> **MARA HELPS CREATORS TURN AUDIENCE INTO A BETTER BUSINESS THROUGH THEIR OWN SITE, DEMAND INTELLIGENCE, COMMERCE, MEMORY AND FULFILLMENT.**

The visible product is the creator's site.
The compounding system underneath is Mara.

# 2. WHAT IS EXPLICITLY DISCARDED

**Mara Vera is no longer part of the product thesis.**
She is not the protagonist, creator zero, acquisition persona, default avatar or structural template.

**Creator World / World is no longer a product concept.**
Do not use World as user-facing positioning, navigation or creator language.
Legacy database tables, API names, telemetry events or migration identifiers containing `world` may remain temporarily as compatibility internals until a safe migration is justified. They must not drive UX language.

Historical files may mention Mara Vera or Worlds only when clearly subordinate to V3.

# 3. PRIMARY USER MODEL

Mara supports:
- recognized creators and influencers;
- pseudonymous creators;
- privacy-protected creators;
- virtual or character-led identities where legitimate.

The primary commercial case is a creator who already has an audience elsewhere and sends that audience to her Mara site.

Privacy is a configurable capability, not the defining identity of every creator.

# 4. THE CREATOR SITE

Canonical public object:

# CREATOR SITE

Conceptual URL:
`mara.com/<creator>`

It should feel like the creator's official digital property, not a seller card inside a marketplace.

A Creator Site can progressively contain:
- identity and bio;
- links and social presence;
- products and offers;
- experiences;
- demand requests;
- memberships and drops;
- community signals;
- fan history;
- personalization;
- fulfillment status.

Modules are optional. The creator should not configure complexity before she has value.

# 5. FAN PROMISE

> **Mara brings you closer to the creators you care about.**
The fan should not need to understand CRM, demand intelligence or SaaS.
The experience should be mobile-first, direct and creator-led.

# 6. CREATOR PROMISE

> **Mara helps you turn your audience into a better business.**

Creator OS must answer:
- what did I sell;
- what does my audience want;
- what should I offer next;
- who is close to buying;
- what must I fulfill;
- what action has the highest expected value.

Action beats dashboard decoration.

# 7. CANONICAL ECONOMIC LOOP

`CREATOR → CREATOR SITE → AUDIENCE → IDENTITY/MEMORY → WANT → DEMAND → COMMITMENT → AGGREGATED DEMAND → OFFER → TRANSACTION → FULFILLMENT → OUTCOME → MEMORY → NEXT BEST ACTION → RETURN`

WANT, PLEDGE, COMMIT and PURCHASE remain distinct economic states.

# 8. FOUR PLATFORM ENGINES

1. **Creator Identity & Site** — creator identity, site configuration, branding, privacy and public presence.
2. **Demand Intelligence** — requests, clustering, WTP, WANT/PLEDGE/COMMIT and opportunity detection.
3. **Commerce & Fulfillment** — offers, checkout, entitlements, delivery, refunds/payout rails when compliant.
4. **Audience Memory & Retention** — history, preferences, outcomes, next-best-action and relevant cross-creator discovery.

# 9. NETWORK ADVANTAGE

Mara is not valuable because it can render profile pages.
Its potential moat is the combination of:
`CREATOR NETWORK + FAN IDENTITY + DEMAND GRAPH + TRANSACTION HISTORY + OUTCOME MEMORY + COMMERCE INTELLIGENCE`.

Each creator relationship should feel owned and direct while Mara compounds network-level intelligence under explicit privacy rules.
# 10. WEB-FIRST RULE

Launch is web-first.
A creator should be comfortable placing her Mara URL in Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube or another profile.

Creator Sites must be:
- mobile-first;
- public-renderable where intended;
- shareable;
- SEO-ready;
- fast;
- clear about commercial terms.

Custom domains are a future capability, not a launch dependency.

# 11. CURRENT TRUST BOUNDARY

The current alpha may remain adult-only while existing legal/content/payment assumptions require it.
Do not activate real payments, payouts, production migrations or external spend without explicit authorization and provider compatibility.

# 12. NORTH STAR

# FULFILLED DEMAND GMV

Leading indicators may include creator activation, site visits, verified demand GMV, demand-to-offer conversion and repeat purchase.
Followers, likes and generated-content volume are not the North Star.

# 13. BUILD FILTER

Build only if it materially improves creator activation, demand quality, conversion, fulfillment, repeat purchase, creator earnings, contribution margin, privacy/trust or operating leverage.

Do not build now:
- metaverse or 3D;
- generic social feed;
- unlimited AI chat;
- native mobile as a launch dependency;
- crypto/token systems;
- feature-specific labs when an existing surface can test the hypothesis.

# 14. PRODUCT LANGUAGE

Use:
- Creator Site;
- tu sitio en Mara;
- Creator OS;
- audience;
- demand;
- offer;
- history;
- activity.

Do not use as current product language:
- Mara Vera;
- Creator World;
- World;
- connected Worlds;
- virtual character as company definition.
