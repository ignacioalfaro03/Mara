# MARA — PRIVATE DEMAND NETWORK FOUNDER AMENDMENT V2

Status: **HIGHEST-PRIORITY STRATEGIC EXTENSION**  
Effective: **2026-09-08**  
Authority: Founder direction. When this amendment conflicts with older Mara-only, chatbot-first, storefront-first, creator-only or experience-only framing, this amendment wins.

> **MARA CONVERTS PRIVATE DESIRE INTO AGGREGATED DEMAND, AND AGGREGATED DEMAND INTO PRODUCTS, EXPERIENCES AND ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITIES.**

---

## 0. WHAT CHANGED

The prior demand-to-experience thesis was directionally correct but too narrow.

Demand inside Mara is not only demand for events or physical experiences. It can be demand for:

- digital products;
- creator content;
- collective requests;
- memberships;
- creator collaborations;
- drops;
- merchandise;
- digital experiences;
- physical experiences;
- hybrid experiences;
- roles;
- Hosts;
- local activations;
- products or formats that do not exist yet.

The company-level category becomes:

# PRIVATE DEMAND NETWORK FOR CREATOR WORLDS

Mara Vera remains **Creator Zero** and the first World, not the ceiling of the company.

---

## 1. THE CORE ECONOMIC LOOP

Canonical loop:

`WORLD → COMMUNITY → WANT → PLEDGE → COMMIT → AGGREGATED DEMAND → SUPPLY → UNLOCK → TRANSACTION → FULFILLMENT → MEMORY → RETURN`

The core user action is:

# MAKE IT HAPPEN

The simplest valid input is:

> **What do you want to happen?**

The user does not need to know how it will be produced.

Mara must transform raw desire into structured, privacy-safe and commercially useful demand.

---

## 2. DEMAND FIRST. SUPPLY SECOND.

Whenever practical:

1. capture desire;
2. group similar demand;
3. measure willingness to pay;
4. collect stronger commitment;
5. only then create / match supply.

Do not force Creators to continuously guess what to make.

Creator-facing product should increasingly answer:

> **WHAT DOES MY COMMUNITY ALREADY WANT FROM ME?**

---

## 3. CREATOR WORLDS, NOT CREATOR PROFILES

A Creator should progressively own a persistent **World** made of:

- identity;
- community;
- active demand;
- content;
- drops;
- memberships;
- rituals / challenges;
- collections;
- memories;
- history;
- status;
- Creator opportunities;
- future Hosts / licensed execution.

The desired user feeling is:

> **I BELONG TO THIS WORLD.**

not:

> **I FOLLOW THIS PROFILE.**

The Creator provides gravity. The community provides activity.

---

## 4. RETENTION REQUIRES CONSEQUENCE

Mara should not rely on endless chat or daily manual publishing for retention.

A user's actions should leave a trace.

Examples:

- created a request;
- joined a demand;
- pledged;
- committed;
- helped unlock something;
- purchased;
- attended;
- contributed;
- completed a collection;
- performed a trusted role.

The product must progressively support:

# MY HISTORY

and later:

# SINCE YOU LEFT

Retention loop:

`ENTER → SOMETHING IS HAPPENING → PARTICIPATE → LEAVE A TRACE → SOMETHING CHANGES → SOMETHING REMAINS PENDING → RETURN`

---

## 5. PRIVACY IS CORE PRODUCT

Mara should optimize for:

> **MAXIMUM PRIVACY WHEN THE USER WANTS IT. MAXIMUM ACCOUNTABILITY WHERE REQUIRED.**

Long-term model:

# VERIFIED PSEUDONYMITY

Mara may know verified identity where required for age, payment, safety, legal or fraud reasons, while the wider ecosystem sees only the minimum information necessary.

Privacy levels should be modeled separately from commercial status.

Examples:

- PUBLIC;
- PSEUDONYMOUS;
- PRIVATE / INVITE-ONLY.

Do not promise perfect anonymity.

Do not expose identifiable individual demand histories to Hosts, Creators or third parties when aggregated information is sufficient.

---

## 6. DEMAND STATES

User economic signal must be progressive:

### WANT
I want this.

### PLEDGE
I would pay around X.

### COMMIT
If the stated conditions are met, I intend to buy.

### UNLOCKED
Enough qualifying demand exists for fulfillment.

The product must not present WANT, PLEDGE and COMMIT as economically equivalent.

Real payment authorization / deposits remain gated by provider, legal and refund architecture.

---

## 7. COMMUNITY IS AN ECONOMIC ENGINE

Community is not a generic social feed.

Community should create:

- demand;
- participation;
- contribution;
- belonging;
- memory;
- discovery;
- repeat transactions.

Future community primitives include:

- tributes;
- challenges;
- rituals;
- collections;
- events;
- user-created demand;
- Creator requests;
- fan-created contributions;
- status;
- trusted roles.

The system should remain useful even when the Creator is offline.

---

## 8. VIP IS A REVENUE LAYER, NOT A CONSENT LAYER

Future VIP products can monetize:

- scarcity;
- priority;
- convenience;
- curation;
- hospitality;
- limited capacity;
- recognition;
- premium production;
- privacy;
- early access;
- exclusive digital artifacts.

Future product families:

- EVENT VIP;
- CREATOR INNER CIRCLE;
- MARA GLOBAL VIP;
- PRIVATE GROUP;
- MARA CONCIERGE.

A higher payment never creates rights over another person's body, consent or participation.

---

## 9. HOST / ROLE / IRL ARE EXPANSION LAYERS

Hosts, Roles, Venues and MARA IRL remain strategically important, but they are not the first proof required.

Future actors may include:

- Creator Host;
- Venue Host;
- Operator;
- event assistant;
- community moderator;
- performer;
- creator representative;
- production support;
- trusted member.

Operating principle:

> **MARA ORCHESTRATES. THIRD PARTIES FULFILL.**

Do not turn Mara into a founder-operated event agency.

Paid operational work must not be disguised as fan volunteering.

A ticket or payment never purchases another person's consent.

---

## 10. MONETIZATION PRIORITY

Initial monetization should favor low-complexity digital demand where one fulfillment can serve many buyers.

Priority order:

1. collective digital Creator requests;
2. digital drops;
3. limited digital experiences;
4. Creator memberships / Inner Circle;
5. repeat purchase / collections;
6. later controlled IRL;
7. later Host / Venue / licensing / concierge layers.

Avoid building expensive operational infrastructure before Mara proves:

`REQUEST → AGGREGATED DEMAND → UNLOCK → PAYMENT → FULFILLMENT → SECOND PURCHASE`

---

## 11. PRIMARY STRATEGIC ASSET

The long-term moat is the combined graph of:

- what people want;
- how many want it;
- which World / Creator it relates to;
- willingness to pay;
- strength of commitment;
- location where applicable;
- preferred fulfillment type;
- privacy requirements;
- transaction history;
- fulfillment quality;
- reputation;
- repeat behavior.

This is the:

# DEMAND GRAPH

Mara should know what a community wants before traditional supply knows what to create.

Do not monetize the Demand Graph by selling identifiable private user histories.

---

## 12. MVP FOUNDER RULE

If a feature does not materially improve at least one of:

- DEMAND;
- FULFILLMENT;
- RETENTION;
- GMV;

it should not enter the MVP.

MVP must prove:

1. a user can express what they want;
2. Mara can represent it as demand;
3. other users can join;
4. users can distinguish WANT / PLEDGE / COMMIT;
5. the Creator / World can see aggregated opportunity;
6. demand can visibly progress toward an unlock;
7. actions become part of user history;
8. the flow supports digital products, not only events.

---

## 13. LOW-MAINTENANCE CONSTRAINT

Founder time is scarce.

Do not build a model requiring the founder or Creator to:

- post every day;
- chat continuously;
- manually coordinate every transaction;
- manually organize every event;
- manually reconcile marketplace payouts;
- act as the default producer.

Preferred architecture:

`AUTOMATION + COMMUNITY ACTIVITY + AGGREGATED DEMAND + THIRD-PARTY FULFILLMENT`
