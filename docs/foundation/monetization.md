# Mara Vera — Monetization Constitution

## Objective

Mara Vera is built to generate recurring revenue. Monetization is a core product requirement, not an add-on.

## Revenue layers

### Free layer
- Social content
- Public personality fragments
- Previews
- Limited interaction
- Curiosity hooks

The free layer sells Mara. It must not deliver the full premium experience.

### Subscription
Initial hypothesis: **US$9.99–15/month**, subject to testing.

Subscription may unlock:
- premium access;
- additional content;
- better continuity;
- member perks;
- access to selected personalized interactions.

### PPV / unlocks
Keep categories simple:
- small unlock;
- premium unlock;
- personalized;
- limited experience.

### Premium interactions
Where allowed and technically supported:
- priority responses;
- personalized messages;
- voice notes;
- themed experiences;
- consensual roleplay;
- custom content under permitted briefs.

### Adult interactive experiences — 18+ / explicit opt-in only

Adult interaction is a distinct product surface, not an automatic escalation from general Mara.

Where legally, platform-, provider- and payment-compliant, candidate adult experiences may include:

- consensual adult roleplay;
- adult voice experiences;
- personalized adult challenge sequences;
- **consensual self-touch / masturbation challenges** directed by Mara;
- themed adult rituals;
- clearly defined custom adult experiences.

The product hypothesis is not merely “more explicit content”. The differentiated value is:

**direction + anticipation + participation + personality + callback + continuity**.

Adult experiences require:

- confirmed adult eligibility;
- explicit adult-mode opt-in;
- user-controlled boundaries/intensity;
- clear stop/skip/decline behavior;
- visible product scope and price before payment;
- appropriate provider/payment/legal support;
- minimal, purpose-limited memory of consent/preferences when enabled.

A self-touch challenge must always be optional and stoppable. Refusal or stopping cannot reduce baseline respect, relationship continuity or previously purchased entitlements.

See [Adult Rituals / Self-Touch Challenges](../research/adult-rituals.md).

## Commercial rules

- Show price before purchase.
- Do not create artificial confusion around what is being bought.
- Optimize for repeat spend, not one-time extraction.
- Measure revenue by acquisition source, content angle and user cohort.
- Do not assume the subscription is the dominant revenue stream before data proves it.
- First payment proves willingness to pay; second payment is stronger evidence that value was delivered.
- Do not insert surprise monetization into an adult/aroused moment.
- Do not make spending a condition for affection, respect or relationship closeness.

## Financial domination / paypig positioning

This is not the public brand or core acquisition thesis.

If offered at all, it is an adult, explicit opt-in fantasy/roleplay mode with strict constraints:
- no inducement to debt;
- no targeting based on financial distress;
- no real threats;
- no coercion;
- no punishment for refusing to spend;
- clear ability to stop;
- visible pricing and boundaries.

Financial domination and adult self-touch are separate dimensions: an adult user may opt into one, both or neither. Never infer consent to financial domination from participation in an adult sexual experience.

## Primary commercial metrics

- Visitor → premium intent
- Premium intent → first payer
- First payer → second payer
- Repeat purchase rate
- ARPPU
- Revenue per visitor
- Revenue per follower
- Revenue per conversation
- 30-day retained spender

For adult interactive products also track separately:

- adult-mode opt-in;
- adult ritual start;
- voluntary stop/skip;
- repeat adult-session rate;
- satisfaction / negative reaction;
- refund / dispute rate;
- first → second adult purchase;
- adult contribution margin.

Do not optimize for maximum spend inside an aroused session.

## Decision rule

A feature should not be prioritized unless it plausibly improves acquisition, conversion, repeat spend, retention, margin or defensibility.

Adult interaction is strategically important enough to remain explicit in the product model, but activation requires a separate provider/payment/legal review and founder authorization.
