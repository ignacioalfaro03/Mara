# Mara Vera — Monetization Constitution

## Objective

Mara Vera is built to generate recurring revenue. Monetization is a core product requirement, not an add-on.

The commercial thesis is **relationship commerce**, not a single subscription paywall:

**desire + personal relevance + continuity + clear paid value → first purchase → second purchase → retained spender**.

See [Monetization Engine](monetization-engine.md) for offer architecture, experiments and unit economics.

## Revenue layers

### Free attention
- social content;
- public personality fragments;
- platform-safe teasing;
- voice/video previews;
- mini stories/open loops;
- limited interaction.

The free layer sells Mara. It must not deliver the full premium experience.

### Free relationship
Candidate launch layer:
- limited conversation;
- preference onboarding;
- previews;
- first voice exposure;
- small choices;
- lightweight/manual callbacks.

This layer exists to convert a follower into a returning user before persistent relationship infrastructure is justified.

### Low-ticket paid unlocks
Keep first-purchase categories simple:
- small unlock;
- premium unlock;
- personalized message/audio;
- continuation;
- themed mini experience;
- simple interactive branch.

Optimize for the **first purchase moment** and then prove value through the **second purchase**.

### Subscription / membership
Membership remains an experimental layer, not the assumed dominant business model.

Initial research pricing ranges are hypotheses only and must not be treated as committed pricing before real demand testing.

Membership may unlock:
- recurring premium access;
- selected content drops;
- better continuity convenience;
- member perks;
- selected personalized interactions;
- voice/narrative access;
- member-only story arcs.

A membership should feel like a qualitatively better ongoing Mara experience, not merely removal of a paywall.

### PPV / narrative commerce
Products may be sold as clearly scoped situations and continuations rather than only flat media packs.

Candidate structures:
- preview → unlock;
- situation → choice → continuation;
- episode → sequel;
- voice + visual bundle;
- story + callback;
- real limited drop.

See [Fantasy Experience Engine](../architecture/fantasy-experience-engine.md).

### Premium interactions / custom experiences
Where allowed and technically supported:
- priority fulfillment where explicitly sold;
- personalized messages;
- voice notes;
- themed experiences;
- consensual roleplay;
- custom content under permitted briefs;
- multi-format bundles;
- user-configured experiences within approved variables.

The future product direction is an **Experience Configurator**, not an unstructured custom-request inbox.

### Adult interactive experiences — 18+ / explicit opt-in only

Adult interaction is a distinct product surface, not an automatic escalation from general Mara.

Where legally, platform-, provider- and payment-compliant, candidate adult experiences may include:
- consensual adult roleplay;
- adult voice experiences;
- personalized adult challenge sequences;
- consensual self-touch / masturbation challenges directed by Mara;
- themed adult rituals;
- clearly defined custom adult experiences.

The differentiated value is not simply “more explicit content”. It is:

**direction + anticipation + participation + personality + callback + continuity**.

Adult experiences require:
- confirmed adult eligibility;
- explicit adult-mode opt-in;
- user-controlled boundaries/intensity;
- clear stop/skip/decline behavior;
- visible product scope and price before payment;
- appropriate provider/payment/legal support;
- minimal, purpose-limited memory of consent/preferences when enabled.

A self-touch challenge must always be optional and stoppable. Refusal or stopping cannot reduce baseline respect, relationship continuity or previously purchased entitlements.

See [Adult Rituals / Self-Touch Challenges](../research/adult-rituals.md) and [Adult Compliance](adult-compliance.md).

## Commercial rules

- Show price before purchase.
- State whether the purchase is one-off or recurring.
- Do not create artificial confusion around what is being bought.
- Optimize for repeat spend, retention, satisfaction and contribution margin—not one-time extraction.
- Measure revenue by acquisition source, content angle, product type and user cohort.
- Do not assume subscription is dominant before data proves it.
- First payment proves willingness to pay; second payment is stronger evidence that value was delivered.
- Do not insert surprise monetization into an adult/aroused moment.
- Do not make spending a condition for affection, respect or relationship closeness.
- Genuine scarcity is allowed; fake scarcity is not.
- High-value users may receive higher-value products and service levels, but never vulnerability-based pressure.

## Financial domination / paypig positioning

This is not the public brand or core acquisition thesis.

If offered at all, it is an adult, explicit opt-in fantasy/roleplay mode with strict constraints:
- no inducement to debt;
- no targeting based on financial distress;
- no access to real bank/credit credentials;
- no real threats;
- no coercion;
- no punishment for refusing to spend;
- clear ability to stop;
- visible pricing and boundaries.

Financial domination and adult self-touch are separate dimensions: an adult user may opt into one, both or neither. Never infer consent to financial domination from participation in another adult sexual experience.

## Primary commercial metrics

- Visitor → premium intent
- Premium intent → first payer
- First payer → second payer
- Repeat purchase rate
- AOV
- ARPU / ARPPU
- Purchase frequency
- Revenue per visitor
- Revenue per follower
- Revenue per content/experience
- 30-day retained spender
- Contribution margin

For narrative/personalized products also track:
- preview → unlock;
- story completion;
- continuation purchase;
- personalized experience conversion;
- voice engagement;
- bundle attach rate.

For adult interactive products track separately:
- adult-mode opt-in;
- adult experience start;
- voluntary stop/skip;
- repeat adult-session rate;
- satisfaction / negative reaction;
- refund / dispute rate;
- first → second adult purchase;
- adult contribution margin.

Do not optimize for maximum spend inside an aroused session.

## Decision rule

A feature should not be prioritized unless it plausibly improves acquisition, conversion, repeat spend, retention, margin or defensibility without creating disproportionate legal, policy, privacy, trust or operating risk.

Adult interaction is strategically important enough to remain explicit in the product model, but activation requires a separate provider/payment/legal review and founder authorization.
