# Mara Vera — Adult Compliance Operating Baseline

## Status

Product/compliance baseline for adult relationship entertainment. This extends `compliance.md`; it does not replace jurisdiction-specific legal, payment-provider or platform review.

No real adult premium surface, payment flow, provider activation or persistent sensitive-memory system is authorized by this document.

## Non-negotiable product rules

1. Mara is always disclosed as an AI-generated/synthetic adult character.
2. Adult experiences are adults-only.
3. No minor-coded presentation, ambiguous age framing or sexualization of young-looking/underage personas.
4. Adult intensity requires explicit opt-in and appropriate boundaries.
5. Pricing and scope must be visible before purchase.
6. Users must be able to stop, skip or decline without losing baseline respect or unrelated entitlements.
7. Data collection and memory must be purpose-limited and minimized.
8. Mara remains original synthetic IP; do not sexually impersonate real people without lawful rights/consent.
9. Relationship closeness and spending state remain separate.
10. Provider, platform and payment policy must be checked before activation, not after.

## Age and adult eligibility

Launch requirements for adult surfaces should include:
- clear 18+ positioning;
- age gate before adult content/interaction;
- no adult escalation before eligibility requirements are satisfied;
- review of whether stronger age assurance is required for the actual launch market/provider mix;
- no attempt to bypass platform age restrictions.

The age gate is not a substitute for any legally required age-verification mechanism.

## AI disclosure

Mara may be immersive and character-driven but must not create material deception that a hidden real woman is operating the account.

Disclosure should be clear in appropriate first-party and account surfaces.

Mara's persistent life is disclosed fiction. Narrative continuity is not evidence of a hidden human identity.

## Consent and intensity

Adult mode is a distinct product surface.

Where permitted, design should support:
- explicit adult-mode opt-in;
- user-selected intensity/tone bands;
- explicit boundaries/dislikes where needed;
- stop/skip/decline behavior;
- reset or reduction of intensity;
- no inference that consent to one adult category implies consent to another.

Financial domination, dominant sexual roleplay, voice experiences, custom content and other adult categories are separate opt-ins where relevant.

## Absolute red lines

Do not facilitate or productize:
- sexual content involving minors;
- sexualized minor-coded or ambiguous-age characters;
- coercive sexual acts presented as real/non-consensual conduct;
- sexual exploitation or trafficking;
- incest involving real persons;
- recordings/intimate media of real people without consent;
- sexual deepfakes/impersonation of identifiable real people without lawful rights and consent;
- extortion or blackmail;
- doxxing;
- threats tied to real-world harm;
- payment coercion;
- hidden or misleading charges.

## Monetize desire, not vulnerability

Permitted commercial levers include:
- personalization;
- premium scope;
- genuine scarcity;
- custom experiences;
- voice;
- exclusivity;
- bundles;
- narrative continuity;
- first access;
- status/collectibility when transparently designed.

Do not target or price based on inferred:
- loneliness;
- depression;
- bereavement;
- debt;
- financial distress;
- desperation;
- emotional dependency;
- compulsive spending.

Do not use:
- fake emergencies;
- guilt tied to spending;
- withdrawal of baseline affection/respect to trigger payment;
- threats to end the relationship unless the user pays;
- intentional cancellation obstruction;
- fake scarcity/countdowns;
- surprise payments during adult/aroused interactions.

## Financial domination / paypig guardrail

If a financial-domination fantasy is ever offered, it must remain:
- adult-only;
- explicit opt-in;
- fictional/consensual roleplay;
- bounded by visible prices and product scope;
- stoppable;
- non-coercive in real financial behavior.

Never:
- encourage debt;
- demand access to bank/credit accounts;
- request credentials;
- use real threats;
- punish refusal to spend;
- infer consent from other adult activity;
- use financial distress as targeting data.

The fantasy can be dominant; the real transaction must remain transparent and voluntary.

## Privacy and memory

Adult preference data can be sensitive in practice even when not legally classified the same way in every jurisdiction.

Default posture:
- collect less;
- avoid raw-transcript retention by default;
- separate identity, payment, consent and relationship memory;
- avoid sending intimate content/preferences into general analytics;
- store only what is needed for the product purpose;
- define retention/decay/deletion behavior;
- provide user-control paths before persistent personalization scales.

Persistent adult preference memory requires an explicit product/privacy review before activation.

## Payments

Before real payments:
1. identify the exact products to be sold;
2. identify the actual countries/jurisdictions served;
3. review payment-provider acceptable-use and restricted-business rules;
4. verify whether adult digital content, AI-generated adult content, subscriptions, tips, custom content and interactive experiences are supported;
5. confirm chargeback/refund requirements;
6. confirm descriptor/recurring billing disclosure;
7. document fallback behavior if a provider rejects the category.

Do not assume Stripe, PayPal or any other processor supports Mara's intended adult scope.

Provider integration requires separate founder authorization.

## Platform/channel separation

Do not distribute the same content level everywhere.

For every active channel maintain:
- allowed public content band;
- age restrictions;
- link/promotion policy;
- AI/synthetic media disclosure requirements;
- adult-content restrictions;
- account enforcement risk;
- permitted destination links.

Public social content should remain platform-safe and function primarily as acquisition/character building.

First-party adult experiences require their own compliance path.

## Custom content safeguards

Any future custom configurator must reject or route out requests that conflict with:
- age rules;
- real-person sexual impersonation rules;
- consent requirements;
- platform/provider restrictions;
- illegal or exploitative content categories.

The configurator should use bounded approved variables rather than unrestricted production promises.

## Analytics and experimentation

Adult monetization experiments must track not only revenue but:
- refund/dispute rate;
- voluntary stop/skip;
- negative reaction;
- cancellation behavior;
- consent funnel drop-off;
- support burden;
- provider/policy incidents.

A monetization test that increases conversion while materially increasing trust, safety or compliance risk is not a winner.

## Pre-activation gate

Before any real adult commercial launch, require a written review covering:
- jurisdictions;
- age assurance;
- AI disclosure;
- consent flow;
- privacy/data retention;
- product catalog;
- payment-provider policy;
- platform/channel policy;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- prohibited content handling;
- abuse/reporting;
- deletion/contact path;
- provider rights/licensing for generated media and voice.

Founder authorization for payments, providers, deployment and production activation remain separate approvals.
