# Mara Vera — 30-Day Launch Roadmap

## Days 1–3 — Constitution
Lock product thesis, character baseline, monetization rules, compliance baseline, analytics taxonomy and web IA.

Exit: one source of truth defining who Mara is, what is sold, what is prohibited and how success is measured.

## Days 4–7 — Character System
Build visual bible, prompt bible, tone of voice, behavior examples, content directions and consistency QA.

Exit: different assets still feel unmistakably like Mara Vera.

## Days 5–12 — Web MVP
Build mobile-first Home, Meet Mara, Premium, legal surfaces, AI disclosure, age gate, analytics events, responsive behavior, basic SEO/performance and configurable premium handoff.

No real provider activation or payments without separate authorization.

Exit: local Web MVP that can be reviewed and tested.

## Days 8–14 — Revenue + Content
Define price hypotheses, offer catalog, PPV structure, personalized experience catalog and launch inventory.

Inventory target:
- 30–50 stills;
- 10–15 vertical videos;
- 15–20 stories;
- 10 premium assets;
- 5–10 paid offer assets.

## Days 15–21 — Soft Launch
Open social channels, owned web and authorized premium surface. Track the full conversion chain.

Do not scale paid acquisition yet.

## Days 22–30 — Optimization
Identify content/personality/offer combinations that create payers and repeat spenders rather than vanity engagement.

## Day-30 gate

### GREEN
Evidence of paying demand plus repeat spend. Indicative target: 20+ buyers and 10+ repeat spenders, with observable organic conversion signal.

### YELLOW
Traffic exists but monetization/retention is weak. Iterate offer, character expression, funnel and pricing.

### RED
No meaningful demand after multiple valid tests. Do not build expensive proprietary infrastructure; pivot or stop.

## Build order after GREEN
1. Improve funnel.
2. Improve content operations.
3. Add consent-based relationship memory.
4. Automate repetitive operations.
5. Test scalable acquisition.
6. Only then evaluate more characters.
