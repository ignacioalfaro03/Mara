# Mara Vera — Product Thesis

## Core thesis

Mara Vera is a premium synthetic character with a persistent identity, designed to convert attention into recurring paid digital relationships.

The product is not image generation, a generic chatbot, or deception about human identity. The product is the relationship layer around a recognizable character IP.

## Commercial loop

**Social discovery → first-party web → premium intent → first payment → second payment → repeat spend → retained relationship**

The primary early milestone is not first purchase. It is **first payment → second payment**.

## Permanent principles

1. Make money: every meaningful feature must tie to acquisition, conversion, ARPPU, retention, margin or defensibility.
2. Mara is always disclosed as AI.
3. Adult experiences are adults-only.
4. Consent before intensity.
5. Monetize desire, not vulnerability.
6. Compliance by design.
7. Do not overbuild before revenue validation.
8. Mara Vera is original synthetic IP, not an imitation of a real person.
9. Data collection must be purposeful and minimized.
10. GitHub is the source of truth for product and architecture decisions.

## What Mara Vera is

- A character IP.
- A social-native content product.
- A conversion funnel.
- A premium interaction product.
- A future consent-based relationship memory product.
- A business measured on revenue and retention, not vanity metrics.

## What Mara Vera is not

- A fake human persona.
- A generic AI companion.
- A porn bot.
- A social account without a monetization system.
- A dark-pattern product designed around coercion or financial distress.
- A reason to build expensive proprietary infrastructure before demand exists.

## Strategic sequencing

1. One character.
2. One owned web funnel.
3. Real traffic.
4. Real buyers.
5. Repeat spend.
6. Relationship Engine.
7. Automation.
8. Scalable acquisition.
9. Only then consider additional characters or a broader Character Commerce platform.
