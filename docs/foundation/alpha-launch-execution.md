# Mara Vera — Public Alpha Launch Execution

## Status

Founder decision — 2026-09-02:

**Launch without a mandatory pre-launch tester gate.**

This replaces the previous assumption that 5–8 private testers were required before any public alpha exposure.

It does **not** remove safety, compliance, truthful disclosure, payment-readiness or merge/deploy controls.

## Launch posture

The public alpha is deliberately:

- free;
- adult-only;
- first-party;
- text-first unless canonical voice is configured;
- lightweight;
- local-state only for the public experience;
- no payment provider;
- no real checkout;
- no real Capricho contributions;
- no external adult-media integration;
- no fake scarcity;
- no claim of production-complete Relationship Memory.

Principle:

> **LAUNCH SMALL. LEARN FROM REAL USE. DO NOT WAIT FOR FEATURE COMPLETENESS.**

## What the alpha must prove

The alpha does not need to prove full monetization yet.

It needs to show whether a real visitor experiences:

1. Mara as a specific character rather than a generic assistant;
2. Mara-led interaction rather than configuration;
3. a visible prediction / interpretation loop;
4. ordinary-life continuity;
5. a reason to come back;
6. low-friction trust around AI disclosure and local memory;
7. enough curiosity that adding canonical visual/voice assets is worth doing.

Primary behavioral signal:

> **DO PEOPLE VOLUNTARILY COME BACK TO MARA?**

## Public alpha product

The public alpha should expose one coherent product surface:

`Home → Meet Mara → /experience → return`

Internal labs remain diagnostic tools and should not become public navigation.

The public alpha experience must not expose:

- P0 jargon;
- DEV controls;
- mock payment as real payment;
- prototype scarcity as real availability;
- browser TTS represented as canonical Mara voice;
- sensitive preference analytics;
- developer scorecards.

## Presence strategy

### Visual

The public product may launch with a neutral branded fallback while the canonical visual asset is being finalized.

Once a canonical image is approved, the web should inject it through the existing public visual slot rather than redesigning the funnel.

### Voice

Text-first is preferable to low-quality synthetic voice presented as canonical Mara.

Canonical voice can be introduced when it reaches the required naturalness and character consistency.

Principle:

> **NO VOICE IS BETTER THAN THE WRONG MARA VOICE.**

## Zero-OPEX launch rule

Until traction exists:

- use existing/free hosting where compatible;
- no paid acquisition;
- no recurring analytics SaaS unless materially necessary;
- no paid realtime voice infrastructure;
- no paid memory infrastructure;
- no vector DB / embeddings merely for architecture completeness;
- no payment processor activation before the payment-readiness gate.

## Initial distribution sequence

1. Make the public alpha reachable.
2. Replace obvious prototype language with public product language.
3. Add canonical Mara visual when available.
4. Add canonical voice only when quality is good enough.
5. Start public-safe organic content around Mara's character, taste, life, confidence and curiosity.
6. Route interested adults to the first-party web.
7. Observe voluntary return, session completion and qualitative reactions.
8. Improve the strongest Mara-specific lever.
9. Only after repeat interest appears, resume WTP / first-payment preparation.

## Organic acquisition thesis

Public acquisition should sell the **character and fantasy of knowing Mara**, not explicit D02 or adult mechanics on channels where that framing is unsuitable.

Useful public themes:

- Mara knows what she wants;
- selective confidence;
- lifestyle texture;
- ordinary life with sharp personality;
- playful prediction;
- "I have a theory about you";
- moments that make the viewer curious about how Mara would read them;
- world progression only when truthful and canonical.

The first-party web is where adult eligibility, consent and deeper desire discovery belong.

## What stays deferred

Even after public alpha launch:

- real payments;
- provider onboarding;
- real Capricho contributions;
- production adult content pipelines;
- external adult-media recommendation;
- persistent sensitive memory infrastructure;
- automated proactive messaging;
- paid acquisition;
- membership;
- public user/community features;
- spend/status leaderboards;
- financial-control automation.

## Decision cadence

The alpha should be improved from observed behavior, not from endless speculative architecture.

Every meaningful post-launch change should answer one of these:

- Did more people enter Mara?
- Did more people finish the first experience?
- Did more people return?
- Did Mara feel more specific/coherent?
- Did trust improve?
- Did a new asset materially increase desire/return?

If not, it is not launch-critical.

## Permanent launch principles

> **REAL USERS BEFORE PERFECT INFRASTRUCTURE.**

> **PUBLIC ALPHA ≠ COMMERCIAL LAUNCH.**

> **FREE FIRST. PAYMENT ONLY AFTER VALUE IS FELT.**

> **TEXT-FIRST IS VALID. BAD VOICE IS NOT.**

> **ONE MARA. ONE PUBLIC EXPERIENCE. MANY INTERNAL EXPERIMENTS.**

> **DO NOT LET LAB COMPLEXITY LEAK INTO THE PRODUCT.**

> **FETISH-LED ACQUISITION. MARA-LED RETENTION.**

> **LIFE FIRST. COMMERCE SOMETIMES ATTACHES.**
