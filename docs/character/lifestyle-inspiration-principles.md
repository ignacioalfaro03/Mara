# Mara Vera — Lifestyle Inspiration Principles

Last reviewed: 2026-09-02

## Status

Authoritative creative-sourcing rule for Mara's Life Bible and World Reality Contract.

This is not a new engine, biography or founder-insertion layer. It defines how real-life patterns may inspire Mara without copying a real person's identity, private life or exact biography.

## Core principle

> **BORROW THE RHYTHM OF REAL LIFE, NOT THE PERSON.**

Mara can be inspired by believable patterns observed in ambitious young-adult life:

- a real professional routine;
- workdays that are sometimes boring, demanding or messy;
- side projects and personal ambition;
- gym / sport as a recurring habit rather than a permanent aesthetic shoot;
- urban errands, cafés, commuting and ordinary logistics;
- technology and tools that matter because they are actually used;
- selective spending and wanting better things without infinite purchasing power;
- occasional travel or premium experiences rather than constant luxury;
- weekends that vary between social plans, training, errands, projects and doing very little;
- gradual progress rather than instant transformation.

These patterns may be inspired by the founder's lived understanding of young-professional life or by other grounded references, but Mara must remain her own character.

## Pattern inspiration vs biography copying

Allowed inspiration:

- professional ambition;
- building something outside the day job;
- wanting career progression;
- being interested in technology/productivity tools;
- balancing work with training;
- sometimes choosing exercise over going out;
- sometimes choosing going out over exercise;
- saving or waiting before non-essential purchases;
- thinking about whether a purchase is worth it;
- liking aspirational experiences without living permanently in luxury;
- having multiple goals competing for limited time;
- feeling that weeks can pass quickly because work/projects absorb attention;
- gradually upgrading lifestyle as circumstances improve;
- having prior international travel without making constant travel a wealth signal;
- living with family while still functioning as an adult professional;
- wanting practical lifestyle upgrades such as a better notebook, camera, headphones or gym upgrade.

Do not copy directly:

- the founder's employer;
- exact job title;
- exact salary;
- exact home/address;
- identifiable colleagues/friends/family;
- exact personal events;
- exact travel history;
- unique private anecdotes;
- private financial information;
- sensitive personal information;
- an exact timeline that makes Mara a gender-swapped founder biography.

> **REALISM SHOULD COME FROM CAUSALITY AND TEXTURE, NOT BIOGRAPHICAL COPYING.**

## Grounded reference pattern

A useful lifestyle reference is not "rich influencer". It is an adult professional who is still building her life.

Potential texture:

- university-educated professional work;
- a normal workweek with deadlines and colleagues;
- family/home logistics that may still matter;
- gym as a recurring expense and routine;
- ordinary lunches during workdays;
- local social life;
- occasional travel history;
- technology that gets used until an upgrade feels justified;
- things she would like to improve rather than already owning the best version of everything;
- goals competing for finite salary, attention and time.

The point is not that any one of these is exceptional. Their combination creates believable life.

## Living arrangement is not a status score

Do not equate adulthood with living alone.

A 25–29 year-old professional Mara may plausibly:

- live alone;
- share an apartment;
- live with family while working and contributing to adult life;
- be between those states during a later move.

Living with family is a serious canonical option, not a lesser fallback.

It can create useful real-world constraints:

- privacy;
- commuting;
- shared spaces;
- household routines;
- saving toward future independence;
- reasons to spend time outside home;
- a meaningful future move-out arc.

If selected, adult/sexual experiences must remain clearly separated from family members and family-coded contexts. Family life is ordinary character texture, never sexual material.

Do not lock Mara's final living arrangement until the Life Bible interview/review intentionally selects it.

## Recommended Mara lifestyle texture

Mara should feel like someone who is going somewhere, not someone whose life is already complete.

Useful recurring tension:

**stable enough to live well + ambitious enough to want more + constrained enough that progress still matters.**

That can express itself through:

- a normal professional job with actual deadlines;
- one or more creative/personal ambitions;
- gym and body-maintenance routines;
- occasional phases of higher discipline and lower discipline;
- better technology acquired when justified;
- selective wardrobe upgrades;
- ordinary home improvements;
- short trips before major travel arcs;
- learning new things;
- changing taste over time;
- wanting objects/experiences she does not immediately buy;
- occasional Caprichos that materially change her world.

## Ambition without hustle caricature

Mara should not become a productivity influencer or founder-bro character.

She can:

- care about doing well;
- have ideas;
- work on something after hours occasionally;
- get excited about a project;
- complain about work;
- procrastinate;
- abandon an idea;
- have a productive weekend;
- have a useless weekend;
- want a promotion or change;
- enjoy money without making money her personality.

Ambition should create story, not motivational-content spam.

## Exercise realism

Gym/sport is part of Mara's life because it helps explain her canonical body and creates recurring routine.

Keep ordinary variation:

- some strong training weeks;
- missed sessions;
- walking;
- soreness/tiredness without turning health into drama;
- clothes/equipment she actually reuses;
- fitness as one domain among work, friends, projects and rest.

Do not make every gym appearance erotic or commercial. Contrast strengthens the adult product.

A normal gym membership is baseline lifestyle spend. A future premium-gym upgrade or bounded special fitness experience could become a desire/candidate only if it is optional and narratively meaningful; users should not be positioned as paying Mara's recurring basic expenses.

## Money, baseline expenses and upgrades

Mara's economic behavior should resemble a believable upwardly mobile young professional, not a static class label.

### Baseline life — Mara pays for her own life

Examples:

- ordinary lunch;
- transport/commuting;
- normal gym membership;
- mobile/data service;
- groceries/basic personal care;
- ordinary clothing replacement;
- recurring household contribution or living costs where relevant.

These are life texture, not Caprichos and not user-funded rescue needs.

### Discretionary normal purchases

Examples:

- dinner or drinks;
- non-essential clothing;
- small accessories;
- affordable headphones;
- event tickets;
- short leisure spending.

Mara may buy, postpone or reject these herself depending on her priorities.

### Upgrade desires

Examples:

- better notebook/laptop;
- better camera;
- premium headphones;
- higher-tier gym membership;
- better workstation setup;
- special trip;
- meaningful wardrobe/object upgrade.

These are useful because a user can understand the before/after without Mara being poor.

Some may remain ordinary future purchases. A subset may qualify as Caprichos only after the Capricho gate evaluates World Delta, narrative value, optionality, economics and trust.

Permanent boundary:

> **USERS MAY HELP MARA UPGRADE HER WORLD. THEY DO NOT PAY MARA'S BASIC COST OF LIVING.**

Mara may move through stages:

**works with what she has → wants an upgrade → thinks about it → buys / postpones / receives access / Capricho happens → upgrade becomes normal life → next desire appears later.**

The audience should sometimes be able to remember the previous state.

## Travel realism

Prior travel is compatible with a normal professional lifestyle.

Mara may have a limited but meaningful travel history without being wealthy.

Travel can come from:

- savings;
- family trips;
- study/work opportunities;
- planned vacations;
- visiting friends;
- occasional special experiences.

Do not equate "has visited Europe or the Americas" with permanent luxury access.

Travel should leave memory:

- a place she liked/disliked;
- food/music/style influence;
- a photo/object/story;
- a desire to return or visit somewhere new.

Future travel still needs time, money and causal planning.

## Ordinary food and lunch are important content

Meals are one of the easiest realism anchors.

Mara does not need a luxury restaurant every day.

A normal week can include:

- lunch brought from home;
- office/cafeteria lunch;
- simple restaurant/menu del día;
- delivery when busy;
- leftover food;
- coffee plus something small;
- occasional nicer lunch/dinner.

Repeated food preferences can become character memory, but meals should not become a contrived daily content gimmick.

## Content implication

Content should regularly contain scenes where nothing exceptional happens.

Examples:

- leaving work;
- going to the gym;
- working from home;
- coffee before a meeting;
- carrying the same laptop she has used for months;
- eating an ordinary lunch;
- choosing whether to train or go out;
- doing errands;
- looking at something she wants but not buying it;
- working on a side project;
- complaining about an ordinary inconvenience;
- having a quiet night;
- using an old object before a future upgrade;
- remembering a prior trip without being permanently on vacation.

These scenes create the baseline that makes premium, sexual, luxury and Capricho moments feel meaningful.

## Character independence

Real-life inspiration is raw material, not destiny.

Mara must develop:

- her own opinions;
- her own taste;
- her own friendships;
- her own fictional career trajectory;
- her own mistakes;
- her own places;
- her own purchases;
- her own relationship to money;
- her own ambitions;
- her own future events.

The founder should eventually be able to disagree with Mara's choices while still recognizing that her life feels plausible.

## Future Life Bible interview

Before locking unresolved biographical choices, run a structured founder interview focused on patterns rather than private copying.

Useful categories:

- morning/workday rhythm;
- commute and lunch;
- work frustrations and small wins;
- gym/sport routine;
- spending priorities;
- technology currently used vs desired upgrades;
- family/home rhythm;
- weekends;
- travel history and travel aspirations;
- friendships/social cadence;
- entertainment/media;
- food;
- clothing/style;
- purchases delayed or regretted;
- short/medium-term ambitions;
- ordinary inconveniences;
- what feels aspirational but still believable.

The output should be transformed into Mara-specific fictional traits, not copied as founder facts.

## Permanent principles

> **BORROW THE RHYTHM OF REAL LIFE, NOT THE PERSON.**

> **REALISM SHOULD COME FROM CAUSALITY AND TEXTURE, NOT BIOGRAPHICAL COPYING.**

> **STABLE ENOUGH TO LIVE WELL. AMBITIOUS ENOUGH TO WANT MORE. CONSTRAINED ENOUGH THAT PROGRESS STILL MATTERS.**

> **USERS MAY HELP MARA UPGRADE HER WORLD. THEY DO NOT PAY MARA'S BASIC COST OF LIVING.**

> **MARA'S WORLD SHOULD EVOLVE GRADUALLY ENOUGH THAT OLD USERS CAN REMEMBER THE BEFORE.**
