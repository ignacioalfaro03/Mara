# Mara Vera — Visual Bible v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines Mara Vera's canonical visual identity.

The objective is not to produce isolated attractive images. The objective is to build a recognizable, monetizable and scalable visual IP that can support social distribution, conversion, premium experiences and future product expansion.

The primary visual KPI is **identity consistency across different contexts**.

Mara Vera must remain recognizably the same adult synthetic woman across lifestyle, fitness, night, editorial and premium content.

---

## 1. Visual positioning

Mara Vera should read as:

> **an adult Latina woman, premium, curvy/fit, confident, slightly dominant, highly attractive, social-native and editorially consistent.**

She should combine:
- contemporary sensuality;
- believable social-media naturalism;
- premium art direction;
- credible Latina beauty;
- a strong, repeatable identity.

She must not feel like:
- a copy of an identifiable real influencer;
- an exaggerated plastic model;
- a generic porn persona;
- obvious CGI;
- a hyper-perfect generic AI girl;
- a minor-coded or adolescent character;
- low-quality or cheap adult-content branding.

The product should borrow category-level visual codes and body archetypes only. It must not reproduce the face, identity or distinctive likeness of any identifiable real person.

---

## 2. Canonical body archetype

Mara's body direction is **curvy / fit Latina** with commercially strong but believable proportions.

Canonical characteristics:
- clearly defined waist;
- wide hips;
- prominent glutes;
- shapely legs with visible volume;
- firm / relatively flat abdomen;
- proportionate bust;
- feminine and harmonious silhouette;
- realistic anatomy;
- attractive proportions that work in ordinary social content as well as premium content.

Internal shorthand:

> **curvy fit Latina, defined waist, wide hips, prominent glutes, shapely legs, firm abdomen, proportionate bust, realistic and sensual proportions.**

Avoid:
- caricatured BBL proportions;
- anatomically implausible waist-to-hip ratios;
- extreme musculature;
- editorial thinness as the default body language;
- obvious body-generation artifacts;
- any body treatment that makes the character feel synthetic rather than desirable and plausible.

The body archetype is a category benchmark, not a biometric copy of any real person.

- recognizable resemblance to a real person.

## 17. Production QA scorecard

An asset is approved only if it passes the following checks:

1. Is Mara unambiguously adult?
2. Is this unmistakably the same Mara as previous approved assets?
3. Does the face remain original and stable?
4. Does the canonical curvy/fit silhouette remain consistent?
5. Are waist, hips, glutes, legs, bust and abdomen anatomically plausible?
6. Does skin and anatomy look human rather than CGI-like?
7. Do hands, teeth, hair, clothing and background objects pass basic realism checks?
8. Does the wardrobe belong to Mara's established visual language?
9. Does the image feel social-native rather than like an AI-generation demo?
10. Does the image carry Mara's confident / selective / slightly dominant energy?
11. Is any identifiable real person being imitated too closely? If yes, reject.
12. Is the asset appropriate for its intended platform and audience?
13. Could this image coexist coherently beside 20 other approved Mara assets?
14. Does it improve brand, acquisition, conversion or character recognition?

A visually striking asset that fails identity consistency or realism is not an approved Mara asset.
