# Mara Vera — Visual Bible v2.0

## Status

Authoritative launch visual identity.

Founder decision on 2026-09-02 supersedes the previous Latina / dark-hair direction.

Mara Vera is now canonically defined by the approved reference generation registered in `docs/character/canonical-visual-reference.md`.

The objective is not to generate isolated attractive women. The objective is to preserve **one recognizable adult synthetic woman** across lifestyle, social, intimate, premium and future adult content.

Primary KPI:

> **THAT IS MARA.**

Not:

> "another AI girl."

---

## 1. Canonical positioning

Mara Vera should read as:

> **an adult fair-skinned woman, curvy but not overweight, physically imposing in a feminine way, premium, highly attractive, calm, selective and unmistakably dominant.**

She is not visually coded as Latina.

Her attraction should come from the combination of:
- strong feminine proportions;
- believable softness and human anatomy;
- adult facial maturity;
- self-possession;
- direct gaze;
- premium styling;
- controlled sensuality;
- subtle intimidation / authority.

She must not feel like:
- a generic AI glamour model;
- an influencer clone;
- a plastic BBL caricature;
- an ultra-thin fashion model;
- an adolescent-coded "girl next door";
- a permanently smiling friendly assistant;
- obvious CGI;
- a porn stereotype with no personality.

---

## 2. Canonical body archetype

Mara's launch body is **curvy, feminine, strong and naturally soft**.

Core characteristics:
- clearly adult body;
- defined but believable waist;
- broad/full hips;
- prominent but anatomically plausible glutes;
- visibly thick thighs;
- legs with substantial volume;
- medium-to-large proportionate bust;
- relatively flat abdomen while standing, but not fitness-stage lean;
- natural softness around abdomen, hips and thighs;
- realistic skin compression and folds when posture creates them;
- no expectation of perfect symmetry or zero body fat.

Internal shorthand:

> **curvy dominant hourglass, thick thighs, full hips, strong feminine lower body, substantial bust, visible waist, natural softness, not fat, not ultra-lean, no plastic BBL look.**

The target is desirable because it looks human and physically present, not because every anatomical detail is mathematically perfect.

Avoid:
- cartoon BBL proportions;
- impossible waist-to-hip ratios;
- extremely narrow thighs;
- bodybuilder musculature;
- perfectly airbrushed abdomen;
- visible ribcage / ultra-thin look;
- obesity as the launch archetype;
- body generation artifacts.

## 3. Canonical face

Mara's face is original synthetic IP and must remain stable.

Desired facial signal:
- clearly adult, target apparent age 25–29;
- fair/light complexion;
- European / non-Latina visual read;
- medium-to-strong cheekbone definition;
- defined jawline without becoming harsh;
- structured brows;
- direct, confident eyes;
- defined lips;
- proportionate nose;
- slightly narrowed or evaluative gaze when context supports it;
- restrained smile or no smile by default;
- facial asymmetry and skin detail sufficient to feel human.

The face must communicate:
- authority;
- self-possession;
- intelligence;
- attraction;
- selectivity;
- control;
- "I decide" energy.

Permanent facial principle:

> **Mara should look more like the person judging the room than the person asking whether the room likes her.**

Avoid:
- "sweet innocent girl" baseline;
- permanently wide-eyed friendliness;
- adolescent facial cues;
- hyperfilled lips / cosmetic-surgery caricature;
- generic Instagram-face symmetry;
- a recognizable resemblance to a real person.

---

## 4. Hair

Canonical launch hair family:
- blonde to dark-blonde / honey-blonde;
- darker natural roots are acceptable and desirable for realism;
- medium-long to long;
- straight, lightly wavy or casually tied;
- healthy but not salon-perfect in every frame.

Hair may vary by situation, but color family and overall identity must remain stable.

---

## 5. Skin and realism

Target:
- fair/light skin;
- realistic pores and texture;
- natural tonal variation;
- subtle freckles / marks / small imperfections are desirable where coherent;
- realistic compression and folds;
- no porcelain/plastic finish.

The goal is not "flawless skin".

The goal is **convincing human presence**.

---

## 6. Dominance system

Dominance is a visual identity layer, not a costume.

Mara should communicate dominance through:
- gaze;
- posture;
- head position;
- stillness;
- deliberate facial expression;
- controlled body language;
- selective attention;
- confidence in fitted clothing;
- lack of approval-seeking.

Preferred signals:
- chin neutral or slightly elevated;
- direct gaze;
- relaxed shoulders;
- hands on hips or composed arms when appropriate;
- minimal smile;
- half-smile only when it reads as knowing rather than eager;
- standing as if she owns the space.

Avoid:
- constant scowling;
- theatrical dominatrix cosplay as the default;
- exaggerated aggression;
- submissive body language;
- overly cute / eager expressions;
- smiling merely to appear approachable.

Permanent rule:

> **DOMINANCE IS COMPOSURE BEFORE IT IS COSTUME.**
