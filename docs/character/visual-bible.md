# Mara Vera — Visual Bible v0.1

## Objective

Mara Vera must be recognizable across many assets without looking synthetic, generic or over-polished.

The primary visual KPI is **identity consistency across different contexts**.

## Canonical elements to lock during character production

- face shape and proportions;
- eye color/shape;
- nose and lip proportions;
- skin tone and texture;
- hair color, length and recurring styling;
- body proportions;
- approximate height;
- recurring manicure;
- tattoos/piercings only if deliberately chosen;
- signature jewelry/accessories;
- wardrobe language;
- recurring environments;
- camera/lighting language.

## Aesthetic direction

Mara should feel:
- premium;
- editorial;
- contemporary;
- clearly adult;
- attractive;
- slightly mysterious;
- aspirational;
- socially native rather than CGI-native.

Avoid:
- hyper-perfect plastic skin;
- impossible anatomy;
- inconsistent facial identity;
- obvious AI artifacts;
- generic luxury clichés;
- porn-bot visual language;
- minor-coded styling;
- direct resemblance to identifiable real people.

## Content contexts

Initial recurring contexts:
- home;
- café;
- gym/fitness;
- street/fashion;
- night/evening;
- selfie/close-up;
- quiet editorial portraits;
- travel/lifestyle only when continuity can be maintained.

## Production QA

Before publishing an asset, verify:
1. Is this unmistakably the same Mara?
2. Does she look clearly adult?
3. Does anatomy pass basic realism checks?
4. Do hands/teeth/background objects look credible?
5. Does the wardrobe fit Mara's established style?
6. Does the asset feel social-native, not like a demo of image generation?
7. Is any real person being imitated too closely?
8. Is the content appropriate for the intended platform?

## Experimentation rule

Hair styling, wardrobe, lighting and environments can vary. Core facial identity and overall character signal should not drift during early launch experiments.
