## TYPE A — VIRTUAL CREATOR

- voice;
- curated photos;
- AI-assisted identity;
- controlled visibility.

A creator who is comfortable showing their real identity and wants better monetization infrastructure.

Real person + avatar + controlled identity.

A person or business capable of executing an experience.

Examples:

- pub;
- nightclub;
- hotel;
- restaurant;
- event producer;
- studio;
- photographer;
- local operator;
- venue;
- experience facilitator;
- activity provider.

## TYPE F — CREATOR HOST

A creator who also executes or manages their own experiences.

# 3. FANVUE — WHAT MARA SHOULD LEARN

Fanvue demonstrates that creators want one platform where they can monetize several forms of attention.

Important mechanics to absorb:

## A. MONETIZATION SHOULD BE GRANULAR

Creators should be able to decide whether something is:

- free;
- subscriber-only;
- paid individually;
- paid through credits;
- VIP-only;
- unlocked after another purchase.

Potential monetizable objects:

- text message;
- voice note;
- photo;
- video;
- personalized response;
- request;
- private interaction;
- scheduled interaction;
- digital experience;
- physical experience.

Mara should NOT impose one monetization model.

The creator should configure their own economy.

## B. AI SHOULD REDUCE CREATOR OPERATIONS

AI is not the product.

AI is infrastructure.

AI should help creators:

- answer faster;
- classify users;
- identify high-value fans;
- summarize conversations;
- remember preferences;
- recommend what to offer;
- detect purchase intent;
- suggest pricing;
- automate repetitive interactions;
- identify upsell opportunities.

IMPORTANT:

Avoid building an expensive always-on chatbot as the core proposition.

AI must reduce operational burden, not create operational burden.

## C. CREATOR ECONOMY DASHBOARD

Creators need to immediately understand:

- revenue;
- conversion;
- active fans;
- high-value users;
- best-selling products;
- requests;
- pending interactions;
- opportunities;
- demand signals.

Translate this into Mara.
