# MARA — FIRST 9 INSTAGRAM POSTS ARTBOARD V1

Status: conceptual artboard for validation.  
Date: 2026-09-12.  
Purpose: turn the visual identity into a real launch sequence without generic AI/SaaS content.

## Grid objective

The first nine posts must establish three things before product explanation:

1. Mara understands creator businesses.
2. Mara sees revenue leaks creators do not clearly see.
3. Mara owns the territory between first purchase and second purchase.

The grid should feel editorial, documentary and commercially literate.

Do not optimize the 3x3 as a decorative checkerboard. Each post must work independently.

## Post 01 — MANIFESTO

### Feed headline

# Followers are not customers.

### Format

Static typography-led post.

### Visual

- Paper background `#F2EEE7`.
- Large Source Serif 4 headline in Ink.
- Small operational line in Inter:
  `ATTENTION IS NOT CUSTOMER MEMORY.`
- One thin Mara Redline under `customers`.
- Small Mara signature bottom-right.

### Caption argument

Creators can know reach, views and followers while still not knowing:

- who bought;
- what they bought;
- what they may need next;
- who is worth contacting;
- who disappeared after purchase.

Close with:

`Your audience lives on social. Your customer relationships should not.`

### CTA

`Do you know exactly who your last 20 buyers were?`

### Strategic job

Define the category problem.

## Post 03 — CATEGORY QUESTION

### Feed headline

# What happens after someone buys from a creator?

### Format

6-slide editorial carousel.

### Visual language

Mostly neutral typography, one original photo of creator operations, one transaction screenshot, restrained red annotations.

# 3x3 visual rhythm

Conceptual order:

| 01 | 02 | 03 |
|---|---|---|
| Manifesto typography | Documentary revenue leak | Editorial explainer |

| 04 | 05 | 06 |
|---|---|---|
| `01 → 02` hero | Real creator photography | DM evidence |

| 07 | 08 | 09 |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical autopsy | Counter-positioning typography | Mara product evidence |

This produces visual tension without checkerboard gimmicks.

# Production hierarchy

Do not produce all nine immediately just to populate a grid.

Recommended real sequence:

1. Produce Posts 01, 03, 04 and 08 from designed editorial assets.
2. Acquire genuine creator/workflow evidence for 02, 05, 06 and 07.
3. Build Post 09 only when product evidence is credible enough.
4. Replace any conceptual scenario with real proof as quickly as possible.

# Art direction constraints

Every post must use at least two Mara-recognizable devices, but never all of them at once.

Examples:

- Redline + serif hierarchy;
- `01 → 02` + paper palette;
- Evidence Stamp + documentary image;
- Revenue Number + Margin Note.

Do not overload frames with branding devices.

# Mobile crop discipline

Before approval:

- inspect at actual phone width;
- confirm headline reads in under ~1 second;
- confirm no important text is dependent on tiny type;
- confirm feed crop still retains the core thought;
- confirm carousel cover is understandable without caption.

# Caption discipline

No generic motivational filler.

Every caption must contain at least one of:

- real observation;
- mechanism;
- commercial consequence;
- evidence;
- decision rule.

# Success signal

The first nine posts succeed if qualified creators begin to say things like:

- `this happens to me`;
- `I literally track this in notes`;
- `I never follow up after the first purchase`;
- `can you look at my setup?`;
- `how would Mara know this?`.

Likes alone do not validate the system.
