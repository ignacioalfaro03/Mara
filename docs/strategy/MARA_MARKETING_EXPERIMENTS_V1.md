# MARA — MARKETING EXPERIMENTS V1

Status: execution backlog  
Effective: 2026-09-12  
Authority: subordinate to the Mara foundational thesis and GTM v1.

## Objective

Find a repeatable path to acquire qualified creators before spending meaningfully on paid acquisition.

Every experiment must answer one of four questions:

1. Can we get the right creators to respond?
2. Can we get them to start a pilot?
3. Can Mara produce a measurable commercial win?
4. Can that win create retention/referrals?

## Experiment 01 — Personalized creator outbound

Hypothesis:

> A creator who already sells will respond to a personalized message about lost repeat revenue more often than to a generic software pitch.

Sample:

- 30 creators;
- 3 vertical clusters;
- 10 per cluster.

Message variants:

A. Revenue leakage  
"Vi que vendes X. ¿Cómo haces hoy para saber quién de los que ya te compraron podría volver a comprarte?"

B. Customer ownership  
"Tienes seguidores en Instagram, pero ¿dónde viven hoy tus clientes reales y su historial?"

C. Next best action  
"Estoy probando una herramienta que intenta decirle a un creador a quién conviene venderle qué esta semana."

Primary metric:

- meaningful reply rate.

Secondary:

- discovery call rate;
- qualified pilot rate.

Pass signal:

- one message clearly outperforms the others and produces qualified conversations.

## Experiment 02 — Revenue teardown offer

Offer:

> "Te hago gratis un mapa de dónde podrías estar perdiendo recompra en tu negocio de creador."

Deliverable:

- one-page commercial teardown;
- current funnel;
- likely repeat-sale leakage;
- 3 specific opportunities;
- no obligation.

Goal:

Use a useful artifact to earn trust before asking for pilot access.

Metric:

- teardown-to-pilot conversion.

## Experiment 03 — 7-day Revenue Sprint

Hypothesis:

> Creators value Mara more when it is sold as a revenue experiment than as software.

Pitch:

> "Durante 7 días probamos si Mara puede detectar una oportunidad real que hoy se te está escapando. Si no encontramos nada útil, igual aprendemos y no te amarras a nada."

Metrics:

- acceptance rate;
- action rate;
- incremental sale rate;
- willingness to continue.

## Experiment 04 — Founder content: creator revenue operations

Channels:

- Instagram;
- LinkedIn;
- TikTok if founder format proves natural.

Content pillars:

1. revenue leaks;
2. creator CRM;
3. repeat purchase;
4. customer ownership;
5. monetization teardown;
6. pilot lessons;
7. product proof.

CTA:

> "Si ya vendes algo a tu audiencia y quieres que revisemos dónde puede haber recompra perdida, háblame."

Metrics:

- qualified inbound conversations;
- profile-to-DM conversion;
- inbound-to-pilot conversion.

Do not optimize for views alone.

## Experiment 05 — Public teardown content

Select public creator businesses where commercial flows are visible.

Create respectful teardown:

- what they sell;
- buyer journey;
- likely repeat-purchase opportunity;
- what a Revenue OS could improve.

Rules:

- use only public information;
- do not imply access to private data;
- do not criticize the creator personally;
- frame as commercial analysis, not exposure.

Goal:

Demonstrate Mara thinking before product scale.

## Experiment 06 — Referral after win

Trigger:

A creator reports a concrete valuable result.

Ask:

> "¿Conoces a 1 o 2 creadores que ya vendan y tengan este mismo problema de seguimiento/recompra?"

Metric:

- referral per successful creator;
- referred creator activation rate.

Pass signal:

- referrals become a meaningful fraction of qualified pipeline.

## Experiment 07 — Niche concentration

After first five pilots, rank verticals by:

- reply rate;
- pilot conversion;
- revenue opportunity density;
- time to first value;
- repeat purchase potential;
- willingness to pay;
- referral likelihood.

If one vertical materially wins, focus the next 20 creator prospects there.

Do not remain horizontally broad for ideological reasons.

## Experiment 08 — Pricing interview

Do not ask only "how much would you pay?"

Test structures:

A. success-based take rate;  
B. monthly SaaS;  
C. hybrid lower subscription + lower take rate.

Ask creators to compare concrete economics against revenue generated.

Canonical starting hypothesis remains low/zero fixed cost with an indicative 10–15% take-rate range for validation, subject to real economics.

Metric:

- preferred model;
- price resistance;
- expected ROI threshold.

### B — Next revenue opportunity

### C — Customer ownership
"Your followers live on social. Your customers live in Mara."

"Apply for a 7-day Revenue Sprint"

## Experiment 10 — Opportunity screenshot/demo

Build marketing around the differentiated moment:

> "Mara found 7 customers likely to buy again this week."

Show:

- evidence;
- recommended action;
- expected value hypothesis;
- button/action.

Goal:

Make the Revenue OS category understandable in under 30 seconds.

## 30-day founder marketing operating board

### Pipeline target

- 50 researched creators;
- 30 personalized outbound messages;
- 10 meaningful conversations;
- 5 pilots;
- 3 creators acting on an opportunity;
- 1 measurable Mara-originated revenue event;
- 1 referral.

### Weekly rhythm

Monday — prospect research + pipeline.  
Tuesday — outbound.  
Wednesday — discovery + demos.  
Thursday — pilot operations + creator follow-up.  
Friday — evidence review + content from learnings.

## Marketing evidence hierarchy

Strongest:

1. incremental transaction tied to Mara action;
2. repeat purchase tied to Mara recommendation;
3. creator continues using Mara;
4. creator refers peer;
5. creator says they would pay;
6. creator completes pilot;
7. creator books call;
8. creator replies;
9. creator views content.

Always optimize upward in this hierarchy.

## Anti-patterns

Do not:

- buy ads before ICP/message fit;
- chase follower growth for Mara as the primary KPI;
- create generic AI content at volume;
- market features that are not usable;
- promise revenue guarantees;
- claim intelligence not supported by data;
- invent customer logos, quotes or case studies;
- over-focus on branding before conversion evidence;
- call every creator a lead if they cannot monetize.

## Decision after 30 days

At the end of the first cycle, decide one of:

### DOUBLE DOWN
A clear segment responds, activates and sees value.

### REFINE
Problem is real but message/onboarding/opportunity quality is weak.

### CHANGE WEDGE
Some verticals show poor repeat-sale potential while another is materially stronger.

### QUESTION THESIS
Qualified creators consistently act on Mara recommendations but no meaningful revenue lift appears.

Marketing exists to discover truth quickly, not to manufacture the appearance of traction.
