# MARA — 90-DAY EXECUTION ROADMAP V1

Status: **CURRENT EXECUTION SEQUENCE**  
Parent authority: `MARA_FOUNDER_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`  
Product map: `docs/strategy/MARA_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md`  
Effective: 2026-09-08

This roadmap exists to prevent strategic ideas from becoming engineering commitments before evidence exists.

The next 90 days should prove only three business hypotheses:

1. target creators value Mara's privacy proposition enough to activate;
2. users produce economically meaningful demand, not only passive engagement;
3. validated demand can become fulfilled digital commerce and repeat purchase with viable economics.

---

# 2. STAGE 1 — CREATOR SUPPLY VALIDATION

## Business question

Will the target creator actually join because Mara reduces identity exposure while still creating a credible path to earnings?

## Actions

- recruit/interview 10–20 target adult creator candidates;
- test the positioning: character/public persona without making civil identity the public product;
- test exposure modes;
- test geo exclusion / protected-city demand;
- test voice modes;
- test what they are willing to sell;
- test acceptable creator workload;
- test payout/take-rate expectations;
- test whether Mara-sourced discovery changes willingness to join;
- use existing `/creators` intake rather than building a new acquisition product.

## Capture structured fields

- preferred exposure mode;
- voice mode;
- geo protection need;
- product family interest;
- desired involvement/time;
- existing audience yes/no;
- expected earnings threshold;
- objections;
- willingness to pilot.

Do not capture unnecessary sensitive identity details in research artifacts.

## Success gate

Directional evidence that a meaningful subset will:

`JOIN PILOT → DEFINE CHARACTER/PRIVACY → DEFINE FIRST OFFER`

Do not call interview enthusiasm PMF.

---

# 3. STAGE 2 — PAYMENT / COMPLIANCE FEASIBILITY IN PARALLEL

## Business question

Can Mara legally and operationally support the intended creator marketplace, content categories and payout structure?

## Actions

- verify provider eligibility for marketplace payouts and actual content categories;
- identify KYC/age/consent requirements;
- document refund/chargeback/reserve implications;
- identify geographic constraints;
- define creator rights/likeness/voice evidence;
- confirm private asset handling model;
- define baseline moderation/report/takedown process.

## Gate

No real-money marketplace activation until provider eligibility is explicit for the actual business model.

A technically successful API payment is not eligibility evidence.

# 4. STAGE 3 — DEMAND BEHAVIOR PROOF

## Business question

Will users express demand strongly enough to guide supply?

## Canonical product evidence surface

`/experience/demand-marketplace-lab`

## Do not create another lab.

Extend or reuse the canonical integrator where necessary.

## Validate

- demand creation;
- duplicate clustering;
- WANT;
- PLEDGE / WTP;
- COMMIT;
- threshold/progress comprehension;
- Creator World context;
- privacy mode comprehension;
- What Your World Wants;
- My History consequence.

## Demand families to prioritize

1. collective digital Creator request;
2. digital drop/product;
3. limited digital experience;
4. membership/Inner Circle.

Physical/IRL examples may remain synthetic research examples but are not an execution dependency.

## Success signals

- meaningful WANT → PLEDGE progression;
- non-trivial PLEDGE → COMMIT progression;
- users understand COMMIT is not the same as payment;
- creators can rank opportunities from aggregate signal;
- demand clusters reduce duplicate fragmentation.

# 5. STAGE 4 — PRIVATE CREATOR PILOT DESIGN

Only after supply interest + provider/compliance credibility exist.

## Initial pilot size

Prefer **3–5 carefully selected creators** for the first operational loop rather than opening a marketplace.

Expand toward 5–10 only after the first creators can complete the loop.

## Required creator capabilities

- private verification/authorization;
- public character;
- baseline exposure/privacy settings;
- at least one digital offer;
- clear boundaries;
- creator ownership of offer/fulfillment;
- earnings/payout visibility when provider enabled.

## Required user capabilities

- discover/enter World;
- express/join demand;
- purchase approved digital offer when enabled;
- receive fulfillment;
- see history;
- receive related next-action/offer without aggressive upsell.

---

# 6. STAGE 5 — FIRST COMMERCIAL LOOP

## Core proof

`DEMAND → APPROVED OFFER → PURCHASE → FULFILLMENT → SECOND PURCHASE`

Do not scale creator count before this loop works.

## First commercial inventory

Favor:

- reusable digital products;
- limited drops;
- audio/digital collections;
- personalized async products only where workload is bounded;
- simple membership only after enough repeat value exists.

Avoid:

- founder-managed physical events;
- high-touch custom operations;
- unlimited messaging;
- complicated live scheduling before demand exists.

## Economic truth

Track:

`GMV - creator payout - processing - refunds - chargebacks - support - moderation - AI/media/infrastructure - creator acquisition incentives = contribution margin`

# 10. 90-DAY DECISION GATES

## Gate A — Creator pull

If target creators do not materially value privacy + character monetization, stop building advanced creator tooling and reassess ICP/value proposition.

## Gate B — Demand strength

If users WANT but do not PLEDGE/COMMIT, do not build supply marketplaces from weak signals. Improve product/offer framing or narrow demand categories.

## Gate C — Payment feasibility

If no viable compliant payment/payout path exists for the chosen initial category/geography, narrow category/geography before further commerce engineering.

## Gate D — Fulfillment economics

If digital fulfillment cannot produce healthy creator earnings and contribution margin, do not expand to more operationally complex IRL layers.

## Gate E — Repeat behavior

If first purchase does not create meaningful second-purchase intent/behavior, improve offer/catalog/World continuity before scaling acquisition.
