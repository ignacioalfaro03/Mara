# MARA — 30 DAY FOUNDER EXECUTION BOARD V1

Status: launch execution board  
Effective: 2026-09-13  
Depends on: `MARA_GO_TO_MARKET_V1.md`, `MARA_FIRST_10_CREATORS_PLAYBOOK.md`, `MARA_PILOT_OPERATING_SYSTEM_V1.md`.

## Objective

Use the next 30 days to answer one question with evidence:

> Can Mara repeatedly help creators who already monetize generate measurable next-purchase revenue or materially improve the workflow required to produce it?

This board optimizes for commercial learning and proof, not vanity growth.

## Founder rules

- do not optimize signups before proving creator value;
- do not fabricate case studies, revenue, testimonials or screenshots;
- do not build features solely because one creator asks;
- do not add a new creator vertical without a reason;
- do not count revenue without evidence and attribution discipline;
- use manual operations when that is faster than premature automation;
- every week must end with a decision, not only activity.

## 30-day target state

By day 30 aim to have:

- 40–60 researched prospects;
- 20–30 high-quality personalized outreach attempts;
- 8–12 discovery conversations;
- 5–10 qualified pilots started;
- >= 5 creators reaching a real Mara opportunity;
- >= 3 creators executing at least one recommendation;
- >= 2 creators producing a measurable commercial outcome or strong falsification result;
- evidence sufficient to identify the strongest initial wedge;
- pricing signal from every completed pilot;
- at least one defensible case-study candidate if the evidence supports it.

These are learning targets, not promises.

# WEEK 1 — FIND THE REAL WEDGE

## Outcome

Qualified conversations with creators who already sell and have repeat/upsell economics.

## Actions

### Prospecting
- research 15–20 creators;
- score each with the 12-point prospect score;
- reject weak monetization/no-repeat candidates;
- complete research card before outreach.

### Outreach
- send 8–10 personalized first touches;
- use a specific commercial observation;
- position Mara around lost repeat revenue / commercial follow-up;
- do not lead with AI, software or feature lists.

### Discovery
Target 3–5 calls.

Capture:
- current offers;
- buyer journey;
- where customer context lives;
- repeat purchase visibility;
- follow-up process;
- one concrete revenue leakage hypothesis;
- what proof would make Mara credible.

## Week 1 gate

Proceed aggressively only if at least 2 creators acknowledge a meaningful follow-up/repeat-revenue problem.

If nobody recognizes the problem:
- review ICP before changing product;
- inspect outreach language;
- distinguish bad targeting from a false problem thesis.

---

# WEEK 2 — OPERATE THE FIRST REVENUE SPRINTS

## Outcome

Move from conversation to action.

## Actions

### Pilot onboarding
Start 2–4 pilots with measurable baselines.

For each creator:
- freeze baseline period;
- capture current offers and prices;
- identify available customer signals;
- define attribution window;
- generate only 1–3 high-confidence opportunities.

### Opportunity quality
Prioritize:
1. past buyer with logical next purchase;
2. high-value buyer now inactive;
3. customer with repeated request/intent;
4. buyer of entry offer suitable for next-tier offer;
5. existing customer suitable for recurring/membership offer.

### Action
Creator approves every outbound action.

Track in `MARA_REVENUE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_V1.csv`.

## Week 2 gate

Healthy signal:
- creators understand why an opportunity exists;
- creators are willing to act;
- recommended action is not merely obvious admin work;
- at least one opportunity progresses commercially.

If creators refuse to act, classify why:
- trust;
- recommendation quality;
- offer quality;
- timing;
- creator effort;
- channel risk.

Do not hide refusal inside “engagement”.

---

# WEEK 3 — PROVE OR FALSIFY SECOND-PURCHASE VALUE

## Outcome

Obtain stronger commercial evidence and identify the best creator archetype.

## Actions

### Expand selectively
Add 2–4 additional pilots, preferentially from the strongest Week 1–2 segment.

### Evidence
For every outcome capture:
- exact action;
- exact timestamp;
- result;
- revenue if any;
- whether creator had already planned the action;
- evidence link/reference;
- attribution confidence.

### Pricing interviews
Ask completed pilots:
- would you continue using Mara?
- would you pay monthly, performance-based, or hybrid?
- what result would justify the fee?
- what feels risky/unfair about each pricing model?

Do not lock pricing from hypothetical willingness alone.

## Week 3 gate

Choose one:

### A — CONCENTRATE
At least one creator archetype shows fast activation + action + measurable value.

Action: focus Week 4 acquisition there.

### B — ITERATE
Problem is real, but opportunity quality or workflow is weak.

Action: improve only the repeated bottleneck.

### C — REPOSITION
Creators value the workflow but not the “next purchase” framing.

Action: preserve evidence and rewrite wedge before adding features.

### D — STOP CURRENT WEDGE
Qualified creators repeatedly do not value or act on recommendations.

Action: document falsification before changing thesis.

---

# WEEK 4 — TURN SIGNAL INTO A REPEATABLE MOTION

## Outcome

Convert scattered learning into a founder-repeatable acquisition and delivery loop.

## Actions

### Narrow ICP
Define the strongest initial archetype with:
- monetization model;
- repeat-purchase mechanism;
- customer-data availability;
- common revenue leakage;
- fastest time to value;
- economic willingness to pay.

### Lock best message
Choose based on replies and discovery quality, not preference.

Document:
- best opening observation;
- best problem wording;
- best one-line Mara explanation;
- strongest objection answer;
- strongest CTA.

### Lock minimum Revenue Sprint
Define the smallest operating package that consistently creates value:

`BASELINE → CUSTOMER SIGNALS → 1–3 OPPORTUNITIES → APPROVAL → ACTION → EVIDENCE → NEXT DECISION`

Anything not required for this loop is secondary.

### Case study decision
Publish only if proof is strong enough.

Minimum credible case study:
- real creator context;
- baseline;
- Mara-originated recommendation;
- creator action;
- measurable result;
- evidence/permission;
- conservative attribution.

Otherwise publish a learning/teardown, not fake success proof.

## Day 30 decision

Choose one primary operating decision:

### SCALE FOUNDER-LED ACQUISITION
Use when a repeatable creator/value pattern exists.

### CONTINUE CONTROLLED PILOTS
Use when signal is positive but sample/evidence is insufficient.

### PRODUCT ITERATION
Use only when the same bottleneck repeats across qualified creators.

### WEDGE CHANGE
Use when the problem/value thesis is materially falsified.

## Decision notes

Every Friday write five lines:

1. What generated the strongest commercial signal?
2. What looked promising but failed in reality?
3. Which creator segment should receive more founder attention?
4. What product work is now justified by repeated evidence?
5. What will we deliberately NOT do next week?

# Operating principle

Mara does not earn the right to scale by shipping more software.

Mara earns the right to scale when creators repeatedly act on its commercial decisions and those decisions produce measurable value.
