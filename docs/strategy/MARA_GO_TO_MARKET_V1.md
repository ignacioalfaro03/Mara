# MARA — GO-TO-MARKET V1

Status: strategy working document  
Effective: 2026-09-12  
Authority: subordinate to `docs/foundation/MARA_FOUNDATIONAL_THESIS.md`

## 1. Strategic objective

Mara must win by proving one business outcome:

> Mara helps creators generate more repeatable revenue from the audience they already have.

Mara is not marketed as another link-in-bio, generic CRM, chatbot, AI tool, storefront builder, or creator social network.

Working category:

# CREATOR REVENUE OS

Working positioning:

> Turn followers into customers. Know your customers. Grow your revenue.

Stronger outcome framing for founder-led sales:

> Mara tells you where your next revenue opportunity is and helps you act on it.

## 2. Initial market wedge

Do not start with every creator.

Initial ICP:

- independent creator or creator-led microbusiness;
- already has an audience, preferably 5K–100K followers as a working range;
- already sells at least one product, service, session, membership, digital product or paid experience;
- receives commercial intent through Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, email or DMs;
- manages customer context manually, in spreadsheets, chat history, payment apps or memory;
- has repeat-purchase potential;
- can legally and policy-compliantly sell through Mara;
- is reachable directly by founder-led outbound.

Revenue signal matters more than follower count.

Priority verticals for discovery:

1. fitness / trainers;
2. beauty / personal care creators;
3. fashion / styling creators;
4. coaches and educators;
5. photographers / visual creators;
6. niche influencers selling digital offers or services;
7. creators with recurring consultations, memberships or repeat digital sales.

Avoid over-specializing before interviews reveal where Mara creates the fastest revenue lift.

## 3. Core pain to sell

Do not lead with features.

Lead with commercial leakage:

- followers are visible but customers are not understood;
- buyers disappear after the first purchase;
- repeat opportunities live inside DMs and memory;
- creators do not know who to contact today;
- creators do not know what offer to send next;
- there is weak attribution between actions and revenue;
- existing tools help publish or communicate but do not necessarily identify the next best commercial action.

The key marketing question Mara should own:

> Who should buy from you next, what should you offer them, and when?

## 4. Product promise

Primary promise:

# TURN AUDIENCE INTO REPEAT REVENUE.

Supporting promises:

- know who your real customers are;
- see who is ready to buy again;
- know what to sell next;
- stop losing repeat revenue inside DMs and spreadsheets;
- measure whether Mara is actually generating additional revenue.

Never promise guaranteed income.

## 5. Competitive angle

The market already has strong creator storefront, membership, email, link-in-bio and commerce platforms.

Current examples:

- Patreon: memberships, one-time digital purchases and creator pages, with a standard 10% platform fee for new creators under its current pricing model;
- Fourthwall: storefront and creator commerce with no monthly base fee on its standard offer and 5% fees on digital products and memberships;
- Beacons: link-in-bio, store, email and creator tools, with a free plan that currently includes a seller fee and paid plans that reduce/remove it.

Mara should not compete by saying it has the same features for slightly less money.

Differentiation target:

> Other tools help creators sell. Mara should learn who buys, detect where revenue is being left on the table, and recommend the next commercial action.

This becomes defensible only if Mara accumulates real creator-scoped commercial memory and proves recommendations improve outcomes.

## 6. Founder-led launch motion

Initial GTM is founder-led and high-touch.

Do not begin with paid ads.

Sequence:

1. identify a creator with existing monetization;
2. research what they currently sell and how fans buy;
3. send a short personalized outreach;
4. conduct a 15–25 minute discovery conversation;
5. map current customer/repeat-sale workflow;
6. onboard creator manually where needed;
7. create or import one concrete offer;
8. surface a small set of revenue opportunities;
9. execute one action;
10. measure whether it generated revenue;
11. collect qualitative + quantitative evidence;
12. turn the result into a case study or referral if permission is granted.

Early manual work is acceptable if it teaches Mara what to automate.

## 7. Acquisition channels — phase 1

Priority order:

### A. Founder outbound

Primary channel for first 10–30 creators.

Sources:

- Instagram creator discovery;
- TikTok niche discovery;
- local creator networks in Chile;
- mutual contacts;
- creator communities;
- agencies / managers only after direct creator learning begins.

Outreach must reference the creator's actual business, not send mass generic DMs.

### B. Founder content

Publish evidence, not startup theatre.

Content themes:

- where creators lose repeat revenue;
- why follower count is not customer ownership;
- examples of simple second-purchase opportunities;
- teardown of a creator monetization funnel;
- anonymized lessons from Mara pilots;
- revenue operations for creators;
- creator CRM explained simply;
- before/after commercial workflows.

CTA:

> I am testing Mara with a small number of creators who already sell. If you want me to map where you may be leaving repeat revenue on the table, message me.

### C. Referrals

Every successful pilot should produce a referral ask.

Target mechanism:

- creator gets a measurable outcome;
- founder asks for 1–2 introductions to creators with similar monetization problems;
- no referral incentive initially unless organic introductions are weak.

### D. Partnerships — later

Only after first direct evidence:

- creator managers;
- small agencies;
- course communities;
- fitness / beauty professional networks;
- creator education communities.

Do not make partnerships a substitute for direct creator learning.

## 8. First offer to creators

Do not sell "software" first.

Sell a concrete pilot:

# MARA REVENUE SPRINT — 7 DAYS

Promise:

> We map your current customers and offers, identify the highest-probability revenue opportunities, and help you act on them. The goal is to generate or identify measurable incremental revenue in seven days.

Pilot structure:

Day 0 — qualify creator and obtain consent for data used in the pilot.  
Day 1 — map offers, current purchase flow and customer records.  
Day 2 — identify 3–10 concrete revenue opportunities.  
Day 3 — creator approves actions/offers.  
Day 4–6 — execute and monitor.  
Day 7 — review revenue, conversion, creator effort and learning.

The first goal is not automation. It is proof.

## 9. Pilot qualification criteria

Accept creators with:

- at least one existing paid offer;
- recent customers or credible purchase intent;
- ability to contact customers/audience lawfully;
- willingness to test one recommendation;
- willingness to share enough commercial outcome data to evaluate the pilot.

Reject or defer when:

- there is no offer to sell;
- there is effectively no audience or customer signal;
- the creator expects Mara to create demand from zero;
- the business is legally/policy problematic;
- there is no path to measure outcome.

## 10. First 10 creator portfolio

Goal: learning diversity without losing comparability.

Suggested composition:

- 3 service/consultation creators;
- 2 fitness/wellness creators;
- 2 beauty/fashion/lifestyle creators;
- 2 digital-product or education creators;
- 1 wildcard creator with unusually strong repeat-purchase behavior.

Each pilot must record:

- audience size;
- existing offer(s);
- current monthly creator revenue range if voluntarily shared;
- number of known customers;
- average ticket;
- repeat purchase rate if known;
- current tool stack;
- how customers are tracked today;
- opportunity recommended;
- whether creator acted;
- resulting revenue;
- time spent by creator;
- qualitative usefulness score;
- willingness to continue/pay/refer.

## 11. Messaging architecture

### One-line category

> Mara is the Revenue OS for creators.

### Outcome line

> Turn followers into customers and customers into repeat revenue.

### Pain line

> Your followers live on social platforms. Your customers should not live inside forgotten DMs.

### Product differentiation line

> Mara does not just show analytics. It tells you where the next commercial opportunity is.

### Creator-facing CTA

> Let Mara find the next revenue opportunities inside the audience and customers you already have.

Avoid:

- "AI-powered all-in-one platform";
- vague productivity claims;
- get-rich-quick language;
- passive-income promises;
- saying Mara replaces every creator tool;
- leading with technical architecture.

## 12. Funnel

Founder-led funnel:

`TARGET CREATOR → PERSONALIZED OUTREACH → DISCOVERY → QUALIFIED PILOT → FIRST OFFER LIVE → FIRST CUSTOMER SIGNAL → FIRST REVENUE OPPORTUNITY → ACTION → INCREMENTAL SALE → RETENTION / REFERRAL`

Track conversion at every step.

Initial operating targets — hypotheses, not commitments:

- 50 highly targeted prospects;
- 20 meaningful replies;
- 10 discovery calls;
- 5 qualified pilots;
- 3 creators executing a Mara recommendation;
- 1+ measurable revenue-lift event;
- 1+ referral from a creator who experienced value.

If reply rate is weak, fix positioning/outreach.  
If calls occur but pilots do not start, fix qualification/value proposition.  
If pilots start but no action occurs, fix product/actionability.  
If actions happen but no incremental revenue appears, question the core thesis before scaling acquisition.

## 13. Pricing strategy

Canonical launch hypothesis from the foundational thesis remains authoritative:

- low or zero fixed entry cost;
- success-based platform take rate;
- optional Creator Pro later.

Indicative validation range: 10–15% take rate, not a permanent pricing commitment.

Do not lock pricing before validating:

- creator willingness to pay;
- payment processing cost;
- support burden;
- fraud/refund/chargeback economics;
- relative value of Revenue Brain and CRM;
- creator contribution margin.

Possible future structure:

### Mara Start
Low/zero fixed fee + higher success-based take rate.

### Mara Pro
Fixed SaaS fee + lower take rate + advanced CRM/automation/intelligence.

### Mara Scale
Team workflows, advanced analytics, larger customer bases, lower marginal take rate.

Do not publish final prices until real creator interviews and pilot economics support them.

## 14. Growth loops

### Revenue proof loop

`Mara recommendation → creator action → sale → measurable lift → case study → new creator`

### Referral loop

`creator wins → creator refers peer → peer pilots Mara → new outcome`

### Intelligence loop

`more transactions → richer commercial history → better recommendations → more successful actions → more transactions`

Never claim cross-creator intelligence or network effects before privacy-safe scale exists.

## 16. Metrics

Primary GTM metrics:

- qualified creator response rate;
- discovery-to-pilot conversion;
- pilot activation rate;
- time to first offer;
- time to first sale;
- time to first Mara-originated opportunity;
- opportunity action rate;
- recommendation conversion rate;
- measurable Mara Revenue Lift;
- creator D30 retention;
- creator referral rate;
- GMV per active creator;
- repeat-purchase GMV.

Vanity metrics such as impressions, registrations and followers are secondary unless they predict revenue activation.

## 17. 30-day launch strategy

### Week 1 — message + pipeline

- assemble 50-target creator list;
- conduct first discovery conversations;
- learn vocabulary creators actually use;
- refine offer and qualification;
- recruit first 2 pilots.

### Week 2 — pilots

- operate pilots manually where needed;
- identify first revenue opportunities;
- capture objections and workflow friction;
- recruit creators 3–5.

### Week 3 — evidence

- push for first measurable second-purchase/revenue-lift event;
- document the full before/action/after chain;
- build first case study;
- begin founder content based on actual lessons.

### Week 4 — repeatability

- compare pilots;
- identify strongest vertical/wedge;
- refine ICP;
- refine onboarding;
- decide whether the next 20 creators should concentrate in one niche;
- only then consider scalable acquisition experiments.

## 18. Decision gates

Do not scale marketing spend until Mara proves:

1. creators understand the promise;
2. qualified creators start pilots;
3. creators actually execute recommended actions;
4. at least some actions create measurable revenue;
5. creators want to keep using Mara;
6. economics can support acquisition.

A strong launch signal is not account creation.

It is:

> A creator says: "Mara showed me a revenue opportunity I would otherwise have missed, I acted on it, and it made me money."

That is the marketing proof Mara needs.
