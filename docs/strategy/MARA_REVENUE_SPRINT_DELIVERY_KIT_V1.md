# MARA — REVENUE SPRINT DELIVERY KIT V1

Status: founder delivery runbook  
Effective: 2026-09-13  
Depends on: `MARA_PILOT_OPERATING_SYSTEM_V1.md` and `MARA_30_DAY_FOUNDER_EXECUTION_BOARD_V1.md`.

## Purpose

Standardize how Mara delivers a 7-day creator Revenue Sprint before the workflow is fully automated.

The sprint must prove value with the minimum creator burden and the minimum data required.

## Promise boundary

Mara may promise:

> We will analyze your current customer/revenue signals, identify a small number of evidence-backed next-purchase opportunities, help you execute them, and measure what happened.

Mara must not promise:
- guaranteed revenue;
- guaranteed conversion;
- magic AI predictions;
- automated messaging without creator approval;
- attribution that cannot be evidenced.

## Day 0 — Qualification + consent

Before onboarding confirm:

- creator already sells something;
- repeat/upsell path exists;
- creator owns or is legitimately authorized to use the customer information supplied;
- creator agrees to the pilot measurement process;
- creator understands Mara recommends actions but does not autonomously contact customers in the early pilot;
- sensitive data not required for the commercial decision is excluded.

Output: `PILOT NOW / NURTURE / REJECT FOR NOW`.

## Day 1 — Baseline intake

### Minimum creator inputs

1. Current offer list
   - offer name;
   - price;
   - what the buyer receives;
   - repeat/upgrade path.

2. Recent commercial signals
   - recent buyers;
   - recent inquiries/requests;
   - previous buyers suitable for repeat/upsell;
   - known high-value/recurrent buyers.

3. Existing follow-up method
   - DM/manual notes/spreadsheet/CRM/none;
   - who decides follow-up;
   - how frequently it happens.

4. Measurement baseline
   - chosen baseline period;
   - sales/revenue if available;
   - repeat purchases if available;
   - active sales conversations already underway.

### Data minimization

Do not collect:
- government IDs;
- unnecessary addresses;
- payment credentials;
- passwords;
- unrelated private conversation content;
- health or other sensitive personal information not required for the commercial task.

Use customer references/pseudonymous IDs where possible during manual analysis.

## Day 2 — Opportunity review

Founder/operator produces 1–3 opportunities.

Each opportunity card:

### WHO
Customer/segment reference.

### SIGNAL
What actually happened?

### OPPORTUNITY
What commercial next step exists?

### RECOMMENDED ACTION
What should the creator do?

### OFFER
What should be offered, if anything?

### WHY NOW
Why is timing justified?

### EVIDENCE CLASS
A / B / C.

### EXPECTED VALUE HYPOTHESIS
A hypothesis, never fake probability.

### CREATOR DECISION
APPROVE / MODIFY / REJECT.

No outbound action before creator approval.

## Day 3–5 — Execution window

For every approved opportunity:

1. agree on the message/offer;
2. record action channel;
3. record timestamp;
4. execute through the creator's existing channel where practical;
5. capture response/progression;
6. avoid repeated pressure on the customer;
7. preserve the creator's tone and commercial relationship.

Mara should optimize selective selling, not spam volume.

## Day 6 — Evidence close

For every action classify:

- WON;
- PROGRESSED;
- NO SALE;
- REJECTED;
- INVALID.

For WON outcomes collect:
- commercial result;
- gross revenue;
- evidence reference;
- whether sale was already independently in motion;
- creator counterfactual assessment;
- Mara attribution confidence.

Update the revenue evidence ledger.

## Day 7 — Creator review

### Review agenda

1. What Mara observed.
2. What creator approved/rejected.
3. What actions happened.
4. What commercial results happened.
5. What cannot be claimed.
6. Creator usefulness score.
7. Continue/pay/referral signal.
8. Next recommendation.

### Close question

> If Mara kept finding opportunities at this quality and showed you which actions actually generated revenue, would you keep using it?

Then ask pricing preference only after discussing actual value.

## Creator-facing deliverable

Keep the sprint summary to one page where possible.

### Section 1 — Baseline
What was true before Mara.

### Section 2 — Opportunities
The 1–3 opportunities identified.

### Section 3 — Actions
What the creator actually did.

### Section 4 — Results
Revenue/progression/no-result with evidence.

### Section 5 — Next best action
One prioritized recommendation after the sprint.

## Internal delivery checklist

Before marking the sprint complete:

- [ ] creator qualified;
- [ ] baseline frozen;
- [ ] attribution window defined;
- [ ] only necessary data collected;
- [ ] opportunities evidence-backed;
- [ ] creator approved actions;
- [ ] actions timestamped;
- [ ] outcomes classified;
- [ ] revenue evidence stored/referenced;
- [ ] attributed GMV conservative;
- [ ] creator feedback captured;
- [ ] willingness to continue/pay captured;
- [ ] product friction tagged;
- [ ] final decision recorded.

## Manual-first rule

The first pilots may use manual founder analysis and creator-owned communication channels.

Automation is justified only when a repeated workflow is proven across multiple qualified creators.

Examples of automation-worthy repetition:
- importing recent purchases;
- identifying first→second purchase gaps;
- ranking dormant high-value buyers;
- generating opportunity cards;
- outcome attribution;
- creator approval queue.

Do not automate one-off founder rituals that have not proven commercial value.

## Sprint success hierarchy

Strongest to weakest:

1. measurable attributed second purchase/upsell;
2. measurable purchase influenced by Mara;
3. qualified commercial progression attributable to Mara;
4. creator repeatedly acts on Mara recommendations;
5. creator reports workflow value but no commercial progression;
6. creator likes the concept only.

Only levels 1–3 are commercial proof.

## Operating principle

Mara's first service layer exists to discover the product truth.

Manual delivery is acceptable.

Unmeasured delivery is not.
