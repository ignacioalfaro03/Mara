## Research question

What actually creates value in findom communities beyond the superficial social-media pattern of `send money because I am attractive`?

## Observed split in the market

Public discussion suggests two recurring models.

### 1. Fast / performative / social-media findom

Common surface patterns:

- `send` / `tribute` language;
- screenshots of transfers;
- spoiled-brat framing;
- `pay me because I exist` content;
- coffee / lunch / shopping asks;
- high-frequency demands;
- little initial context;
- fast burn / fast churn;
- side-hustle education content telling newcomers this is easy money.

This format is highly legible in short-form social media and therefore useful for acquisition/virality research.

But community discussions repeatedly criticize it as shallow, repetitive, transactional and often disconnected from actual domination or durable D/s dynamics.

### 2. Relationship / power-exchange findom

Recurring community language emphasizes:

- power exchange;
- structure;
- protocol;
- anticipation;
- pacing;
- rules;
- ritual;
- trust;
- boundaries;
- consent;
- personalization;
- long-term continuity;
- the dominant actually understanding the submissive;
- money as an expression of the dynamic rather than the only content of the dynamic.

A useful distilled principle:

> **MONEY IS THE MEDIUM. POWER / RELATIONSHIP / RITUAL IS THE EXPERIENCE.**

This is strategically more relevant to Mara than copying public `send now` content.

## Pacing / anti-burnout implication

Recent community discussion explicitly criticizes accelerated spend sequences and constant quick drains as increasingly transactional and intimacy-destroying.

For Mara this reinforces:

- commercial attention budget;
- no offer after every intense moment;
- varied session rhythm;
- ordinary conversation after power play;
- occasional small gestures rather than constant asks;
- open loops / later callbacks;
- long-term continuity over maximum same-session extraction.

> **DO NOT OPTIMIZE A FINDOM USER FOR MAXIMUM SENDS PER SESSION.**

The community itself distinguishes consensual findom from financial abuse.

Mara's hard firewall remains stronger than many public findom practices.
