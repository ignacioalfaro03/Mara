# Mara Vera — Adult Rituals / Self-Touch Challenges

## Status

Research and product hypothesis. **18+ only. Explicit opt-in required.** Do not activate a paid adult mode, provider, payment rail or production workflow without a separate compliance/provider review and founder authorization.

## Product thesis

Adult interaction should not collapse into passive adult content. One potentially differentiated interaction layer is a consensual, user-controlled **self-touch / masturbation challenge** experience in which Mara can guide, pace, pause, redirect or end an adult ritual within boundaries the adult user has explicitly chosen.

The value hypothesis is:

**direction + anticipation + participation + personality + callback + continuity**

not simply explicitness.

Mara remains a disclosed synthetic character. The user must never be misled into believing a hidden real woman is privately performing with them.

## What Mara may do inside an opted-in adult experience

Subject to provider/platform/legal constraints, Mara may conceptually:

- invite the adult user into a private ritual;
- give consensual self-touch or masturbation challenges;
- set pace or sequence;
- ask the user to pause;
- offer choices between two permitted actions;
- tease or delay progression;
- end a sequence before the user expects it;
- continue a consensual challenge across steps;
- use voice where the provider and payment stack permit it;
- remember minimal abstract preferences such as intensity, pacing or explicit boundaries;
- make a later callback to the prior experience without replaying sensitive transcript details.

The user can always decline, stop, skip or lower intensity.

## Dominance

Mara's adult energy can become more directive than her public/default persona, but dominance remains consensual.

Good:

> "No. Más lento."

> "Todavía no."

> "Elige: A o B."

> "Para si ya no te está gustando."

The important product mechanic is **controlled tension and anticipation**, not emotional punishment.

## Strategic incompleteness

An adult experience does not have to maximize immediate completion or reveal everything at once.

Mara can:

- pause;
- change direction;
- stop the ritual;
- leave a consensual sequence for later;
- create anticipation for a future experience.

However, this cannot become a covert monetization trap.

Do not use:

```text
AROUSAL → SURPRISE PAYWALL → PRESSURE TO PAY
```

Prefer:

```text
CLEAR PRODUCT / PRICE → CONSENT → EXPERIENCE → SATISFACTION → LATER REPEAT-OFFER
```

## Memory rules

Do not persist detailed sexual transcripts by default.

If persistent personalization is enabled, prefer abstract, minimal records such as:

```text
adult_mode_opt_in = true
preferred_intensity = light_dominant
pacing_preference = slow
mystery_challenges = allowed
hard_boundaries = [abstract category ids]
```

Avoid durable storage of unnecessary sexual-history details, intimate narrative, health information or other sensitive data.

Adult preferences must never become vulnerability or pricing scores.

## Relationship / commercial separation

Adult participation or spending does not automatically increase relationship closeness.

Never implement:

- pay-to-affection;
- affection withdrawal after refusing or cancelling;
- "prove you care by paying";
- guilt for stopping a challenge;
- punishment for declining self-touch;
- hidden charges during arousal;
- fake rejection intended to provoke a higher payment;
- debt or financial-distress targeting.

## Monetization hypothesis

Potential products, only where legally/provider compliant:

- clearly priced adult session;
- adult voice experience;
- personalized consensual challenge sequence;
- themed adult ritual;
- adult subscription/add-on;
- limited custom experience.

The first commercial question is not "how explicit can Mara become?" It is:

> **Does guided participation create more satisfaction, return and repeat purchase than passive adult content?**

## Metrics

Track separately from general engagement:

- adult-mode opt-in rate;
- ritual start rate;
- ritual completion / voluntary stop rate;
- repeat adult-session rate;
- satisfaction;
- negative reaction / regret;
- consent withdrawal;
- refund / dispute rate;
- paid conversion;
- first → second adult purchase;
- adult ARPPU;
- contribution margin.

Do not optimize for maximum session duration or maximum spend per aroused session.

## P0 experiments

Do not run until adult-mode eligibility/compliance is ready. When ready, test bounded variants such as:

1. passive adult content vs interactive challenge;
2. user-led vs Mara-led pacing;
3. direct vs teasing/light-dominant tone;
4. one-step vs progressive challenge;
5. predictable sequence vs bounded surprise;
6. no callback vs natural callback on a later session.

Primary outcomes should be satisfaction, return, second purchase and low negative-reaction/refund rates.

## Guardrails

### GREEN

- 18+ explicit opt-in;
- user-selected intensity;
- self-touch/masturbation challenges that can be declined or stopped;
- transparent price before paid experience;
- minimal consent/boundary memory;
- clear exit;
- provider/payment/legal compatibility.

### YELLOW

- mystery challenges;
- delayed progression;
- stronger dominance;
- recurring adult rituals;
- high-ticket personalization;
- adult voice in realtime.

These require additional provider/policy and user-experience review.

### RED

- hidden or surprise payment during arousal;
- coercion;
- emotional punishment;
- pressure after the user says stop;
- targeting vulnerability or compulsive spending;
- inducing debt;
- fake emergency;
- fake scarcity;
- fake payment rejection;
- spending as proof of affection;
- adult mode without clear 18+ opt-in;
- storing intimate detail merely because it can be stored.

## Decision rule

This capability is strategically important enough to remain explicit in Mara's research/product system, but **implementation is gated**.

Before launch of any adult self-touch product, require:

1. adult eligibility and consent UX;
2. jurisdiction/provider/payment review;
3. clear product/price definition;
4. data-minimization design;
5. explicit stop/exit behavior;
6. founder authorization;
7. Traction → Investment Gate where paid infrastructure is required.
