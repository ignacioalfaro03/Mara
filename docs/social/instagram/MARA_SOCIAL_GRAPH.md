Status: **SMALL RECURRING ADULT FICTIONAL CAST — OPERATIONAL LEDGER**

This file implements the social-world principle from `web/MARA_WORLD_CANON_MANDATE.md` and the found-footage/secondary-character rules from `web/MARA_CHARACTER_PORTFOLIO_MANDATE.md`.

> **MARA MUST NOT LOOK LIKE SHE LIVES ALONE INSIDE AN IMAGE GENERATOR.**

The answer is not “invent many friends.” The answer is a deliberately small, stable cast with recurring POVs and consequences.

---

## 1. Status vocabulary

- `ROLE_EXISTS` — role/hypothesis exists in higher canon.

- `RETIRED` — no longer active but historical facts remain.

All visible secondary characters must be unmistakably adult fictional people.

## 4. Social presence classes

A recurring person does not need a face-forward portrait every time.

Allowed social evidence:

- full person;
- partial body;
- hand reaching into frame;
- glass/plate belonging to someone across table;
- reflection;
- shadow;
- laugh/voice in video;
- phone screen camera interface only if text/privacy issues are avoided;
- POV angle known to belong to Sofi/friend;
- group composition.

This creates **presence without cast inflation**.

---

## 5. Companion continuity lock

Within a Scene Packet record:

- person ID;
- approximate position;
- clothing visible;
- hair/appearance anchors;
- relationship to camera;
- objects they hold;
- seat/table relationship;
- whether they are behind the camera for any frame.

If Sofi is seated opposite Mara in F1 and her hand/glass enters F3, it must be physically plausible from that position.

Do not regenerate the same friend as a materially different adult later in the same event.

---

## 6. Social graph expansion rule

A new recurring person earns a persistent ID only if they create a distinct narrative/production job.

Good reasons:

- recurring friend-camera POV;
- specific group context;
- multi-post arc;
- world discovery;
- distinct relationship to Mara;
- cross-surface callback.

Bad reason:

- “the feed needs more people.”

Default bias: deepen existing recurring characters before adding new ones.

---

## 7. Privacy / truth boundary

These are fictional characters in Mara's synthetic world.

Do not imply that a real identifiable person is Mara's friend/partner without legitimate authorization and context.

Do not fabricate real celebrity relationships, partnerships or endorsements.
