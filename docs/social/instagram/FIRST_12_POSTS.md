# MARA VERA — FIRST 12 INSTAGRAM POSTS

Status: **LAUNCH CONTENT SEQUENCE V1**

Use together with:

- `MARA_CHARACTER_CANON.md`
- `INSTAGRAM_CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`
- `SCENE_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md`

This is not a rigid calendar. It is the initial **feed rhythm**: Mara should feel like she already has a world, habits, taste and ordinary life around the hero images.

Permanent rule: every numbered post below is an **episode/carousel**, not a folder of unrelated renders.

---

## Feed sequence logic

The first 12 posts should establish four things in parallel:

1. **Who Mara is** — face, body, attitude, visual signature.
2. **How Mara lives** — places, routines, food, home, movement, city.
3. **How Mara feels** — playful, close, self-confident, subtly challenging, quietly dominant.
4. **Why the account is worth following** — variety, continuity, taste and a believable camera roll.

Do not front-load twelve maximum-intensity glamour posts. Contrast creates memory.

## POST 01 — Late lunch, first real introduction

**Mode:** NATURAL + COQUETA

**Concept:** Mara at a warm, small restaurant in late-afternoon light. The account opens as if we caught her in an actual day, not at a launch photoshoot.

**Frames:**

1. Cover: Mara seated at table, direct restrained gaze.
2. Exact plate from frame 1 in close-up.
3. Candid from across table while she looks away / fixes hair.
4. Table detail: same glass, fork, napkin, bag edge.
5. Full outfit near restaurant mirror or exterior after eating.
6. Closing exterior / street light / doorway.

**Continuity priority:** plate, table, glass, bag, jewelry, hair and light.

**Caption direction:** tiny, understated. Example energy: `llegué con hambre`.

## What the 12-post grid should communicate

After these 12 posts, a new visitor should not think:

> “This is a folder of AI pictures of a blonde woman.”

They should understand, almost instantly:

> “This is Mara. I recognize her. She has a specific look, attitude, routines, places and way of being photographed.”

That is the launch threshold.

## Production rule for every post in this file

Before generating frame 1:

1. create a Scene Packet;
2. lock wardrobe, props, food and environment;
3. plan the full carousel;
4. generate one frame at a time;
5. update state after every accepted frame.

If frame 2 contradicts frame 1, **frame 2 is wrong even if it is prettier**.
