## 7. Frame-level mix target

Across final generated frames, not just post concepts:

- Mara-primary frames: approximately 55–70%.
- environment/object/food/social-primary frames: approximately 30–45%.
- deliberately imperfect-but-valid: at least 10%, preferably 10–20% when natural.
- direct hero/pose frames: no more than needed to carry recognition and cover quality.

If actual asset generation drifts above ~80% Mara-primary frames, the sequence design is not being followed.

## 8. Continuity debt score

Before approving any new durable visual fact, score it:

### LOW debt

Easy to reproduce and likely useful:

- simple bag;
- phone case;
- mug;
- basic clothing;
- stable home corner.

### MEDIUM debt

Needs careful references:

- distinctive café;
- gym locker zone;
- car interior;
- recurring companion outfit/style.

### HIGH debt

Avoid unless strategically worth it:

- complex hotel room with many distinctive geometric details;
- elaborate patterned clothing intended to recur;
- highly intricate jewelry;
- crowded scenes with multiple recurring identities;
- prominent text-heavy environments.

Rule:

> **DO NOT CREATE HIGH CONTINUITY DEBT FOR A ONE-SECOND VISUAL IMPROVEMENT.**
