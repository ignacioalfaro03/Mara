## 1. Launch-arc objectives

By Post 20, a new follower should be able to feel — without being told explicitly — that Mara has:

- one stable identity;
- a recognizable home;
- a recurring phone/case;
- recurring jewelry;
- at least one recurring bag;
- clothes she rewore;
- a café / urban routine;
- a gym/routine space;
- a small social circle;
- a friend-camera POV;
- a timeline;
- ordinary days between stronger visual moments.

The emotional objective is:

> **“Ya conozco un poco su vida y quiero ver qué sube después.”**

# PHASE I — INTRODUCE MARA WITHOUT LOOKING LIKE A LAUNCH CAMPAIGN

## POST 01 — LATE LUNCH

**Primary job:** face recognition + social-native introduction.

**Mode:** NATURAL + COQUETA.

**World job:** establish the first external episode and one or two reusable objects.

**Scene:** late-afternoon small restaurant/café-restaurant. Warm but not luxury-coded.

**Frames:**

1. Cover: Mara seated, restrained direct gaze; enough table visible to establish meal and materials.
2. Exact same meal close-up.
3. Candid from across table; Mara looking away/fixing hair/reaching for something.
4. Table detail: same glass, cutlery, napkin, bag edge/phone if established.
5. Full outfit only if physically plausible via nearby mirror/exterior.
6. Closer: doorway/street/emptying table/late-afternoon exterior.

**Must not happen:** changing food, plate, table, bag, phone or jewelry between F1–F4.

**Reality beat:** food visibly progresses.

**Caption energy:** tiny and understated. Example direction: `llegué con hambre`.

**Story support:** optional before-story from street or menu; no hard CTA.

# 5. Quantitative launch targets

These are production guardrails, not vanity quotas.

By the end of the 20-post plan, before final publication order is locked:

- at least 5 recurring object candidates, with multiple actual reappearances after approval;
- at least 4 wardrobe pieces reworn;
- at least 3 recurring places;
- at least 2 recurring adult social identities/POVs where canon rollout permits;
- at least 2 hard multi-post arcs;
- at least 20% of total frames with Mara absent or not the primary visual subject;
- at least 10% deliberately imperfect-but-valid captures;
- no more than 2 consecutive posts whose covers have near-identical pose/crop grammar;
- no post where every frame is a glamour portrait.

If the final generated inventory does not meet these conditions, revise before launch.

# 7. Publication discipline

Do not publish P01 merely because its cover is ready.

Before launch:

1. first 20 episode briefs must remain coherent as a set;
2. first 6–9 posts must be sufficiently production-ready to avoid immediate improvisation;
3. recurring objects/wardrobe must have clear `PROPOSED` vs `LOCKED` state;
4. P05–P07 continuity must be feasible;
5. P17–P18 continuity must be feasible;
6. the feed must have context/detail assets, not only Mara portraits;
7. caption directions must be compatible with `CAPTION_SYSTEM.md`;
8. every active production episode must use a Scene Packet.

Permanent rule:

> **DESIGN THE 20-POST WORLD BEFORE SPENDING THE FIRST POST'S CONTINUITY BUDGET.**
