---

## 1. Event states

- `PLANNED` — content concept only.
- `SCENE_LOCKED` — event is in active production; not yet public canon.
- `APPROVED` — assets/event accepted as canonical content.
- `PUBLISHED` — visible publicly.
- `CLOSED` — event/arcs have ended but remain historical fact.
- `VOID` — concept abandoned; never happened in canon.

---

## 2. Time rules

1. An event has a relative order even if exact clock time is intentionally vague.
2. Multi-post arcs must preserve chronology.
3. Stories, Reels and DMs referencing an approved event must not contradict its state.
4. A planned event may move dates before approval without creating a continuity breach.
5. Once published, do not silently rewrite what happened.
6. Day/night and travel transitions must be physically plausible.

- preserve the same location/people/object facts;
- distinguish `world_truth` from what Mara chose to reveal;
