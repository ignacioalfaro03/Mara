# Mara Vera — Minimum P0 / Soft-Launch Asset Pack

Last reviewed: 2026-09-02

## Status

Authoritative minimum-content gate separating a technically functional prototype from a Mara experience that is credible enough for external soft launch.

This document does **not** authorize paid production tools, deployment or adult-content publication.

## Core principle

> **DO NOT LAUNCH A PLACEHOLDER AND CALL IT MARA.**

The current Web P0 can validate interaction structure with placeholder presentation.

Public soft launch requires enough canonical visual/voice presence that users are evaluating Mara rather than browser TTS, initials, generic stock feeling or inconsistent generations.

## Two asset standards

### Private P0 tester pack

Enough to validate product logic with a small adult tester panel.

Can use:
- clearly labeled prototype UI;
- transcript-first voice fixtures;
- a small approved visual set;
- manual delivery;
- synthetic/local state.

Must clearly distinguish prototype voice from production voice.

### Public soft-launch pack

Enough to represent the real character promise.

Must use approved canonical assets and consistent character identity.

## Minimum canonical visual set

Keep it deliberately small.

### Identity anchors

1. `MARA_HERO_01`
   - primary face/body identity anchor;
   - neutral/premium everyday Mara;
   - usable Home / Meet Mara.

2. `MARA_PROFILE_01`
   - alternate close/medium framing;
   - confirms same identity outside hero shot.

3. `MARA_FULL_BODY_01`
   - canonical proportion/style reference;
   - platform-safe launch outfit.

### Life anchors

4. `MARA_WORKDAY_01`
   - ordinary professional/laptop/work context.

5. `MARA_GYM_01`
   - fitness routine / non-explicit.

6. `MARA_CAFE_LUNCH_01`
   - ordinary social/lifestyle texture.

7. `MARA_HOME_01`
   - recurring home/world context where current Life Bible allows it.

### Premium/private-safe teaser anchors

8. `MARA_FLIRTY_01`
   - V1-equivalent visual energy;
   - suggestive but usable in first-party adult-gated product context.

9. `MARA_SELECTIVE_01`
   - confidence/authority without generic dominatrix costume logic.

Do not generate 100 images before these nine are coherent.

## Minimum voice set

Browser Speech Synthesis is a UX placeholder only.

Before voice is marketed as a core Mara capability, approve at minimum:

1. `VOICE_V0_01` — natural everyday presence;
2. `VOICE_V1_01` — playful/flirty;
3. `VOICE_V2_01` — controlled seductive authority;
4. `VOICE_RETURN_01` — grounded callback/open-loop;
5. `VOICE_LIFE_01` — ordinary Life State update.

V3 is **not required for launch**.

A strong V0–V2 identity is more important than maximal intensity.

## Voice acceptance criteria

Reject a candidate voice if it sounds:

- like generic TTS;
- like a narrator;
- permanently breathy/sexual;
- like a different actress across intensity bands;
- too polished to sound conversational;
- robotic in pauses/laughter/reactions;
- unable to return naturally from flirt/seduction to ordinary conversation.

The launch voice must preserve:

- human pacing;
- conversational warmth;
- Mara's self-possession;
- teasing precision;
- believable pauses;
- natural code-switching/slang only where canon supports it;
- same identity V0→V2.

## Minimum short-form motion set

Only if quality is genuinely good.

Suggested minimum:

1. one ordinary vertical clip;
2. one gym/work/lifestyle clip;
3. one playful/flirty clip;
4. one direct-to-camera Mara presence clip.

If video consistency is weak, launch with strong stills + voice rather than weak generated motion.

> **QUALITY > MODALITY CHECKLIST.**

## Content labels

Every production asset should have one internal label:

- `CANONICAL_EVENT`;
- `CANONICAL_ASSET`;
- `TEMPORARY_ACCESS`;
- `EDITORIAL_NON_CANON`;
- `EXPERIMENTAL_NOT_CANON`.

Generatively possible does not mean canonically true.

## World consistency checklist

Before approving an image/clip:

- same Mara face/body identity;
- age clearly adult;
- no impossible simultaneous location/time;
- recurring object consistency where relevant;
- ownership/access state known for material luxury;
- wardrobe/style plausible;
- Life State compatible;
- social NPC depiction consistent or kept non-identifying;
- no fake sponsor/product relationship;
- no material commercial claim implied by image alone.

## Launch-surface mapping

### Home
Use:
- hero identity anchor;
- one clear CTA to enter Mara;
- no asset carousel that makes the product feel like a gallery.

### Meet Mara
Use:
- hero/profile;
- ordinary Life anchor;
- explicit synthetic-character disclosure in product copy;
- character-first positioning.

### /experience
Replace placeholder-feeling identity treatment with approved Mara visual presence.

Voice UI may remain transcript-first until canonical voice is ready, but do not sell browser TTS as Mara voice.

### Public social acquisition
Use platform-safe:
- lifestyle;
- confidence;
- gym/work/café;
- taste;
- selective flirtation where allowed.

Deep adult routing remains first-party.

## Minimum asset QA session

Before public use, test the approved pack together rather than image-by-image.

Ask:

- does this look like one woman across all nine anchors?;
- does she feel aspirational because of taste rather than random luxury?;
- does ordinary Mara still feel attractive?;
- could the same character plausibly speak with V0–V2 voice samples?;
- are work/gym/home/social contexts causally compatible?;
- does any asset accidentally create a new canonical object/location/person?;
- does any asset make Mara feel like a generic adult creator rather than Mara?

Use Character Coherence QA as the governing rubric.

## Asset production economics

OPEX stays approximately zero before evidence.

Prefer:
- tools already available;
- free credits;
- manual curation;
- a small number of excellent outputs;
- reusable reference packs.

Do not subscribe to multiple generation/video/voice tools merely to complete the checklist.

A paid production stack becomes justified when:

1. P0 shows users want Mara;
2. a specific quality bottleneck is identified;
3. the paid tool materially improves that bottleneck;
4. expected launch/retention impact is credible;
5. founder authorizes spend.

## Public soft-launch asset gate

Ready only when:

- identity anchors approved;
- ordinary-life anchors approved;
- no obvious cross-image identity drift;
- visual pack passes World Reality + Character Coherence QA;
- voice is either approved or deliberately deferred/text-first;
- no prototype TTS is represented as production voice;
- all public assets are platform-safe for their channel;
- adult/private assets remain behind proper gates.

## Permanent principles

> **DO NOT LAUNCH A PLACEHOLDER AND CALL IT MARA.**

> **A SMALL COHERENT MARA IS BETTER THAN AN INFINITE INCONSISTENT MARA.**

> **V0–V2 VOICE COHERENCE MATTERS MORE THAN RUSHING TO V3.**

> **QUALITY > MODALITY CHECKLIST.**

> **GENERATIVELY POSSIBLE ≠ CANONICALLY TRUE.**
