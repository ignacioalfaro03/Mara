## 1. Hard gates before taste

A candidate is rejected before subjective ranking if any of these fail.

### G1 — Native Spanish

The output must sound like a woman whose mother tongue is Spanish.

Preferred launch order:

1. native Spanish + believable soft Chilean/South-American variant;
2. native neutral Latin-American Spanish;
3. reject.

A technically impressive voice with English-language phonetics, drifting accent or translated-TTS cadence is not Mara.

### G2 — Clearly adult identity

Target apparent voice age: approximately mid-to-late twenties.

Reject:
- adolescent-coded voice;
- childlike pitch/affect;
- exaggerated anime/girl voice;
- intentionally age-ambiguous output.

### G3 — One woman across V0/V1/V2

The timbre and identity must remain recognizable when moving from ordinary presence to flirt to seductive authority.

If V2 sounds like a different actress, reject.

### G4 — Human presence

Reject if the candidate sounds like:
- audiobook narration;
- call center;
- radio commercial;
- generic assistant;
- robotic TTS;
- permanently breathy porn parody.

### G5 — Rights path

Internal audition is not the same as commercial publication.

Before any audio becomes a canonical/public Mara asset, rights must be explicit for the exact plan/model/voice source and intended use.

No identifiable real-person voice may be cloned or imitated without documented rights and affirmative consent.

### G6 — Adult-use compatibility is a separate gate

Mara's public Alpha voice can remain public-safe V0/V1/V2.

Future highly erotic/explicit commercial voice production must not be assumed permitted merely because a provider allows ordinary TTS or commercial use.

Before that future use, re-check current provider terms and obtain written clarification when the policy is ambiguous.

# 2. Candidate A — ElevenLabs

## Why it is shortlisted

Strongest current low-friction benchmark for:
- highly natural TTS;
- broad emotional range;
- Voice Design from text descriptions;
- pace/tone/accent control;
- multilingual production;
- character-oriented voice workflows.

Current official product documentation says Voice Design can generate unique voices from prompts describing language, age, gender, accent, tone, pacing and speaking style. ElevenLabs recommends explicit language/regional direction and says accent fidelity depends on the voice/source attributes.

Current pricing observed 2026-09-03:
- Free: US$0, 10k credits/month, Voice Design available;
- Starter: US$6/month, adds commercial license and instant voice cloning;
- Creator: US$22/month, promotional first month shown as US$11, adds professional voice cloning.

**Mara action:** use Free only for internal audition. Do not pay yet.

## Audition configuration

Preferred first path:
- Voice Design, not imitation of an existing creator;
- explicitly request native Latin-American Spanish;
- original synthetic adult female voice;
- avoid over-specifying “sexy”;
- generate multiple preview candidates, then run the exact benchmark scripts below.

Conceptual Voice Design direction:

`Native Spanish, Latin American. Adult woman, apparent age 26–30. High natural audio quality. Persona: intelligent, selective, self-possessed. Emotion: warm, subtly playful, confident. Feminine medium register, natural conversational rhythm, controlled warmth, restrained breathiness, believable micro-pauses, calm authority. Sounds like a native speaker talking to one person, never like narration or advertising.`

Do not force a Chilean accent if the output becomes artificial.

## Rights status

- Free plan is suitable for internal testing, not canonical commercial publication.
- Commercial-use path exists on paid plans according to current pricing.
- ElevenLabs' current Prohibited Use Policy explicitly prohibits sexual content involving minors and other harmful/illegal uses; this review does **not** treat absence of a blanket adult-consensual ban as affirmative permission for Mara's future explicit commercial voice.
- Re-verify exact adult-use terms before any explicit paid content.

## Candidate risk

A beautiful English-origin/generated voice can still sound non-native in Spanish.

**Native Spanish is a hard gate, not a bonus.**

# 3. Candidate B — Hume AI Octave

## Why it is shortlisted

Octave is a strong challenger specifically for Mara's Human Presence thesis because its speech-language approach emphasizes:
- expressive delivery;
- character/personality prompting;
- emotional interpretation of the text;
- acting instructions;
- pauses and pace;
- voice design/cloning;
- conversational rather than narration-first speech.

Official Hume material describes Octave as able to generate voices/personality from descriptive prompts and guide emotional delivery. Current API guidance exposes acting instructions including emotional tone, delivery style, intimate-conversation context, speed and trailing silence.

Spanish is supported; the audition must still prove native quality rather than trusting the language label.

Current pricing observed 2026-09-03:
- Free: US$0, 10,000 TTS characters (~10 min), unlimited voice cloning shown as create/use;
- Starter: US$3/month, 30,000 characters;
- Creator: US$14/month, current page shows first month US$7, 140,000 characters.

Hume's current Terms say Free and Starter are non-commercial; Creator and above may be used commercially subject to policies.

**Mara action:** Free only for internal audition. Do not pay yet.

## Audition configuration

Use original voice/personality prompting where available.

Direction:

`Mujer adulta hispanohablante nativa, 26–30 años. Voz femenina natural, cálida y segura. Inteligente, socialmente fluida, selectiva. Conversa con una sola persona; no narra. Ritmo humano con pequeñas pausas, sonrisa audible ocasional y autoridad tranquila. La coquetería aparece en el timing, no en respiración exagerada.`

Use acting instructions only to make the intended V0/V1/V2 distinction; do not rewrite the benchmark text.

## Rights status

# 4. Candidate C — Resemble AI Chatterbox Multilingual V3 / LatAm Spanish

## Why it is shortlisted

This is the strategic zero-subscription hedge.

The official open-source Chatterbox repository currently provides:
- Chatterbox Multilingual V3;
- Spanish support;
- a dedicated **LatAm Spanish** single-language pack;
- voice cloning from reference audio;
- expression controls such as exaggeration / CFG;
- an MIT-licensed open-source code/model distribution.

The dedicated LatAm Spanish pack matters because Mara's rule is native language before forced locality.

## Cost posture

Software/model license: open-source MIT according to the official repository.

That does **not** mean production compute is free.

Lean evaluation order:
1. free demo / existing compute if available;
2. local/internal inference if hardware permits;
3. only consider paid GPU/hosting after it proves materially better or strategically necessary.

Do not buy GPU hosting merely because self-hosting is possible.

## Voice-source rule

Chatterbox requires a legitimate voice source/reference for identity cloning.

Allowed direction:
- a voice we own;
- a licensed actor with explicit synthetic-voice rights;
- another source whose license clearly grants the required commercial derivative/synthetic rights.

Not allowed:
- copying Leina, Belu, Isidora or any other identifiable creator;
- using a random online woman's voice;
- laundering an unlicensed voice through a model and calling it original.

For the first engine audition, a bundled/default/demo reference may be used only to judge Spanish naturalness and expression. It does **not** automatically become Mara's canonical voice.

## Rights status

The open-source project is MIT-licensed according to its official repository.

Commercial/legal safety still depends on:
- rights to the reference voice;
- rights to any additional model/assets used;
- host/infrastructure AUP if not self-hosted;
- applicable law;
- truthful AI/synthetic disclosure where required.

## Candidate risk

Higher operational burden and potentially lower polish than the best managed TTS providers.

It wins only if it gets close enough in Human Presence while giving Mara materially better control, rights clarity and long-term economics.

# 5. Identical audition script

Every candidate receives the **same text**.

Do not rewrite a line to rescue a provider.

Do not compare one provider's best dramatic demo against another provider's default output.

## A — V0 Natural Presence

Text:

> “Hoy tuve un día bastante más largo de lo que esperaba. Igual ya estoy en mi casa, así que sobreviví.”

Target:
- ordinary conversation;
- slight fatigue;
- natural soft finish;
- no seduction performance.

## B — V1 Light Flirt

Text:

> “No sé si debería contestarte eso… aunque igual me dio curiosidad que lo preguntaras así.”

Target:
- subtle smile;
- adult confidence;
- implication through timing;
- restrained breathiness.

## C — V2 Seductive Authority

Text:

> “No. Esa respuesta fue demasiado fácil. Piénsalo un poco más… y después me contestas.”

Target:
- controlled pacing;
- deliberate silence;
- calm authority;
- no aggression;
- no porn caricature.

### Native-language stress test after a candidate passes A/B/C

Use the canonical callback from the launch pack:

> “Ya. Volviste. Y sí, me acuerdo que la otra vez dijiste que ibas a entrenar… así que espero que no haya sido puro discurso.”

This checks whether natural Spanish survives longer phrasing, `ya`, rhythm and teasing continuity.

# 6. Blind scorecard

Remove provider names before founder comparison where practical.

Score 0–3 each:

1. **Native Spanish** — hard gate; below 3 rejects the voice.
2. Human presence.
3. Adult identity.
4. Natural conversational timing.
5. Timbre fit with Mara.
6. V0 credibility.
7. V1 flirt without caricature.
8. V2 authority without aggression.
9. Same identity across V0/V1/V2.
10. Micro-pauses / breath / smile realism.
11. Pronunciation stability.
12. Absence of TTS artifacts.
13. Repeatability across regenerated takes.
14. Rights clarity for public-safe commercial launch.
15. Long-term control / portability.
16. Cost fit before traction.

### Hard rejection overrides total score

Reject even with a high total if:
- Spanish is not genuinely native;
- the voice sounds adolescent-coded;
- V2 becomes porn parody;
- identity changes materially between bands;
- commercial rights are not clear enough for the intended publication;
- the source voice lacks rights/consent.

# 7. Decision rule

Do not choose the voice that sounds hottest in V2.

Choose the voice that makes all three statements believable:

- `Mara puede hablar de un día normal y quiero seguir escuchando.`
- `Mara puede coquetear sin sonar actuada.`
- `Mara puede dar una instrucción con calma y se siente más dominante sin subir el volumen.`

The winner must first own **V0**.

If no candidate passes, ship text-first and continue searching.
