# Mara Vera — Launch Voice Production Pack v1.0

## Purpose

Convert the Voice Bible into a minimum executable launch pack for Mara's first canonical voice.

Authoritative dependency:
- `docs/character/voice-bible.md`

Permanent rule:

> THE PROVIDER IS REPLACEABLE. MARA'S VOICE IDENTITY IS NOT.

The objective is not to generate a lot of audio. The objective is to freeze a recognizable adult voice and prove it survives ordinary life, flirt, authority, callbacks and short social content without sounding like TTS.

---

## 1. Launch voice milestone

Produce and approve **8 canonical voice assets** before expanding the library.

They must cover:
- V0 natural presence;
- V1 playful / flirty;
- V2 seductive authority;
- ordinary Life State;
- return / callback;
- short initiative;
- social / reel delivery;
- no-sale continuity.

V3 high-intensity intimate performance is **not required for launch**.

---

## 2. Voice identity lock

Once a candidate wins, freeze:

- apparent adult age;
- timbre family;
- pitch range;
- base speaking rate;
- Chilean / South-American accent intensity;
- vowel / consonant behavior;
- laugh style;
- pause style;
- breathiness ceiling;
- smile-in-voice level;
- teasing rhythm;
- dominant rhythm;
- default warmth;
- emotional restraint.

These become identity constraints.

Do not let every script produce a different Mara.

---

## 3. File naming

Use:

`mara_voice_launch_[nn]_[band]_[context]_v[version]`

Examples:
- `mara_voice_launch_01_v0_intro_v1.wav`
- `mara_voice_launch_05_v1_callback_v1.wav`
- `mara_voice_launch_08_v2_authority_v1.wav`

Keep rejected provider outputs outside canonical public directories.

## 4. The 8 canonical launch voice assets

### 01 — V0 identity anchor

**Purpose:** establish Mara's everyday speaking voice.

Script:

> “Hoy tuve un día bastante más largo de lo que esperaba. Igual ya estoy en mi casa, así que sobreviví.”

Performance:
- conversational;
- slight fatigue but not flat;
- soft natural smile at the end;
- no theatrical seduction;
- 7–11 seconds.

Primary use:
- voice identity reference;
- Meet Mara;
- internal provider benchmark.

---

### 02 — V0 short spontaneous reaction

Script:

> “¿Qué? ¿En serio? Ya, esa no me la esperaba.”

Performance:
- genuine immediate surprise;
- short first reaction;
- natural pitch change;
- no radio-announcer delivery.

Purpose:
- prove Mara can react rather than narrate.

---

### 03 — V1 playful Mara

Script:

> “Jajaja, ya… no te creo nada. Pero sigue, quiero ver hasta dónde llegas con esa historia.”

Performance:
- audible restrained laugh;
- playful disbelief;
- small pause after “ya”;
- confident, not eager.

Purpose:
- establish the core flirt/play rhythm.

---

### 04 — V1 light flirt

Script:

> “No sé si debería contestarte eso… aunque igual me dio curiosidad que lo preguntaras así.”

Performance:
- slightly slower;
- subtle smile;
- implication rather than exaggerated breathiness;
- adult and self-possessed.

Purpose:
- prove attractive voice without generic porn-TTS behavior.

---

### 05 — Return / memory callback

Script:

> “Ya. Volviste. Y sí, me acuerdo que la otra vez dijiste que ibas a entrenar… así que espero que no haya sido puro discurso.”

Performance:
- recognition in tone;
- lightly teasing;
- no “database memory” emphasis;
- natural callback.

Primary use:
- `/experience` return flow;
- owned web.

---

### 06 — Life State initiative

Script:

> “Te iba a decir algo antes y se me fue. Al final sí fui al gym… aunque estuve a punto de inventarme una excusa ridícula para no ir.”

Performance:
- sounds like Mara initiated because she remembered something;
- small self-correction / imperfect spoken rhythm;
- ordinary-life realism.

Purpose:
- prove Human Presence outside direct response mode.

---

### 07 — No-sale continuity

Script:

> “Ya, déjalo. No tienes que comprar nada ahora. Igual quería seguir hablando contigo de esto.”

Performance:
- calm;
- zero disappointment;
- zero manipulation;
- relationship tone unchanged.

Purpose:
- commercially important proof that Mara remains Mara when no sale happens.

---

### 08 — V2 seductive authority

Script:

> “No. Esa respuesta fue demasiado fácil. Piénsalo un poco más… y después me contestas.”

Performance:
- controlled;
- deliberate pauses;
- quiet authority;
- seductive through timing, not breathy acting;
- no aggression.

Purpose:
- define upper launch intensity without jumping to V3.

---

## 5. Social voice derivatives

Once the 8 anchors are approved, derive:

- 3–5 second story reactions;
- 6–10 second reel hooks;
- 10–20 second first-person day updates;
- one short gym update;
- one night-out open loop;
- one “I have a theory about you” CTA;
- one no-CTA ordinary-life voice note.

Do not generate 50 clips before the identity is frozen.

---

## 6. Web integration mapping

Current Web Alpha already exposes:

`NEXT_PUBLIC_MARA_VOICE_URL`

P0 rule:
- start with one approved V0/V1 canonical audio;
- do not dynamically orchestrate dozens of clips yet;
- use text fallback when canonical audio is unavailable;
- never fall back to browser TTS presented as Mara's real voice.

Recommended first web asset:
- 05 Return / memory callback, or
- 06 Life State initiative.

Why:
They demonstrate something text-only competitors struggle to make emotionally convincing: continuity and presence.

## 7. Voice QA scorecard

Score each candidate 0–2 on:

- same Mara identity;
- natural spoken timing;
- adult clarity;
- Spanish naturalness;
- accent believability;
- warmth;
- confidence;
- self-possession;
- emotional fit;
- laugh/breath realism;
- absence of TTS cadence;
- repeatability across scripts;
- rights / provenance clarity;
- production cleanliness.

Maximum = 28.

### Approval threshold
- 25–28: canonical candidate;
- 21–24: revise / limited use;
- <=20: reject.

Hard failures:
- sounds adolescent;
- sounds like an identifiable real person imitation;
- robotic cadence;
- caricatured Chilean accent;
- excessive breathiness as default;
- provider/model rights unclear for intended commercial use;
- identity drifts badly between scripts.

---

## 8. Provider evaluation protocol

Do not choose a provider from one demo.

For each serious candidate provider, run the **same 8 launch scripts**.

Compare:
- consistency;
- naturalness;
- emotion;
- Spanish;
- latency only if relevant;
- controllability;
- export quality;
- commercial rights;
- effective cost.

Blind comparison is preferred when practical.

Provider decision ends in one of:
- KEEP;
- ITERATE;
- KILL;
- NOT ENOUGH DATA.

---

## 9. Lean-cost rule

Before traction:
- free/internal trials first;
- no recurring subscription merely because one provider sounds nicer;
- no realtime voice infrastructure;
- no expensive cloning workflow;
- no multi-provider production stack.

Paid tooling is unlocked only if:
1. the voice materially improves Mara;
2. the free/current workflow is a measured bottleneck;
3. rights are commercially suitable;
4. the cost fits the Traction → Investment Gate.

---

## 10. Rights and provenance record

For every approved canonical sample record:

- provider;
- model / voice ID;
- generation date;
- script ID;
- commercial-use status;
- voice origin;
- whether any custom training occurred;
- restrictions;
- source file hash / archive identifier where practical.

Never clone or imitate an identifiable real person's voice without explicit rights.

---

## 11. Audio-first video workflow

For talking video:

`script → Mara personality pass → approved canonical audio → animation/video → lip sync → QC → publish`

Never let the video generator invent a new Mara voice.

---

## 12. Launch exit criteria

Voice is launch-ready when:

- one V0 anchor passes QA;
- one V1 playful/flirty anchor passes QA;
- one V2 authority anchor passes QA;
- callback continuity sounds natural;
- Life State initiative sounds natural;
- no-sale continuity sounds emotionally stable;
- rights are clear enough for the intended public use;
- the same voice identity survives at least 6 of the 8 launch scripts.

If V2 is not good enough but V0/V1 are strong, launch voice may still proceed with V0/V1 only.

Do not ship a weak V2 just to check a box.

---

## 13. Production order

1. Generate 01 across candidate workflows.
2. Pick 2–3 strongest identity candidates.
3. Run 02, 03 and 05 on those candidates.
4. Kill identities that drift.
5. Run 04, 06, 07 and 08 on the remaining candidates.
6. Freeze one canonical voice.
7. Export clean masters.
8. Connect first approved file to Web Alpha.
9. Produce social derivatives only after the voice is frozen.

The first goal is not maximum seduction.

The first goal is:

> “That sounds like Mara.”
