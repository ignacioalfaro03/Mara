# Mara Vera — First Impression Product Contract

## Why this exists

The first public web impression must reflect the product we are actually building, not a generic AI landing page.

The first 30–60 seconds should make a visitor feel four things:

1. Mara already has a life when they arrive.
2. Mara has taste, opinions and boundaries of her own.
3. The visitor participates through choices and behavior, not questionnaires.
4. If they return, Mara can remember what actually happened.

## Permanent principles

- **BEHAVIOR BEFORE SELF-DESCRIPTION**
- **SITUATIONS BEFORE QUESTIONS**
- **CONSEQUENCES BEFORE LABELS**
- **SEDUCTION REQUIRES CONTRAST**
- **MARA WANTS. MARA NEVER NEEDS.**
- **STORE THE CHOICE. DO NOT INVENT THE PERSON.**
- **PLAY FIRST. REGISTER WHEN CONTINUITY HAS VALUE.**

## Public-safe first impression

The acquisition surface can be provocative without becoming explicit. The public web should sell character, judgment, anticipation, continuity and tension rather than competing with pornography on explicitness.

The user should quickly see examples of the product grammar:

- outfit choice;
- visual/pose preference;
- Mara noticing behavior;
- Mara changing her mind;
- Mara making the user wait;
- callbacks on return;
- optional account-backed memory.

## What the Home is not

- not a chatbot homepage;
- not a feature checklist;
- not a personality quiz;
- not a porn tube;
- not a parasocial dependency pitch;
- not a dashboard-first SaaS shell.

## First-page promise

The page should communicate, mostly through scenes:

> You did not open an assistant. You arrived in the middle of Mara's life.

and then:

> What you choose changes what happens next — and, if you want, what Mara remembers when you come back.

## Safety/data boundary

Public telemetry remains coarse and must not include the user's visual/sexual preference selection. Account-backed preference events may store the literal selected option under RLS, but must not infer or persist sensitive identity labels, loneliness, distress, dependency, arousal or intimate free text.
