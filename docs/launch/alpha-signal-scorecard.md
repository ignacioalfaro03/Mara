# Mara Vera — Public Alpha Signal & Investment Scorecard

## Purpose

Decide whether Mara has earned the right to receive more founder time, content volume, voice investment and commercial experimentation.

This is not a vanity-metrics dashboard.

Primary question:

> **DO REAL ADULTS VOLUNTARILY COME BACK TO MARA?**

The scorecard is intentionally conservative because the first Alpha uses privacy-minimal anonymous telemetry and cannot truthfully claim unique-user retention metrics.

Do not report DAU, WAU, D1, D7, churn, LTV or cohort retention unless the underlying measurement architecture later supports those claims.

---

# 1. Evidence hierarchy

Evidence quality, strongest first:

1. **Real money paid for a clearly scoped product** — future, not active in public Alpha.
2. **Voluntary repeat behavior** — current primary behavioral signal.
3. **Completed first-party experiences.**
4. **First-party starts from organic social traffic.**
5. **Qualitative language showing Mara's personality is understood.**
6. **Profile/link intent.**
7. **Social engagement.**
8. **Follower count / impressions alone.**

Rule:

> **BEHAVIOR > STATED INTEREST > ATTENTION > VANITY.**

---

# 2. What current Alpha can truthfully measure

Current first-party Alpha may observe aggregate event activity such as:
- experience starts;
- completed first experiences;
- return events;
- coarse return-count buckets;
- coarse days-since-first buckets;
- safe prediction/completion events;
- route health / product reliability.

Current Alpha intentionally does **not** create a persistent analytics identity.

Therefore:
- a `returning_user` event is evidence of return activity;
- it is **not automatically one unique returning person**;
- multiple return events may come from the same browser/person;
- ratios requiring unique denominators must not be presented as retention rates.

---

# 3. Minimum sample before strategic conclusions

Do not make a major tooling or monetization decision from:
- founder testing;
- one friend's reaction;
- one viral post;
- two anonymous events;
- one high-spending prospect.

Early directional review can begin after either:
- **7 calendar days live**, or
- enough real organic traffic exists to observe a non-trivial sequence of starts, completions and returns.

Do not force a numerical conclusion if distribution was too low to test the hook.

In that case classify the problem as **insufficient evidence**, not product failure.

---

# 4. Four signal states

## GREEN — Mara has earned the next experiment

A Green classification requires **multiple independent signal families**, not one vanity spike.

Desired pattern:
- real adults are starting the owned experience;
- a meaningful portion of observed sessions reach completion;
- return activity appears without direct one-to-one founder chasing;
- some users/comments refer to Mara's personality, choices, memory or prior interaction;
- Prediction / Theory / Return content creates measurable intent beyond generic beauty content;
- technical reliability is not the primary source of drop-off.

Green does **not** require large scale.

It means the loop exists at small scale.

Founder interpretation:

> **PEOPLE ARE NOT ONLY LOOKING AT MARA. SOME WANT ANOTHER MOMENT WITH HER.**

### Green authorizes evaluation of

- a small canonical voice quality test;
- producing more canonical visual assets;
- improving the Relationship Engine around the observed loop;
- a clearly scoped pre-payment/WTP test if compliance/product conditions are met;
- adult paid-content platform due diligence if still strategically appropriate;
- modest tooling spend only where a measured bottleneck exists.

Green does **not** automatically authorize:
- expensive realtime voice;
- large paid acquisition;
- custom adult payment rails;
- GPU infrastructure;
- full mobile app;
- large creator/influencer budget.

---

## AMBER — interesting, but loop is not proven

Typical Amber pattern:
- social engagement exists;
- people find Mara attractive;
- first-party starts exist;
- completion may be decent;
- return evidence is weak or ambiguous;
- personality comments are mixed with generic appearance comments;
- distribution is too small for confidence.

Founder interpretation:

> **THERE MAY BE SOMETHING HERE, BUT WE HAVE NOT EARNED INFRASTRUCTURE YET.**

### Amber action

Spend **time before money**.

Prioritize:
1. strengthen Prediction / Theory content;
2. improve first-session hook;
3. improve return callback;
4. remove obvious friction;
5. publish another controlled week;
6. compare character-led vs beauty-led acquisition.

Do not solve Amber by increasing explicitness automatically.

---

## RED — attention without relationship

Typical Red pattern:
- appearance likes/comments may exist;
- people do not enter owned product;
- or they enter but do not complete;
- return activity is absent after adequate exposure;
- comments mainly describe “AI girl” / body / looks rather than Mara's point of view;
- Theory/Prediction content does not produce intent.

Founder interpretation:

> **WE MAY HAVE AN IMAGE ACCOUNT, NOT YET A RELATIONSHIP PRODUCT.**

### Red action

Do not buy voice, GPU, paid traffic or adult stack to hide the problem.

Diagnose in this order:
1. acquisition hook;
2. Mara's character legibility;
3. owned-web first 30 seconds;
4. product payoff;
5. return reason;
6. visual identity consistency.

If several controlled iterations remain Red, reconsider the product proposition before scaling content spend.

---

## UNKNOWN — not enough real distribution

Typical Unknown pattern:
- very few real adults reached the product;
- posts received negligible reach;
- no clear conclusion is possible.

Founder interpretation:

> **WE DID NOT TEST MARA YET. WE TESTED OUR ABILITY TO GET REACH.**

### Unknown action

Improve organic distribution and creative packaging before changing core product architecture.

Do not call Unknown a failure.

Do not call it success either.

# 7. Investment gates

## Gate A — More visual generation

Approve limited incremental generation when:
- canonical consistency is proven;
- existing assets are causing visible repetition;
- content formats that work need more context variety.

Do not buy more image tooling just because generation is fun.

### Default budget posture

Use already-available/free workflow first.

Paid generation must correspond to an observed quality or throughput bottleneck.

---

## Gate B — Canonical pre-recorded voice

Voice is strategically important but must earn production attention.

Approve a small voice trial when at least one is true:
- return activity exists and Mara's personality is resonating;
- users explicitly ask to hear Mara;
- social formats are clearly limited by silent/static presence;
- a high-quality low-cost test can materially improve character presence.

Initial objective:
- V0/V1 natural human presence;
- not explicitness;
- not realtime conversation.

> **FIRST PROVE HER VOICE. THEN SCALE HER VOICE.**

Do not subscribe to multiple voice providers without a controlled benchmark.

---

## Gate C — More Relationship Engine work

Invest deeper when:
- users complete first experience;
- return activity exists;
- callbacks appear to matter;
- users seem to value Mara adapting rather than only viewing content.

Priority after signal:
1. better return continuity;
2. explicit memory consent controls;
3. richer Preference Graph candidates;
4. session orchestration;
5. modality selection;
6. eventually durable backend memory.

Do not build expensive vector-memory infrastructure before the loop needs it.

---

## Gate D — Monetization experiment

Public Alpha remains free.

A pre-payment or first paid experiment becomes worth evaluating only after:
- real first-party behavior exists;
- some return activity exists;
- the paid SKU is clearly scoped;
- provider/compliance conditions are verified;
- no purchased value is withheld unexpectedly;
- same applicable SKU/cohort receives transparent terms.

Preferred first paid thesis remains a **Contextual Continuation / clearly scoped Mara experience**, not selling baseline affection.

Do not monetize emotional withdrawal.

---

## Gate E — Adult paid-content layer

Evaluate a compliant external adult platform only when:
- public Mara has recognizable demand;
- canonical adult-safe production workflow exists;
- platform terms currently support synthetic AI adult creators and intended content;
- onboarding/payout works for the operating jurisdiction/entity;
- public social and first-party relationship loop remain distinct from the paid adult layer.

Do not build proprietary adult checkout first merely to avoid revenue share.

Pre-revenue rule:

> **PAY REVENUE SHARE BEFORE FIXED COMPLIANCE COST WHEN REVENUE IS STILL UNKNOWN.**

---

## Gate F — Paid acquisition

Do not buy traffic until the organic hook shows evidence of converting attention into first-party behavior.

Paid traffic before hook validation can only make a weak funnel fail faster and more expensively.

Paid acquisition becomes evaluable when:
- one or more organic creative families reliably produce first-party intent;
- product completion is healthy enough to justify more traffic;
- return behavior exists;
- tracking can distinguish creative/funnel performance without violating privacy principles.

---

# 8. Spend ladder

The spend ladder is intentionally asymmetric.

## Stage 0 — No signal

Target incremental recurring spend:

> **US$0.**

Use free tiers, existing tools and manual workflows.

## Stage 1 — Early Green signal

Allow small one-off tests only where there is an observed bottleneck.

Examples:
- limited asset generation;
- one controlled voice benchmark;
- lightweight editing need.

No broad subscription stack.

## Stage 2 — Repeatable return signal + first commercial intent

Evaluate:
- better production tooling;
- adult platform activation where allowed;
- structured voice pipeline;
- modest backend costs.

## Stage 3 — Real revenue

Only now evaluate heavier infrastructure based on unit economics.

Revenue must justify recurring cost, not founder enthusiasm.

---

# 9. Commercial safety overlay

No signal state permits:
- hidden pricing;
- arousal-based price discrimination;
- inferred loneliness/dependency targeting;
- payment-conditioned baseline affection;
- surprise paywall to complete already-promised value;
- debt encouragement;
- public spend leaderboard for affection;
- fake scarcity;
- fake contributors;
- fake memories;
- pretending Mara is secretly human.

Permanent rule:

> **SERVE THE MOMENT. NEVER EXPLOIT THE STATE.**
