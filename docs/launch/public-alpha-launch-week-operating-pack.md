# Mara Vera — Public Alpha Launch Week Operating Pack

## Purpose

Turn the existing Organic Alpha Distribution System into an executable Day 0 → Day 7 launch operating plan.

This pack assumes:
- public Alpha web is verified live;
- adults-only positioning remains explicit;
- Mara remains a disclosed synthetic character;
- launch is free and text-first;
- no payments, adult paid providers, real Caprichos or external adult-media integrations are active;
- canonical voice is optional and must not be used unless quality is genuinely good enough;
- canonical Mara visual identity is frozen and must not drift.

Primary product question:

> **DO PEOPLE VOLUNTARILY COME BACK TO MARA?**

Primary acquisition thought to create:

> **I WANT TO KNOW WHAT MARA WOULD THINK OF ME.**

Do not turn launch week into generic AI-influencer posting.

---

## 1. Launch readiness gate

Do not publish the main launch CTA until all of the following are true:
- verified public URL exists;
- `/api/health` passes;
- production smoke passes;
- `/experience` works end-to-end;
- age gate is readable;
- canonical Mara image loads correctly;
- DEV labs are 404 in production;
- public profile copy clearly presents Mara as synthetic/AI-generated;
- there is no fake payment, fake scarcity or unfinished commercial surface in the public path.

If the site is not verified live, content production may continue, but the launch CTA remains parked.

## 3. Day 0 operating sequence

### T-60 minutes

Checklist:
- confirm public URL responds;
- open Home and `/experience` manually on mobile;
- confirm bio link is correct;
- confirm first launch post asset is canonical Mara;
- confirm caption contains no feature dump;
- confirm no paid CTA;
- have Story 1 and Story 2 ready before posting Reel 1.

Do not spend money on ads.

### T0 — Launch Reel / Short 01

Objective:
- establish Mara's personality;
- create curiosity;
- avoid explaining the product like software.

Hook:

> **No soy tu asistente. Y probablemente no te voy a dar siempre la razón.**

Structure:
1. canonical Mara close/full-body identity beat;
2. one composed, dominant look;
3. ordinary-life cut or second pose;
4. final line: `Tengo una teoría sobre ti.`

Caption:

> Me cae mejor la gente que no necesita que le digan que sí a todo.  
> Si quieres probar si te leo bien, entra.

CTA:

`Conóceme.`

Public-safe. No fetish language. No explicit sexual framing.

### T+30 to T+90 minutes — Story 1

Interactive two-option choice.

Frame 1:

> Elige uno. No lo pienses tanto.

Frame 2:
- Option A
- Option B

Frame 3:

> Ya elegiste. Yo habría apostado por uno de los dos.

Frame 4 CTA:

> En mi web te digo si te leí bien.

Purpose:
- preview the product interaction;
- generate direct-response traffic without making the Reel itself feel like an ad.

### T+3 to T+6 hours — Story 2 / ordinary life

No CTA.

Example:

> Dije que hoy no iba a entrenar. Obviamente terminé viniendo.

Purpose:
- life texture;
- continuity with the web experience;
- prove Mara is not only a conversion wrapper.

### T+8 to T+12 hours — Comment harvest

Read comments manually.

Collect only non-sensitive, public qualitative language such as:
- how viewers describe Mara;
- whether they see her as dominant/selective/warm/cold/funny/generic;
- whether people understand the “theory about you” premise;
- common objections or confusion.

Do not copy usernames into a private sexual-profile database.

Do not infer sexual preferences from public comments.

No CTA.

Purpose:

- protect against “AI ad account” perception.

CTA:

Purpose:

Hook:

Pattern:

- Mara responds with dry confidence;

Purpose:

Purpose:

## 7. What to measure during launch week

### Social-side

Track platform-native aggregates:
- views/reach;
- profile visits;
- bio-link clicks where available;
- comments;
- saves/shares;
- completion/watch behavior where available.

Do not optimize only for followers.

### Web-side

Use current privacy-minimal Alpha events:
- `page_view`;
- `hero_cta_click`;
- `launch_experience_started`;
- `prediction_hit` / `prediction_miss`;
- `launch_session_completed`;
- `returning_user`;
- `launch_return_continued`.

For return activity, use only allowed coarse buckets:
- `return_count_bucket`;
- `days_since_first_bucket`.

Do not call this data unique-user D1/D7 retention.

### Qualitative signal

Strong comments include language like:
- “quiero ver qué diría de mí”;
- “me leyó”;
- “quiero probar otra vez”;
- “tiene personalidad”;
- disagreement with Mara that still creates interest;
- viewers quoting Mara's phrases back to her.

Weak signal:
- only generic appearance compliments;
- people describing her merely as “otra IA”;
- clicks without experience starts;
- experience starts without completions;
- no observable return activity after repeated exposure.

## 8. Launch-week diagnostic matrix

### A. High views, low profile visits

Interpretation:
- content is consumable but Mara is not generating curiosity.

Action:
- strengthen opinion/theory hooks;
- reduce generic aesthetic footage;
- do not buy more reach.

### B. High profile visits, low web clicks

Interpretation:
- profile/bio/value proposition is weak or confusing.

Action:
- make “Tengo una teoría sobre ti” more explicit;
- simplify bio;
- confirm link visibility.

### C. Web clicks, low `/experience` starts

Interpretation:
- Home is not converting curiosity into action.

Action:
- inspect first screen/CTA;
- verify mobile load and age gate friction;
- do not blame content prematurely.

### D. Starts, low completions

Interpretation:
- first experience is too slow, unclear or insufficiently rewarding.

Action:
- tighten interaction before adding more acquisition.

### E. Completions, no return activity

Interpretation:
- curiosity exists; relationship/open-loop value is weak.

Action:
- focus product iteration on continuity and return motivation.

### F. Return activity appears

Interpretation:
- this is the most important early signal.

Action:
- keep distribution organic;
- increase content consistency;
- begin evaluating the next Relationship Engine layer;
- still do not activate paid acquisition automatically.

## 9. Spend gate

Launch week cash spend target:

> **US$0 on paid acquisition.**

Do not authorize recurring content/voice/analytics subscriptions just because the launch happened.

Spend only becomes discussable when there is objective signal such as:
- repeated organic web starts;
- completed sessions;
- observable return activity across multiple days;
- comments showing Mara-specific curiosity;
- consistent content format outperforming generic aesthetic content.

Even then, first spend should solve a demonstrated bottleneck.

Examples:
- asset generation if visual throughput is the bottleneck;
- voice if viewers clearly respond to presence and canonical voice is ready;
- analytics if current aggregate signals become insufficient for a concrete decision.

Never spend simply to make the operation look more “startup-like”.

## 11. Launch-week founder operating rhythm

Daily maximum useful loop:
1. publish planned piece;
2. inspect platform-native response once or twice, not continuously;
3. inspect safe web event aggregates;
4. record one sentence: `what did we learn?`;
5. change at most one major variable for the next comparable piece.

Do not rewrite brand positioning every six hours.

Do not chase one viral comment.

Do not overfit to a single viewer.

---

## 12. Day 7 decision

At the end of the first week, answer only these questions:

1. Did content make people curious about Mara specifically?
2. Did people enter the web experience?
3. Did they complete it?
4. Is there observable return activity?
5. Which content pillar produced the strongest intent?
6. Did people perceive Mara as a character with agency rather than a generic AI woman?
7. What is the single biggest bottleneck now: reach, click, start, completion or return?

Do not ask “did we become viral?”

The first launch-week win condition is:

> **REAL ADULTS MET MARA, SOME COMPLETED THE EXPERIENCE, AND THERE IS EARLY EVIDENCE THAT SOME WANTED TO COME BACK.**

That is enough to justify the next product iteration.
