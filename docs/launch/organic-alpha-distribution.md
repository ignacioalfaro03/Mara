# Mara Vera — Organic Alpha Distribution System

## Purpose

Get real adults to meet Mara with near-zero OPEX while preserving character quality, channel safety and first-party conversion.

This is not a paid-acquisition plan.

The goal is to create enough curiosity that a person chooses to visit Mara's owned web and return voluntarily.

## Funnel

`public-safe content → Mara profile → first-party web → /experience → return`

Do not make every piece of content sell.

Mara should feel like a person with a life and a point of view before she feels like a funnel.

## Core public thesis

Public acquisition should make the viewer think:

> **I WANT TO KNOW WHAT MARA WOULD THINK OF ME.**

Not:

> **I FOUND ANOTHER AI TOOL.**

And not:

> **THIS ACCOUNT ONLY EXISTS TO SELL ADULT CONTENT.**

## Four content pillars

### 1. Mara Has Opinions

Short, decisive takes that reveal taste and standards.

Examples:
- something Mara would never wear;
- a trend she thinks is overrated;
- what makes someone instantly more attractive to her;
- a song everyone loves that she would skip;
- a restaurant/order she would choose without asking the group;
- "no, I would not let you pick that for me."

Purpose:
- character recognition;
- authority;
- comment bait without fake controversy;
- make Mara feel non-generic.

### 2. Mara Has a Life

Ordinary texture:
- workday;
- gym;
- café;
- outfit decision;
- getting ready;
- friends;
- food;
- small purchase;
- deciding not to buy something;
- walking home;
- doing nothing interesting.

Purpose:
- diegetic presence;
- realism;
- future callback material;
- make commercial moments rarer and more meaningful.

Rule:

> **LIFE FIRST. CTA SOMETIMES.**

### 3. Mara Has a Theory About You

This is the strongest direct bridge to the product.

Formats:
- two-option prediction;
- "I can probably tell something about you from this";
- visual A/B;
- "choose one and I will tell you what I think";
- "you think you want X, but I think you would choose Y";
- small playful challenge;
- response to audience comments.

CTA when appropriate:

> **Let Mara try on you.**

→ first-party `/experience`.

### 4. Mara Chooses

Mara makes a decision instead of polling endlessly.

Examples:
- chooses outfit;
- chooses meal;
- chooses between two plans;
- refuses an option;
- changes her mind;
- says no to a suggestion;
- chooses what she wants to know about the audience.

Purpose:
- reinforce Mara-led experience;
- preview agency;
- differentiate from obedient assistant UX.

## Initial ten-piece backlog

The first batch should be executable from one visual shoot/generation session plus simple editing.

### Piece 01 — Introduction

Hook:

`No soy tu asistente. Y probablemente no te voy a dar siempre la razón.`

Beat:
- face / full-body identity;
- quick Mara attitude;
- no feature explanation.

CTA:
`Conóceme.`

### Piece 02 — Mara Predicts

Hook:

`Elige uno. Creo que ya sé cuál vas a elegir.`

Two visually clear choices.

Second frame/reel beat:
Mara gives her prediction.

CTA:
`Tengo otra teoría en mi web.`

### Piece 03 — Ordinary Gym Day

Hook:

`Dije que hoy no iba a entrenar. Obviamente terminé viniendo.`

Purpose:
- mirror public launch Life State;
- create continuity with `/experience`.

No CTA required.

### Piece 04 — Taste / Refusal

Hook:

`No. Ese no me lo pondría aunque me lo regalaras.`

Show two options; Mara rejects one.

Purpose:
- agency;
- standards;
- selective character.

### Piece 05 — Coffee / Workday

A mundane moment with one sharp Mara line.

Example:
`Tengo 14 mensajes atrasados y cero intención de contestarlos en orden.`

No CTA.

### Piece 06 — The Theory

Hook:

`La gente que elige esto casi siempre dice que quiere algo tranquilo. No les creo.`

CTA:
`Prueba si te leo bien.`

### Piece 07 — Going Out

Mara deciding what to wear / where to go.

She chooses; audience can disagree but does not control her.

Purpose:
- lifestyle;
- aspirational presence;
- social graph future surface.

### Piece 08 — Voice Tease

Only after canonical voice is good enough.

Very short V0/V1 note.

Not overtly sexual.

Purpose:
- demonstrate presence;
- move viewers toward first-party experience.

If voice quality is not ready, do not publish this piece.

### Piece 09 — Mara Says No

Hook:

`No porque puedas elegir significa que te voy a dejar elegir todo.`

Purpose:
- Mara Leads;
- authority;
- direct product thesis.

CTA:
`Entra y vemos cuánto te dejo decidir.`

### Piece 10 — Return

Hook:

`Me acuerdo de lo que elegiste la última vez.`

This must only reference a truthful product capability.

CTA:
`Vuelve.`

## Asset efficiency

One canonical production batch should try to generate enough material for multiple posts.

Minimum efficient batch:
- 3 looks;
- 2 environments;
- close portrait;
- full-body;
- seated/café/work beat;
- gym/athleisure beat;
- evening look;
- 5–10 short neutral motion clips;
- optional 3 voice pieces when canonical voice exists.

Do not create 50 mediocre assets.

Prefer:

> **10 RECOGNIZABLE MARA ASSETS > 100 GENERIC AI WOMAN ASSETS.**

## Cadence

Initial practical cadence:
- 4–5 short-form pieces per week;
- 2–3 still/carousel posts per week where useful;
- lightweight stories/updates from Mara's day when assets exist;
- no requirement to publish commercially every day.

Founder may batch-create and schedule, but strategy and creative direction remain first-party.

## CTA budget

Do not attach a hard CTA to every post.

Suggested early mix:
- ~50% pure Mara / life / taste;
- ~30% interactive prediction / choice;
- ~20% direct `Enter Mara` conversion content.

This is a starting operating hypothesis, not a validated optimum.

## What to measure first

Before paid acquisition, observe:
- profile visits;
- link clicks;
- `/experience` starts;
- first-session completions where measurable;
- voluntary returns;
- comments that show people understand Mara's personality;
- repeated phrases users use to describe her;
- whether prediction/choice content produces more intent than generic aesthetic content.

Do not optimize for follower count alone.

## Acquisition anti-patterns

Avoid:
- generic AI influencer glamour with no character;
- fake luxury;
- fake DMs;
- fake users/whales;
- pretending Mara is a hidden human;
- making every post sexual;
- making every post a CTA;
- copying benchmark creators' exact scripts/content;
- public `paypig` targeting as the core brand;
- fetish labels in contexts where channel policy or audience fit makes them inappropriate;
- buying followers;
- mass-spam comments/DMs;
- paid acquisition before we know the organic hook.

## First-party conversion principle

Public social should create curiosity.

Owned web should deliver adaptation and continuity.

> **SOCIAL SHOWS MARA. THE WEB LETS MARA START KNOWING YOU.**

## First milestone

The first meaningful acquisition milestone is not a follower number.

It is:

> **A SMALL GROUP OF REAL ADULTS WHO VOLUNTARILY VISIT MARA AND SOME OF THEM COME BACK.**

Only after that signal should the team decide whether to invest in more content automation, voice infrastructure, analytics, paid tools or monetization activation.
