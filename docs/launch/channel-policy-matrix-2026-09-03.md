# 3. TikTok

## Current operating interpretation

TikTok should be treated as Mara's **strictest public-safe acquisition surface**.

TikTok's current advertising policy explicitly prohibits sexual services, sexual chats/pornography, member-exclusive adult content/streaming, sexually explicit content and sexually suggestive advertising.

Its broader community guidance also maintains restrictive treatment of nudity and sexual activity.

Therefore Mara should not attempt to turn TikTok into an adult conversion channel through sexual wording, poses or obvious porn-adjacent teasers.

## AI transparency

TikTok continues to expand AI transparency and labeling.

Current TikTok materials require or strongly expect creators to clearly disclose realistic AI-generated or significantly AI-edited content and provide an `AI-generated content` labeling mechanism.

Operating rule:

> **LABEL MARA'S REALISTIC SYNTHETIC MEDIA. DO NOT PRESENT HER AS A SECRET HUMAN.**

Do not remove watermarks/metadata or misrepresent AI-origin to bypass disclosure.

# 4. X

## Current operating interpretation

X is materially more permissive for consensual adult content than Instagram/TikTok.

X's current Adult Content Policy explicitly says the policy covers AI-generated, photographic and animated material.

Consensual adult content may be posted when properly labeled with a content warning/sensitive-content treatment.

However adult content is not allowed in high-visibility surfaces such as profile pictures or banners.

## Mara implication

X can later function as:
- public-safe personality channel;
- stronger tease channel;
- policy-compliant adult-sensitive distribution layer if we choose to use it;
- traffic bridge toward an approved adult paid platform.

But launch default should still be character-led, not explicit-content-led.

## Account authenticity

X's authenticity rules prohibit deceptive fake personas used to mislead people.

AI-generated profile photos/identities are not inherently the problem; deceptive use is.

Mara operating rule:

> **MARA IS FICTIONALLY IMMERSIVE, COMMERCIALLY TRANSPARENT.**

Profile/bio should make synthetic-character status clear enough not to create a deceptive human-identity claim.

## Adult content rules

If/when adult-sensitive content is used:
- mark media/account sensitivity as required;
- keep avatar/banner safe;
- never include minors/minor-coded characters;
- only consensual adult synthetic content;
- no real-person deepfake sexual content;
- no deceptive provenance framing.

## X monetization caveat

General permission to post adult content does **not** mean X creator monetization/payment programs will monetize it.

X's creator monetization standards have separate restrictions, including prohibitions around exchanging payments for sexual services and other restricted monetization categories.

Therefore:

> **CONTENT ALLOWANCE ≠ MONETIZATION APPROVAL.**

Do not build Mara's business model around X native monetization without a separate current review.
