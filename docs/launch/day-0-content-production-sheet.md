# Mara Vera — Day 0 Content Production Sheet

## Purpose

This is the final production sheet for Mara's first public-Alpha launch day.

It converts the Launch Week Operating Pack into exact publishable beats.

Dependencies:
- `docs/launch/public-alpha-launch-week-operating-pack.md`
- `docs/production/launch-visual-production-pack.md`
- `docs/character/visual-bible.md`
- `docs/character/canonical-visual-reference.md`

Do not publish the main CTA until the verified public Alpha URL exists and production smoke passes.

No paid acquisition on Day 0.

---

## 1. Day 0 objective

The viewer should not leave thinking:

> "Otra influencer IA."

The viewer should leave thinking:

> **"Quiero saber qué pensaría Mara de mí."**

Day 0 is not a feature announcement.

Day 0 introduces a woman with:
- a point of view;
- standards;
- a life;
- enough curiosity about you to make you enter her world.

## 9. Day 0 measurement checklist

### Social

Record platform-native aggregates at sensible checkpoints, not every five minutes:
- Reel views/reach;
- profile visits;
- link clicks where available;
- Story views;
- poll participation;
- shares/saves;
- comments.

Suggested checkpoints:
- around T+2h;
- around T+8–12h;
- next day.

### Web

Observe safe Alpha events:
- `page_view`;
- `hero_cta_click`;
- `launch_experience_started`;
- `prediction_hit` / `prediction_miss`;
- `launch_session_completed`;
- `returning_user`;
- `launch_return_continued`.

Do not claim unique-user retention from anonymous aggregates.

### One-line learning log

At end of Day 0 write exactly one primary conclusion:

`What did Day 0 teach us about the strongest bottleneck: curiosity → click → start → completion → return?`

Do not redesign everything from one metric.

## 10. Day 0 success hierarchy

Best signal:
1. people voluntarily return to Mara;
2. people complete `/experience`;
3. people start `/experience`;
4. people click from social to web;
5. people visit the profile;
6. views/followers.

Follower growth alone is not launch validation.

A small number of real people completing and returning is more strategically useful than a large passive view count.

## 11. Production fallback if no canonical motion exists

Do not block Reel 01 on video generation.

A valid first Reel can be built from 2–4 approved still images using:
- slow crop/zoom;
- subtle motion;
- typography;
- music;
- clean cuts.

Avoid:
- excessive AI animation that changes Mara's face;
- lip-sync before canonical voice exists;
- motion interpolation that distorts body/eyes/hands.

Still-image consistency is preferable to unstable motion.

## 12. Publish gate

Reel 01 may go live only when:
- verified public URL exists;
- link in bio points to it;
- Home and `/experience` pass production smoke;
- canonical visual is present;
- caption disclosure is correct;
- no paid checkout is active;
- Story sequence 01 is ready;
- at least one ordinary-life Story is ready;
- no asset identity drift remains.

Then publish.

No extra launch ceremony is required.
