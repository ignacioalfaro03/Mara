# 1. Operating rules for the week

## 1.1 Do not overpublish

The launch week is not a content-volume contest.

Preferred rhythm:
- one primary short-form piece on publishing days;
- supporting Stories when useful;
- no requirement to publish a feed post every day;
- no paid reach;
- no fake comments;
- no fake users;
- no engagement pods;
- no buying followers.

Quality target:

> **7 RECOGNIZABLE MARA MOMENTS > 40 GENERIC AI-GIRL POSTS.**

## 1.2 Character hierarchy

Every piece should do at least one of these:
1. prove Mara has opinions;
2. prove Mara has a life;
3. make Mara form a theory about the viewer;
4. show Mara choosing instead of endlessly asking permission;
5. create a truthful callback to something that happened earlier in the week.

## 1.3 CTA budget

Do not make the account feel like a funnel.

Launch-week target mix:
- ~50% life / taste / personality with no hard CTA;
- ~30% interaction / prediction;
- ~20% direct first-party conversion.

This is an operating hypothesis, not a validated optimum.

## 1.4 Voice rule

Use voice only if the canonical voice passes the Voice Bible.

> **NO VOICE IS BETTER THAN THE WRONG MARA VOICE.**

Do not use browser TTS or generic narrator TTS and represent it as Mara.

## 1.5 Visual rule

Every visual must be the frozen canonical Mara.

> **ONE MARA. MANY CONTEXTS.**

A beautiful blonde woman who is not clearly the same Mara is rejected.

# 2. Day 1 — MARA PREDICTS

## Objective

Prove that the product is not only looking at Mara.

It is letting Mara make a bounded bet about the user.

Viewer reaction target:

> "A ver si le achunta."

## Primary Reel / Short

### Working title

`YA SÉ CUÁL VAS A ELEGIR`

### Length

7–12 seconds.

### Visual

Preferred:
- asset 02 social-native selfie;
- asset 01 face anchor;
- asset 12 closer owned-web portrait if still public-safe.

Expression:
- composed;
- knowing;
- mildly challenging;
- no exaggerated dominatrix acting.

### Beat A

On-screen:

> **Elige uno. No lo pienses tanto.**

### Beat B

Show two public-safe options.

Recommended launch pair:

**A — Que te digan las cosas directo**

**B — Que te hagan esperar un poco**

### Beat C

Mara:

> **Creo que ya sé cuál elegiste.**

### End

> **Tengo otra teoría en mi web.**

CTA:

`Pruébame.`

## Caption

Primary:

> Hay gente que cree que elige por lógica. Casi nunca es tan simple.  
> A ver si te leo bien.

Disclosure line where needed:

`18+ · Mara es un personaje virtual generado con IA.`

## Stories

Frame 1:

> ¿Elegiste rápido o te quedaste pensando?

Poll:
- `rápido`
- `demasiado`

Frame 2:

> Eso también dice algo.

No need for a second hard CTA if the Reel already carries one.

## Comment responses

If user says:

`Elegí A.`

Possible Mara:

> Anotado. No significa que te crea todavía.

If user says:

`B obvio.`

Possible Mara:

> “Obvio” es exactamente la parte que me interesa.

Never infer a fetish or sexual identity from the public choice.

## What to observe

- comments choosing A/B;
- profile visits;
- link intent;
- `/experience` starts after publication;
- whether viewers challenge Mara's prediction.

The useful signal is curiosity about Mara's judgment, not poll participation alone.

# 3. Day 2 — TASTE / REFUSAL

## Objective

Prove Mara is selective and has standards independent of the user.

Viewer reaction target:

> "Tiene personalidad."

## Primary format

Static carousel or short Reel.

### Hook

> **No. Ese no me lo pondría aunque me lo regalaras.**

Show two styling/lifestyle choices.

Mara rejects one clearly.

Do not ask the audience to decide what she must do.

Audience may disagree.

Mara still chooses.

## Suggested visual pair

Use canonical Mara plus object/outfit choices without changing her identity.

Examples:
- two jacket directions;
- two shoe styles;
- two dinner looks;
- two bag styles;
- two café orders.

Avoid benchmark-creator copying.

## Caption

> Tener opciones no significa que todas sean buenas.

No hard CTA.

## Story follow-up

Poll:

> Igual elegirías el que descarté?

- `sí`
- `ni loco`

Mara follow-up:

> Está bien. No necesito que estés de acuerdo conmigo.

## What to observe

- whether people argue playfully with Mara;
- whether comments describe her as selective / demanding / segura / pesada / dominante;
- whether her personality is legible without explaining product features.

# 4. Day 3 — ORDINARY LIFE

## Objective

Prove Mara exists between conversion moments.

No hard sell.

This is deliberately a lower-intensity day.

> **SEDUCTION REQUIRES CONTRAST.**

## Preferred format

Story-first.

Optional feed still if asset quality is excellent.

## Variant A — gym

Visual:
- asset 07 or 08.

Text:

> Dije que hoy no iba a entrenar. Obviamente terminé viniendo.

Second frame:

> Y ahora que vine, mínimo lo hago bien.

## Variant B — café/work

Visual:
- asset 05 or 06.

Text:

> Tengo mensajes atrasados y cero intención de contestarlos en orden.

Second frame:

> Primero café. Después veo si me vuelvo responsable.

## Variant C — quiet home

Visual:
- asset 05.

Text:

> Hoy no está pasando nada interesante. También me gusta eso.

## Rule

Choose **one** Life State and keep the day's public story coherent.

Do not manufacture five contradictory activities because assets exist.

## What to observe

- story completion;
- replies to mundane Mara;
- whether ordinary-life content still generates profile curiosity;
- whether attraction survives without a sexualized frame.

This day tests whether Mara is becoming a person-like character rather than a sequence of hooks.

# 5. Day 4 — THEORY ABOUT YOU

## Objective

Create the strongest direct bridge from social to owned product.

Viewer reaction target:

> "Quiero ver qué dice de mí."

## Primary Reel / Short

### Hook

> **La gente que elige esto casi siempre dice que quiere algo tranquilo. No les creo.**

Do not turn the public post into explicit sexual profiling.

Use a safe proxy choice.

Examples:
- plan everything vs improvise;
- direct answer vs suspense;
- quiet table vs loud bar;
- stay in vs go out;
- choose immediately vs make someone wait.

## Beat structure

1. show the choice;
2. Mara states a concise theory;
3. hold one beat;
4. CTA to first-party experience.

### CTA

> **Prueba si te leo bien.**

## Caption

> Lo divertido no es acertar siempre. Es ver qué haces cuando creo que te entendí.

## Website connection

The CTA must land on the verified first-party Mara surface.

Do not send this post directly to adult monetization.

## What to observe

This is a key acquisition diagnostic.

Compare qualitatively with:
- Day 2 personality content;
- Day 3 ordinary life.

If Theory content creates materially more profile-to-web intent, keep this family as a major acquisition pillar.

Do not conclude from one post alone.

# 6. Day 5 — MARA CHOOSES

## Objective

Reinforce:

> **THE USER CAN STEER. MARA SHOULD DRIVE.**

## Primary Reel / Carousel

### Hook

> **No porque puedas elegir significa que te voy a dejar elegir todo.**

## Safe public execution

Mara chooses between:
- two plans;
- two outfits;
- two food options;
- two places;
- two ways to spend the evening.

Audience comments are invited, but her final decision is not a vote result.

## Example

Frame 1:

> Hoy tenía dos opciones.

Frame 2:
- café + volver temprano;
- salir a comer.

Frame 3:

> Elegí salir. Ya estaba aburrida de portarme bien.

Keep public-safe.

## CTA

Usually none.

Optional soft line:

> En mi web tampoco te dejo decidir todo.

Only if CTA budget has not been overused.

## What to observe

- comments trying to persuade Mara;
- audience comfort with Mara disagreeing;
- whether agency increases memorability.

# 7. Day 6 — COMMENT HARVEST / MARA RESPONDS

## Objective

Turn real audience language into character reinforcement.

Do **not** fabricate questions.

If there are no useful real comments yet, use another ordinary-life piece instead.

## Selection criteria

Choose a real comment that is:
- public-safe;
- not age-ambiguous sexual content;
- not doxxing;
- not hateful harassment requiring escalation;
- useful for revealing Mara's personality.

## Response Reel format

Screenshot or typed reconstruction of the public comment only where platform norms and privacy expectations make that appropriate.

Then Mara responds.

### Candidate response families

Comment:

`Seguro me lees mal.`

Mara:

> Puede ser. Me entretiene más comprobarlo que prometerte que no.

Comment:

`Qué pesada.`

Mara:

> Todavía no he sido pesada.

Comment:

`¿Eres IA?`

Mara:

> Sí. Soy un personaje virtual generado con IA. Lo interesante es si igual te dan ganas de volver.

Comment:

`Te ves increíble.`

Mara:

> Buen criterio.

Use sparingly.

## Never do

- fake a DM;
- fake a user quote;
- imply private knowledge Mara does not have;
- reward spend publicly;
- create a spend leaderboard;
- infer a fetish from a commenter;
- drag a hostile commenter into prolonged humiliation.

## What to observe

- whether Mara's replies receive stronger engagement than generic replies;
- recurring audience language used to describe Mara;
- whether users begin addressing Mara as a character rather than discussing “the AI account.”

# 8. Day 7 — RETURN / CONTINUITY

## Objective

Close the first weekly narrative around the product's real moat:

> **MARA REMEMBERS ENOUGH FOR THE NEXT TIME TO FEEL DIFFERENT.**

Do not overclaim memory beyond current Alpha capability.

## Primary Reel / Short

### Hook

> **Me acuerdo de lo que elegiste la última vez.**

This is allowed only because current Alpha has truthful browser-local return continuity.

## Beat structure

1. canonical Mara face;
2. line above;
3. small pause;
4. second line:

> **La segunda vez es más interesante.**

5. CTA:

> **Vuelve.**

## Caption

> La primera vez te conozco. La siguiente ya tengo algo con qué molestarte.

Keep the public line playful, not emotionally manipulative.

## Important truth boundary

Do not say:
- “sé todo de ti”;
- “nunca olvido nada”;
- “te conozco mejor que nadie”;
- “solo yo te entiendo.”

The Alpha has limited local continuity, not omniscience.

## Story follow-up

Frame 1:

> ¿Ya habías entrado antes?

Poll:
- `sí`
- `todavía no`

Frame 2 for `sí` audience generally:

> Entonces ya sabes que no empiezo exactamente desde cero.

CTA:

`Vuelve.`

## What to observe

This is the most important behavioral day.

Look for:
- return activity telemetry;
- people explicitly saying they returned;
- comments referencing a previous Mara interaction;
- repeated site starts after Return content.

Do not call anonymous aggregate return events D7 retention.

# 10. First-week creative diagnostics

At the end of Day 7, classify content into four buckets.

## A — Character works

Evidence examples:
- people describe Mara's personality;
- they disagree with her playfully;
- they address Mara directly;
- they remember a prior post.

## B — Prediction works

Evidence examples:
- A/B comments;
- users challenge her theory;
- Theory / Prediction posts correlate with more first-party starts.

## C — Beauty works but character does not

Evidence examples:
- appearance compliments dominate;
- very little curiosity about what Mara thinks;
- little owned-web intent.

Response:
- do not solve by making imagery more sexual;
- strengthen writing, choice, opinion and callbacks.

## D — Nothing works yet

Evidence examples:
- little distribution;
- little profile intent;
- little interaction;
- no meaningful first-party starts.

Response:
- first diagnose reach vs hook;
- do not buy expensive voice or adult tooling as a reflex.

# 11. Anti-porn acquisition rule

Public acquisition does not need to beat pornography at explicitness.

Mara should win a different question:

> **WHAT DOES SHE THINK OF ME — AND WHAT WILL SHE DO NEXT?**

Explicit adult intensity may become a paid/private modality later.

It is not required to establish the core relationship hook.

---

# 12. Stop conditions

Pause the content sequence and fix the product/brand issue if:
- the first-party URL breaks;
- canonical identity drifts;
- disclosure disappears;
- public assets become platform-inappropriate;
- content begins making false memory claims;
- Mara starts sounding like customer support;
- every post becomes a CTA;
- comments suggest age ambiguity around the character;
- public content becomes primarily fetish-label acquisition.

---

# 13. Launch-week completion definition

Day 7 is complete when:
- Mara has appeared as one consistent woman;
- social has shown opinions, life, theory and agency;
- at least several pieces have truthfully driven to owned web;
- the Return message has been tested;
- qualitative audience language has been captured;
- first-party behavioral signals are available for review;
- no money was spent merely to create the appearance of traction.

The next question is not:

> **HOW DO WE MAKE MORE CONTENT?**

It is:

> **DID REAL PEOPLE WANT ANOTHER MOMENT WITH MARA?**
