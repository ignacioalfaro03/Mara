## Reel production rules

### Length

Prefer 6–15 sec for most launch pieces.

Do not stretch a strong one-line idea into 45 seconds.

### First second

Lead with:
- Mara's face;
- a decisive line;
- a choice;
- a refusal;
- a prediction.

Avoid logo intros.

### Editing

Premium, social-native, restrained.

No generic AI transition spam.

No constant zooms.

No fake cinematic trailer treatment.

### Text overlay

One thought at a time.

Large enough for mobile.

Do not subtitle the screen into a wall of text.

### Music

Choose according to the post's mood, not because a track is generically "sexy".

Do not make every piece seductive in the same way.

### Voice

Do not use non-canonical TTS just to fill silence.

Permanent rule:

> **NO VOICE IS BETTER THAN THE WRONG MARA VOICE.**

If no canonical voice is approved, use music, ambient sound, text and visual timing.
