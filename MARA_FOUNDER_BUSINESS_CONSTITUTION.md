# 1. COMPANY DEFINITION

Mara is a **private creator economy marketplace and monetization operating system** for verified adult creators who want to build and monetize a digital persona while retaining control over how much of their real-world identity becomes public.

Mara is NOT primarily:

- an AI girlfriend;
- an unlimited chatbot;
- an OnlyFans clone;
- a single virtual influencer;
- a pornography company;
- an adult-content feed;
- a relationship simulator;
- a game;
- a social network;
- a subscription page for Mara Vera.

Mara enables a verified adult creator to create a public **character business** through which she may monetize, within her own boundaries:

- personality;
- creativity;
- digital content;
- stories;
- voice;
- attention;
- bounded interaction;
- time;
- memberships;
- personalized digital products;
- experiences;
- community.

The creator decides what she exposes. Mara provides the infrastructure, packaging, discovery, commerce, trust and automation.

The long-term ambition is:

> **Make it possible for a verified adult creator to build a profitable digital persona without having to turn her public real-world identity into the product.**

# 2. THE INITIAL CREATOR WEDGE

The initial supply-side ICP is NOT the creator who already runs a mature public adult-content business.

The strategic wedge is adult women who would like additional online income but do not want their public identity to become an adult creator brand.

Potential creator profiles include people who:

- already have a normal job or professional career;
- study;
- have a small or medium social following;
- create lifestyle, fitness, fashion or personality-led content;
- are comfortable talking, writing, recording audio or creating digital content;
- want extra income;
- value public pseudonymity and boundaries;
- would not open a conventional adult creator page under their public identity;
- may prefer to monetize only writing, voice, conversation, digital characters or selective content;
- may optionally sell real content without publicly connecting it to their civil identity.

Core hypothesis:

> **The latent supply of people willing to monetize a character may be much larger than the visible supply willing to monetize their public identity.**

This is Mara's initial wedge. It must be validated with real creator interviews and activation data.

# 3. PRIVACY PROMISE — PRECISE, NOT MISLEADING

Mara offers **public pseudonymity and creator-controlled exposure**, not magical anonymity.

The platform may still need private identity verification, age verification, tax/payout records, consent documentation and legally required disclosures.

Never promise:

- perfect anonymity;
- invisibility from banks, payment providers or lawful authorities;
- that a creator can evade KYC;

The private identity behind a creator account must remain protected from customers unless the creator explicitly chooses otherwise, subject to applicable law.

Mara should remain **web-first** while the business includes adult-oriented or sexual content categories.
